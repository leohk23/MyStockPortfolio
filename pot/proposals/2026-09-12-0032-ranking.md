## What the run decided, and why

### Tradeweb is not cheap, and has not been since February

This is the finding. `signals.json` fires TW at **24.21×, the 1.1st percentile of 261 weeks**, 4.8% above a
23.09× floor. That multiple is computed on trailing EPS of $4.19, and the four quarters behind it include
Q4 2025.

In Q4 2025 Tradeweb booked **$205.4m of realised and unrealised gains on Canton Coin holdings** in
*Other income (loss), net* — a non-operating line — inside total non-operating other income of $207.1m.
For the full year the figure was **$270.9m**, against operating income of $835.3m. Its own release states
income before taxes of $1,174.974m, a 21.6% effective rate, consolidated net income of $921.500m and
$812.792m attributable to Tradeweb Markets Inc.
([Q4/FY2025 release, 8-K exhibit 99.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000014/tw-2025xq4xex991.htm)).
At 31 December 2025 it held 1.6 billion Canton Coins worth $242.7m.

Tax-effecting the Q4 gain at the quarter's own 19.6% rate and allocating it in the same 88.5% proportion the
filing uses for that quarter leaves roughly **$146m** of the $897.2m trailing attributable net income that is
a token mark. Trailing EPS becomes about **$3.51** rather than $4.19, and the multiple **~28.9× against a
23.09× floor — 25% above it**, out of the cheapest decile and nowhere near a signal. Yahoo's `norm` for
FY2025 equals filed attributable net income to the dollar, so the vendor removed nothing and the `oneOff`
flag never fired.

**Twelve TW proposals have been written on that number**, each one refining thresholds on fees per million
and TRACE share while the denominator underneath was wrong. The entry is now in
[`pot/adjustments.json`](../adjustments.json) with the company's own release as its source, so the next
`fetch-prices.js` run will build a `peLowOwn`/`pePctileOwn` band for TW — fy `2025-12-31` exists in
`earnings.json`, so unlike the other two entries written today this one will actually bite.

Nothing here is a sell instruction: no TW proposal was ever accepted and the pot holds none.

### §5 — a data-quality finding worth more than one proposal

**`pot/adjustments.json` keys on filed fiscal-year rows; the multiple that fires `6.1` does not.**
`adjustedYears()` in `fetch-prices.js` matches an entry when `a.fy === y.date` for a **year** row in
`earnings.json`. Two of the three entries written today — MWA's fiscal 2026, which ends 30 September 2026,
and Novo's 2026 — fall in fiscal years that **are not filed yet**, so they match no row and do nothing until
those accounts land. Both one-offs are nevertheless sitting inside the trailing four quarters that produce
the EPS the signal fires on, today.

So the store cannot correct the screen in precisely the window where a one-off is freshest and the agent is
most likely to be fooled by it. The entries are still worth writing — they stop the work being thrown away a
fifth time, and they will apply on schedule — but the gap is real and it is logged to
[`pot/data-wishlist.md`](../data-wishlist.md) rather than asserted as a bug, because it may be a deliberate
choice about only adjusting audited years.

Second finding, smaller: **`peWeeks` is under three years for five ranked or near-ranked names** —
RSGN.SW 144, GME 129, AMSC 84, GWRE 46, MRVL 41 — and `peWindow` says "5y" for all of them. RSGN.SW is the
one already handled through `HISTORY_FROM`; the others are simply short vendor histories rather than
business changes, so nothing needs adding to `fetch-prices.js`. Stated here so the label is never read as a
measurement.
