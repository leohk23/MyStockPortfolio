## 2. Thesis

Intuit owns tax, accounting, payroll, payments and credit workflows used by roughly 100 million
customers, and the embedded records, integrations and expert network give its high margin a better
chance of persisting than an AI interface without the underlying financial context
([company profile](https://investors.intuit.com/)). Why now: FY2026 revenue grew 14%, GAAP
operating income grew 20%, QuickBooks Online Accounting grew 23% and TurboTax Live grew 37%, while
the share still clears Leo's valuation filter on current GAAP and recurring earnings
([FY2026 results](https://investors.intuit.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000896878-26-000029/fy26q4earningspressrelease.htm),
[prices.json](../../prices.json)). The market appears to read slower FY2027 guidance as maturity;
what it may be missing is that automating more of a customer's financial work can increase paid
attach and operating leverage even when the underlying tax-software unit count is flat or down.

## 6. The case against

Intuit may be harvesting its installed base rather than widening its moat. Total U.S. TurboTax
units fell 2%, Q4 QuickBooks growth came from higher prices and mix as well as customer growth,
FY2027 guidance slows total revenue to 9%–10%, and Mailchimp is guided down 1% to flat; a general
AI agent or government tax-filing option could weaken the front-end relationship before revenue
shows it because price and expert attach can temporarily offset lost users. At a 27.4% GAAP
operating margin, customer acquisition, AI investment or price resistance has room to make both
earnings quality and the multiple contract
([FY2026 results and FY2027 guidance](https://investors.intuit.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000896878-26-000029/fy26q4earningspressrelease.htm)).

Why this is not the 7532.T mistake again: this is not a generic AI rebound thesis. The case starts
with five years of rising GAAP margins, current revenue and EPS, explicit return-on-capital work and
the actual product growth mix; valuation is only the gate, and both falsifier tiers test whether
the underlying customer franchises weaken while price and attach make headline revenue look
healthier.
