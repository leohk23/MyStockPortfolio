## 2. Thesis

Mueller supplies equipment that existing water systems must maintain and replace, providing a recurring use for its manufacturing capacity. Its filed operating margin rose from 9.99% in FY2023 to 13.82% in FY2024 and 18.23% in FY2025, while annual sales rose from $1.276bn to $1.430bn. My inference is that weak construction expectations may be obscuring a business whose operations have already improved over several years, but the proposed return depends on those assets producing cash rather than on another round of price rises or tax benefits. [Filed annual statements](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059325000066/R5.htm), [local annual revenue](../../earnings.json).
