## 2. Thesis

water networks operating, a replacement and upgrade market in which failure costs and product
qualification matter. Four complete local years show revenue rising each year, GAAP operating
margin widening from 10.1% to 18.8%, and FY2025 ROIC reaching 13.1%, while Q3 FY2026 still
delivered 4.1% sales growth and a 20.4% GAAP operating margin
([earnings.json](../../earnings.json), [prices.json](../../prices.json), [Q3 release](https://ir.muellerwaterproducts.com/press-releases/2026/08-05-2026-211118748)). The market prices the improvement as a temporary cycle; this order is a monitored test of whether the foundry programme, portfolio work and faster-growing Water Management business can lift the earnings base while the EPA still estimates $422.9bn of US distribution and transmission investment need over 20 years ([EPA needs survey](https://www.epa.gov/dwsrf/epas-7th-drinking-water-infrastructure-needs-survey-and-assessment)).

## 6. The case against

**Dominant risk (`financial`):** The margin expansion may be a temporary peak built on pricing,
non-recurring tariff refunds and lower incentive compensation while physical volumes remain weak.

**Earliest observable:** The FY2026 result, locally estimated for **5 November 2026**, will print
sales, adjusted EBITDA and free-cash-flow conversion against the $1.470bn, $367m and 70% thresholds
in P3 ([company outlook](https://ir.muellerwaterproducts.com/press-releases/2026/08-05-2026-211118748), [prices.json](../../prices.json)).

Water Flow Solutions sales fell 0.6% in Q3, inventory rose from $328.7m to $379.8m over nine months
and absorbed $59.9m of cash, and foundry capital spending is increasing. If pricing support
reverses while inflation, residential construction or municipal budgets soften, a low P/E
capitalizes peak earnings and both earnings and the multiple can contract
([Q3 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000036/mwa-20260630.htm)).

**Combined exposure:** MWA is not in the human book, so the existing name weight is 0%; the order
adds another US industrial exposure to a book already **66.16% US** by market value
([signals.json](../../signals.json)).

starts with four filed years of revenue and GAAP margin, explicit capital-return and cash evidence,
