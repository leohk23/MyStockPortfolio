## Research that decided the fresh allocation

### Stevanato: a cash inflection is not yet a capital-return case

The local Q3 revenue €326.063m, profit €41.843m and EPS €0.15 reconcile to the issuer's rounded €326.1m, €41.8m and €0.15. Conversely, local Q2 operating income €51.282m excludes the roughly €12.2m disposal charge: the issuer's reported €39.1m controls, as the existing adjustment record explains.

Q3 free cash flow turned positive at €21.7m, but nine-month free cash flow was **−€4.8m** versus +€16.9m. High-value sales grew only 1%, and management expects low-single-digit growth in 2027 as GLP-1 volumes disappoint. These are business reasons to decline even half-size, alongside local FY2025 ROIC of 8.1% and turnover 0.47. [Issuer Q3 release](https://ir.stevanatogroup.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/187/stevanato-group-delivers-8-revenue-growth-for-the-third).

The presentation also says Q2/Q3 revenue included variable compensation for lower GLP-1 volumes; its amount is not separately quantified. Compensation for unused volume is not evidence that the factories are filling. Fishers reached gross-profit breakeven, but gross-profit breakeven is not a return on all invested capital. [Issuer presentation, slides 5, 7 and 11](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849853/000119312526445354/stvn-ex99_1.htm).

Local reported EPS $0.59 gives 26.78x; company-adjusted `researchEps` $0.650003 gives 24.31x. The latter is directly sourced but has no certified rolling historical percentile. The store already contains FY2025 and the interim adjusted periods, so no duplicate entries were appended. T1–T3 do not bear directly on this drug-container investment case. The future checkpoint is FY2026 cash and the formal FY2027 outlook, review by 31 March 2027; [the calendar](https://ir.stevanatogroup.com/news-events/ir-calendar) has not announced that results date.

### Addus: the cash mismatch is substantially better than the annual screen suggests

Q3 revenue $387.733m, operating profit $39.818m and net income $28.594m reconcile locally. Filed quarter EPS totals about $5.998 against the quote's $6.01; this rounding/basis difference is disclosed, not a new earnings estimate. The reported and vendor multiples are nearly identical, so the `oneOff` flag is not proof of a huge hidden gain.

Nine-month OCF is $133.290m against net income $81.270m, whereas the local cash multiple uses the older annual cash basis. That improves the cash reading. Personal-care organic revenue grew 6.9%; total billable hours grew about 4.0% and revenue/hour about 5.0%, but those last two cover total operations and are **not** a decomposition of same-store organic growth. Comparable patient census fell from 50,037 to 49,848. [Q3 release](https://addus.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/addus-homecare-announces-third-quarter-2026-financial-results).

The $275m AccentCare acquisition would add about $280m annual revenue and use cash/revolver funding. The risk is paying for more revenue without improving returns, not the disappearance of home-care demand. [Acquisition announcement](https://addus.gcs-web.com/node/14701). The 10-Q identifies $6.4m of nine-month ARPA support reducing costs, and timing effects in cash collection; both qualify apparent improvement. [Q3 10-Q](https://addus.gcs-web.com/static-files/50bc62cc-34a9-48b4-b65b-688d38b90fe1).

I retain WAIT for the annual cash/ROIC checkpoint in calls.md, and would also need patient growth to resume rather than relying solely on price and care intensity. This is stricter judgement than accepting today's improved collections as enough; it is not a claim that incomplete earnings history prohibits buying. T1–T3 do not directly determine this care-delivery case. [Trading 212 Invest lists ADUS](https://www.trading212.com/trading-instruments/invest/ADUS.US), so fees are not the reason to wait.

### Guidewire: reconcile the cash before calling it cheap

FY2026 revenue $1,475.363m, operating income $149.874m, net income $139.283m and EPS $1.63 agree with the issuer. Local OCF is $389.716m and free cash flow $377.660m. The release shows **$181.799m share compensation** in OCF's reconciliation. FCF less that economic cost is approximately $195.861m, an analytical sensitivity, not a company cash-flow metric. Reported P/E is 97.07x; company-adjusted annual EPS $3.43 implies 46.13x, but excludes compensation and is not owner earnings. [FY2026 release](https://ir.guidewire.com/news-releases/news-release-details/guidewire-announces-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2026).

The business is growing and moving into profit; those facts survive the check. What does not follow is that the low cash percentile proves a bargain. ROIC of 8.4% and the economic cost of compensation leave too much of the proposed return dependent on future operating leverage. T1 could lower model costs, T2 could reduce the value of workflow interfaces, and T3 could support governance. No external adoption evidence was established to resolve their net effect, so I do not assert the moat survives them. This is a substantive rejection of the current investment case, not a WAIT caused only by an unreconciled accounting field.
