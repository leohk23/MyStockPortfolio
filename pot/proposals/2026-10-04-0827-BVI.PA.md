## 6. The case against

**Theses:** T3 bears through assurance demand, but the company's AI-driven category also contains physical infrastructure services; it is not evidence that regulation mandates buying an AI audit. T2 does not directly describe this per-service inspection business; no seat-retention premise is used.

**Dominant risk (financial):** Accelerating acquisitions and portfolio exits consume the cash produced by inspections, leaving higher leverage without adequate shareholder returns.

**Earliest observable:** Acquisition-financing announcements as made, with the complete cash and leverage bridge on 25 February 2027; P3 tests both.

The [September strategy update](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3365950/0/en/bureau-veritas-confirms-leap-28-ambition-upgrades-total-revenue-growth-to-double-digit-in-2027-2028-and-targets-eur-1-billion-revenue-in-ai-driven-markets-and-services-by-2030.html) explicitly accelerates M&A and narrows the leverage ambition to 1.5–2.0x. Its EUR1bn AI-driven revenue target for 2030 includes an estimated EUR370m end-2026 baseline and acquisition effects; neither is a filed AI-assurance revenue stream. The independent [European Commission Article 43 reference](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/article-43) says Annex III categories 2–8 ordinarily use internal conformity assessment without a notified body: compulsory outside assurance is a narrower market than a broad T3 story implies. Check legal changes when published and separately disclosed assurance revenue at each result, next 25 February; an undisclosed amount stays unknown. No T3 premium is included. The 15.4% historical discount cannot be attached to the current quote multiple: the latter is only 3.4% below that historical median on a different earnings base.

**Combined exposure:** No BVI listing is in the human holdings, so direct company weight is 0%. The purchase adds to Europe, 5.84% of the human book, and Industrials, 7.07% ([signals.json](../../signals.json)). Its activities are global, not solely European.

**Why this is not the 7532.T mistake:** Sector demand is checked against multi-year margins and capital returns, the position is halved for earnings uncertainty, and acquisition cash is tested directly. The comparison is in EUR with a separate GBP conversion; no favourable FX outcome is assumed.
