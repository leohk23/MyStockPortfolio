## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none.

**Dominant risk (financial):** Acquisitions and reinvestment absorb the operating recovery, leaving shareholders with more debt rather than more distributable cash.

**Earliest observable:** Q3 guidance on 28 October is an interim warning only; the direct cash/leverage test is the FY2026 statement, reviewed by 31 March 2027. H1 company FCF already fell, and its definition excludes acquisition consideration. P3 therefore tests the full investment cash bridge, not sales as a substitute.

Brand distribution and specialised formulations are reasons margins may persist, not guarantees: food-safety problems, retailer bargaining and competition can erode both demand and economics. The reported annual decline makes a permanently higher restructuring burden a real possibility. The issuer's recurring ROIC must not be confused with the local calculation.

**Combined exposure:** Danone is absent from the public human-book holdings, so this introduces a name rather than adding to an existing company position. It adds European consumer exposure; that is disclosure, not the reason it ranked first.
