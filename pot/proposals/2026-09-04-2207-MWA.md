## 2. Thesis

Mueller sells valves, hydrants, metering and leak-detection equipment used to keep North American
water networks operating, a replacement and upgrade market in which failure costs and product
qualification matter. Four complete local years show revenue rising each year, GAAP operating
margin widening from 10.1% to 18.8%, and FY2025 ROIC reaching 13.1%, while Q3 FY2026 still delivered
4.1% sales growth and a 20.4% GAAP operating margin ([earnings.json](../../earnings.json),
[prices.json](../../prices.json), [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000034/exhibit991pressreleasefy26.htm)).
The market appears to treat the improvement as a temporary pricing cycle; what it may be missing is
that foundry investment, portfolio work and the faster-growing Water Management business have
changed the earnings base as utilities continue replacing ageing infrastructure.

non-recurring tariff refunds and lower incentive compensation while physical volumes remain weak.

**Earliest observable:** The FY2026 result, locally estimated for **5 November 2026**, will print
sales, adjusted EBITDA and free-cash-flow conversion against the $1.470bn, $367m and 70% thresholds
in P3 ([company outlook](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000034/exhibit991pressreleasefy26.htm),
[prices.json](../../prices.json)).

Water Flow Solutions sales fell 0.6% in Q3, inventory rose from $328.7m to $379.8m over nine months
and absorbed $59.9m of cash, and foundry capital spending is increasing. If pricing support reverses
while inflation, residential construction or municipal budgets soften, a low P/E capitalises peak
earnings and both earnings and the multiple can contract
([Q3 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000036/mwa-20260630.htm)).

**Combined exposure:** MWA is not in the human book, so the existing name weight is 0%; the order

([signals.json](../../signals.json)).
