## 2. Thesis

**The business:** Eckert & Ziegler supplies radioactive materials and manufacturing services to medical and industrial customers. Revenue comes from isotope products, services and occasional licences; it is not a bet on one drug reaching approval.

**The engine:** The issuer's FY2022–25 EBIT margins were **21%, 18%, 20% and 24%**, rather than an uninterrupted climb. Revenue grew **11.97% annually** across those years. Qualified manufacturing, handling infrastructure and integration into customers' production provide a reason margins might persist; the return on added capacity remains to be earned. [Annual report](https://www.ezag.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/euz25e.pdf).

**Why now:** The [12 November release](https://www.ezag.com/press/detail/?newsid=9eed2a67-11a7-4433-b1a4-a8d3576b3b73) reports Q3 sales growth of 6% and adjusted EBIT growth of 25%. It retains approximately EUR320m revenue and EUR80m adjusted EBIT guidance.

**What the market is missing:** My inference is that episodic licensing obscures progress in the supply business: nine-month revenue grew 2%, or 4% excluding the previous licence contribution. That is evidence of underlying activity, not proof the apparent valuation discount is entirely mistaken.

**What has to happen:** Operating growth must leave cash after investment, leases and financing extended to related parties. If that remainder disappears, a growing medical end market is insufficient reason to own the shares.
