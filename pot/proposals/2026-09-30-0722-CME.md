## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none. Per-contract exchange fees are not seat-priced workflow subscriptions.

**Dominant risk (financial):** unusually active markets normalize while technology and licensing costs remain, so high operating margins prove cyclical.

**Earliest observable:** the monthly ADV release, checked by 8 October, followed by fee revenue and GAAP margin on October 21. This is within one quarter and directly tests the risk; the 64% warning can fire well before the two-quarter break.

Q2 already showed the tension: GAAP operating income was lower despite slightly higher revenue. A 22x multiple is not a distressed price, and the 8.47% local ROIC is much less impressive than the sales margin. Clearing balances make total-asset turnover hard to compare with an industrial company; no invented adjusted asset base is used. Rival venues and data-pricing pressure are additional risks.

**Combined exposure:** no direct CME holding appears in the human-book holdings list, so this opens a new company exposure. It adds US exposure to an already US-heavy book and overlaps the pot's TW exposure to trading infrastructure; owning both does not make that economic exposure independent.
