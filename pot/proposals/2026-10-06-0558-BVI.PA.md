**Earliest observable:** the H1 cash warning already exists; the 21 October sales release can disclose new acquisition commitments, and the 25 February 2027 FY cash statement provides the next complete test. This is within two quarters.

The [22 September issuer capital-markets update](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/22/3365950/0/en/bureau-veritas-confirms-leap-28-ambition-upgrades-total-revenue-growth-to-double-digit-in-2027-2028-and-targets-eur-1-billion-revenue-in-ai-driven-markets-and-services-by-2030.html) relies on stronger M&A for double-digit total growth in 2027–28 and moves target leverage to 1.5–2x. Track the Commission's notified-body/designation framework and actual issuer AI-assurance revenue separately at each quarterly review, next 21 October, then FY February. No such separately verified revenue series is valued here. T3 is a possible positive option, not an established explanation for the present cheap screen; the proposed price must work on existing operations.
