## 2. Thesis

## 6. The case against

NVIDIA is increasingly helping finance and underwrite the ecosystem that buys its products. The
Q2 filing shows $279bn of supply and capacity commitments, $25bn of future equity commitments,
$36bn of AI-cloud agreements and large guarantees; customers may delay deployments or fail to
turn infrastructure into profitable demand, leaving NVIDIA with inventory, credit and capacity
risk ([Q2 filing](https://investor.nvidia.com/files/doc_financials/2027/NVDA-2027-Q2-10Q-Final-including-exhibits.pdf)).
The main book already has 7.17% in NVIDIA, China remains constrained, and a 31× recurring multiple
is cheap only against NVIDIA's extraordinary recent history, not in absolute terms. If hyperscaler
returns disappoint, custom accelerators improve, or NVIDIA-supported financing is masking end
demand, earnings and the multiple can contract together.
