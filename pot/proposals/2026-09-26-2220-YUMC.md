## 2. Thesis

The business case is a restaurant network converting repeat transactions and incremental stores into rising operating profit, with annual asset turnover improving in the local accounts rather than merely relying on a Chinese consumption rebound. The completed Pizza Hut rights purchase supplies a specific operating change before the next result: future China Pizza Hut royalties to Yum Brands have been removed, for a cash purchase whose return must now be measured ([seller's completed-transaction filing](https://d18rn0p25nwr6d.cloudfront.net/CIK-0001041061/b36a1203-9aff-4577-b56b-a4c5b1ba207b.html)). My inference is that the market gives too little weight to that operating progress relative to weak ticket spending; the case does not assume that repurchases fund themselves or that the acquisition earns an adequate return.

## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none.

**Dominant risk (financial):** The rights purchase and shareholder payouts consume liquid capital faster than the restaurants replenish it, allowing per-share earnings growth to disguise deteriorating owner cash generation.

**Earliest observable:** The Q3 cash and balance-sheet statements, locally estimated for **4 November 2026**, reviewed by **6 November**. C, R and net liquidity are the dated measures; the H1 payout-coverage warning is already active. No half-size exemption based on an unobservable distant risk is being claimed.

The acquisition creates a return hurdle, not free earnings. A low multiple can fairly price weak household demand, discounting and delivery costs; traffic alone does not prove that each extra meal earns enough. Repurchases can lift EPS while aggregate attributable profit grows much more slowly. Yuan operating economics also expose a GBP investor to currencies that the USD listing does not eliminate.

**Combined exposure:** YUMC is absent from the human book, so its existing direct weight is **0%**. The purchase adds China consumer exposure alongside an existing **6.69% China** geographic weight; it is a new business but not a new country exposure. Main-book US weight is **67.65%**.
