## 2. Thesis

Mueller sells equipment whose repair and replacement demand extends beyond a single construction cycle, while its local FY2023–25 accounts show sales rising from $1.276bn to $1.430bn and improving profitability ([earnings.json](../../earnings.json)). The investable opportunity is for the enlarged manufacturing base to produce more cash from that installed demand, with local FY2025 ROIC of 13.12% versus 8.39% in FY2023 providing a starting question about capital productivity rather than proof it will persist ([prices.json](../../prices.json)). My inference is that modest exposure is warranted while the cash conversion commitment is tested, but lower physical volumes and a charge-sensitive margin mean a further top-up needs separate volume, margin and asset-return exit routes.
