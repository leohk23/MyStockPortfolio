## 2. Thesis

R&S Group supplies the transformers required by grids, renewables, storage, industrial infrastructure and data centres, and FY2025 earnings quality is visible in 8.6% organic sales growth, a 20.9% EBITDA margin, CHF48.1m free cash flow and leverage reduced to 0.7× ([2025 annual report](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/media/document/0fec9fb0-b91a-4deb-aef1-5cb2afb265d8/assets/RSG_Annual_report_2025_def.pdf?disposition=inline)).

The market may be treating the delivery delay as a transformer down-cycle when the backlog mix and new Łódź capacity suggest part is timing and capacity conversion; converting signed orders while holding the 19%–21% margin range would restore growth without requiring another commodity-price surge.

This is not the 7532.T mistake again: the order does not rely on a rebound story, headline growth
inflated by an acquisition, or favourable currency. It separates 47% reported growth from 8.6%
organic growth, confronts the current sales and order-intake declines, checks cash conversion and
leverage, and makes backlog conversion—not the share price—the falsifiable claim.

## 6. The case against

The share may be cheap because earnings are already rolling over. H1 adjusted order intake fell
10%, sales fell 13%, EBITDA fell 23%, and FY2026 revenue guidance of CHF410m–CHF420m is roughly flat
with FY2025 and below the mid-term growth range. Utility customers have excess inventory and
installation bottlenecks, while the higher power-transformer mix takes longer to produce; backlog
can look reassuring for years before becoming cash, and factory delays would hurt revenue and
margins ([H1 update](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/news/rands-group-provides-trading-update-for-h1-2026/bb41967c-ac88-4041-be50-200b3f26951b)).

The accounting deserves equal scepticism. Much of FY2025's reported growth came from the first full
year of Kyte Powertech; CHF180.7m of acquisition goodwill was written directly against equity under
Swiss GAAP FER, and the report's theoretical capitalization view shows CHF131.4m residual Kyte
goodwill and CHF36.1m of 2025 Kyte goodwill amortisation that did not pass through reported profit
([2025 annual report](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/media/document/0fec9fb0-b91a-4deb-aef1-5cb2afb265d8/assets/RSG_Annual_report_2025_def.pdf?disposition=inline)).
This is also a short listed history—179 weeks—and a small SIX line where the bid/ask spread and a
second commission can matter. The strongest bear case is that the market correctly sees peak
transformer margins, acquisition-flattered growth and an expensive capacity build meeting
normalized utility demand; the 12.70× multiple is only a gate, not an answer.
