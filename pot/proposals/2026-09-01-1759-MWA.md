## 2. Thesis

American water networks, where a large installed base and failure costs support replacement demand
and pricing. Why now: four locally filed years show revenue rising every year, GAAP operating
margin widening from 10.1% to 18.8%, and FY2025 ROIC of 13.1%, while Q3 FY2026 still delivered
4.1% sales growth and a 20.4% GAAP operating margin ([earnings.json](../../earnings.json),
[prices.json](../../prices.json), [Q3 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000034/exhibit991pressreleasefy26.htm)).

missing is that portfolio work, foundry investment and the faster-growing Water Management
business have reset normalized economics upward, while the installed base and qualification burden
help those margins persist ([Q3 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000036/mwa-20260630.htm)).
