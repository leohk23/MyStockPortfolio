## 2. Thesis

room to earn more from the existing product and distribution base. Filed FY2023–25 operating margin
improved **9.99% → 13.82% → 18.23%** as sales rose $1,275.7m → $1,429.7m — evidence across several
years, not one strong quarter. The proposed return comes from converting that operating improvement
into cash on the factory investment; the market may under-credit that conversion, but the newest
quarter's price-led sales and exceptional benefits make it a **testable opportunity at reduced size**,
not a finished success.

**Why this is not 7532.T:** essential infrastructure is the opening observation, not the buy argument.

cash break stop the "water spending" story standing in for earnings quality. Equipment compatibility
and utility qualification could support persistence; they do not make the margin unassailable.
