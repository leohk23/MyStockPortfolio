movement from OCF. The statement also records substantial liquidity, so negative FCF is a return-on-
investment and funding concern, not proof of imminent insolvency.
[HKEX announcement, balance sheets, cash statement and tax note, pp. 33–35, 40, 43](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1119/2026111900873.pdf).
