## 2. Thesis

**A recovery case, with the sign stated.** Danone's IFRS operating profit fell in FY2025 (−13%,
€3.379bn to €2.940bn, as read from the issuer's FY release by the 14 October run; not re-read
this run), while its recurring operating margin rose 44bps to 13.4% on +4.5% like-for-like sales.
It guides 2026 like-for-like sales to *grow* +3% to +5% with recurring operating income growing
faster. So the recurring business is growing and the reported figure has yet to follow. The case
rests on repeat-purchase nutrition: yoghurt, water, infant formula and medical nutrition are bought
weekly or on prescription, not once. Margin has edged up for four years (local operating margin
11.8%, 12.6%, 12.7%, 13.6% for FY2022–25). The local return on capital rose from 5.6% to 8.6% over
the same years. **What the market is discounting:** the shares are down 17% in three months and 20%
in a year after a first half at the low end of guidance (+3.5%), free cash flow of €0.9bn, a
cash-and-debt acquisition (Huel) and a €1.5bn bond. **What I think it is missing:** the recurring
margin is still rising under that strain. Growth resumes *in the reported figure* when FY2026 IFRS
operating income exceeds FY2025's, in the FY2026 release (February 2027). Whether volume holds is the
open question, and P3 tests it rather than assuming it.

## 3. Falsifier

These are analyst thresholds, not company guidance, except where the guidance is quoted.

**Warning** (trips a re-read, not a sale), any of:

- At the **28 October 2026 Q3 sales release**: Q3 volume/mix is **negative**, *or* like-for-like
  growth is **below +3%**, *or* the FY2026 outlook ("LFL sales growth expected between +3% and +5%,
  with recurring operating income growing faster than sales") is cut below that range or loses the
  "faster than sales" clause. H1's comparison point is volume/mix +1.7% and price +1.8%.
- **Outside the filings (Rule 3), monthly:** Kantar's GB grocery market-share release reports
  own-label growth year on year. Warn if own-label growth beats branded growth by **5 points or
  more in two consecutive monthly releases**. That is the shopper moving down to private label,
  which shows in volume before it shows in Danone's margin.

**Break** (propose a sale under §5.1), any of:

- **Volume/mix negative in both Q3 and Q4 2026** (Q4 in the FY2026 release, February 2027) while
  full-year like-for-like growth is below +3%. That means growth was bought with price and the
  shopper has left.
- **FY2026 recurring operating margin below FY2025's 13.4%.** That would mean recurring operating
  income did not grow faster than sales: the guidance missed on the measure the thesis rests on.
- **FY2026 IFRS operating income not above FY2025's** as stated in the FY2025 release. This is the
  §2.4b recovery test; read both figures from the issuer's own tables, not from `earnings.json`.

## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none. Danone sells food, water and nutrition. T1, T2 and T3 concern AI models, seat-priced
software and AI governance, and none of them touches what Danone sells or how it is priced.

**Dominant risk (`competitive`):** private label and price-led rivals take volume, so the +3% to +5%
like-for-like growth arrives only through price while the shopper trades down.

**Earliest observable:** Q3 volume/mix in Danone's sales release on **28 October 2026**. Outside the
filings, Kantar's monthly GB own-label versus branded growth. Both are within two quarters, so Rule 4
does not halve the order (§7.2a already does).

**Secondary arguments, put to persuade.**

- **The cheapness may be correct.** A 2.15th percentile is measured against 2023–24, when Danone
  traded at 35–48× reported earnings that IFRS charges had depressed (FY2023 EPS €1.36). On recurring
  earnings it is about 16×. That is a fair price for a 13% margin food company, not a gift.
- **Cash is the weak line.** H1 free cash flow was €0.9bn. Huel was paid for in the same months the
  bond was raised. A recovery in recurring operating income that does not reach cash is the
  2.4b-shaped trap: the recurring measure rises while the reported one does not.
- **Safety and recall risk** is intrinsic to infant formula and medical nutrition, and it can hit
  volume and reputation in one quarter.
- **Currency translation.** Reported sales were −0.3% in FY2025 against +4.5% like-for-like. Emerging-market
  currencies have been taking most of the organic growth before it reaches euros.

**Why this is not 7532.T again.** That mistake was buying a rebound story without the accounts. Here
the recurring margin has risen four years running in the filed accounts, and guidance commits to
recurring profit outgrowing sales. The falsifier fires on the first quarter the volume behind that
weakens. Where it *could* still be 7532.T: the reported profit has not yet recovered. P3's third
break line is there for exactly that.

(`signals.json` `book.byCompany`), another French consumer-staples name, which this does not add to.
