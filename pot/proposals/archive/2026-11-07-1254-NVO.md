## 2. Thesis

**The business:** Novo sells diabetes and obesity medicines, including injectable and oral GLP-1
treatments, through healthcare channels. **This is a recovery:** FY2025 reported operating profit
fell 0.5%, and 2026 adjusted sales and operating-profit guidance is **−2% to +2% at CER**, a flat
midpoint.

**The engine:** Filed operating margins were 42.3%, 44.2%, 44.2% and 41.3% in 2022–25.
Revenue compounded 20.43% annually over those years, but declining capital productivity makes the
old growth rate unsuitable as a forecast. [Local accounts](../../../earnings.json).

**Why now:** The [4 November result](https://www.novonordisk.com/content/nncorp/global/en/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=919765)
moves the recovery from a promised launch to actual quarterly group growth. It improves the
outlook from a **6% decline to flat** to a **2% decline to 2% growth**, not to assured expansion.

**What the market is missing:** My different view is that a modest, cash-generating recovery can
be sufficient; the purchase need not assume a return to the former growth rate or historical
multiple. The evidence is a growing obesity business alongside positive international sales.
Whether that survives competitive pricing remains the question, not a premise.

**What has to happen:** Group adjusted sales must keep growing into 2027, with US obesity growth
outweighing diabetes erosion. P3 tests that directly. This addresses the 7532.T mistake through
filed sales and a dated recovery test, rather than a familiar product or a falling price.

   draft's P6 names "generic entry" and "lower US prices" in one clause, with no figure and no date.
