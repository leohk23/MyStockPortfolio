## 2. Thesis

**This is a recovery investment: FY2025 reported operating profit fell 13%, to €2.940bn**, while repeat-purchase nutrition demand continued to grow ([FY2025 release](https://www.danone.com/content/dam/corp/global/danonecom/investors/en-all-publications/2026/pressreleases/danonepr200226.pdf)). H1 2026 reported operating profit resumed growth, up 7.6%, with positive volume/mix rather than price alone supporting sales ([H1 release](https://www.danone.com/content/dam/corp/global/danonecom/investors/fr-all-publications/2026/pressreleases/cpdanone290726.pdf.coredownload.pdf)). My inference is that the market gives too little credit to this existing demand and profit recovery; the business case requires it to persist through FY2026, and does not assume acquisition spending will pay back.

**Why this is not 7532.T:** the recovery is already observable in reported interim operating profit, rather than inferred from a demographic or tourism story. Full-year reported growth must follow by the analyst deadline below. Brands, distribution and medical-nutrition relationships may sustain margins, but are not proof of durable returns; cash after purchased growth is the harder test.
