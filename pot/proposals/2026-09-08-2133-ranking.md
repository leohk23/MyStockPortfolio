## Why the third ticket remains cash

It is evidence that “more electronic volume” is insufficient to establish the earnings thesis. I prefer the two small positions over paying for continued platform monetisation before seeing the next quarter's fee conversion. Revisit at the locally scheduled 29 October result if revenue growth catches up without weaker operating margin. This is a business judgment, not a ban caused by uncertified earnings, share price or prior proposals.
