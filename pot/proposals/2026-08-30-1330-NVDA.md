## 2. Thesis

NVIDIA's case is the business, not the screen: FY2023–FY2026 revenue grew eightfold while operating margin rose from 20.7% to 60.4%, and Q2 FY2027 revenue, Data Center revenue and GAAP operating income grew 106%, 117% and 124% respectively ([local earnings](../../earnings.json), [Q2 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).
Why now: Rubin has begun production shipments and management guides Q3 revenue to $108.0bn ±2% without assuming China Data Center compute revenue, while current recurring earnings still clear the local valuation gate ([Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm), [Q2 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).
The market may be over-discounting an imminent hyperscaler pause and underestimating how inference, sovereign AI and physical AI broaden demand beyond today's largest buyers, but sustained margin and independent end demand—not a cheap-looking multiple—are the tests.

This is not the 7532.T mistake again: four filed years show accelerating USD revenue and high
margins, the latest filing refreshes the forward prospect, and the valuation is corrected for
equity gains. Customer concentration, ecosystem financing, China restrictions and enormous supply
commitments are treated as risks rather than waved away as part of an industry rebound.

## 6. The case against

NVIDIA is increasingly financing and underwriting the ecosystem that buys its products. The 10-Q
shows $279bn of supply and capacity commitments, $25bn of future equity commitments and multiyear
cloud agreements; customers can delay deployments or fail to turn infrastructure into profitable
demand, leaving NVIDIA with inventory, credit and capacity risk
([Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm)).
One direct customer supplied 16% of Q2 revenue, export controls can remove markets, and the human
book already has 7.06% in NVIDIA. If hyperscaler returns disappoint, custom accelerators improve,
or financing support masks end demand, earnings and the multiple can contract together; 31×
recurring earnings is cheap only against NVIDIA's extraordinary recent history, not in absolute
terms.
