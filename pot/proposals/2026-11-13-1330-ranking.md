**The stored test fails, and it fails on the basis that makes EUZ look cheapest among the consistent ones.** The FY2025 denominator of €0.78 still carries the Q1 2025 licence, recorded as €5m of revenue and €4.8m of pre-tax profit ([adjustments.json](../adjustments.json), both `arithmetic: false`). Remove it and the annual P/E would be higher, not lower. No after-tax per-share figure is stated, so I do not compute one.

**Why the 7.4th percentile does not clear §6.1.** Each week in the stored history is divided by the latest *annual* EPS already published, at least 90 days after year-end. So its denominators lagged a rising EPS by 3 to 15 months: from 31 March 2025 to 30 March 2026, for example, every week was priced on FY2024's €0.53 while the earnings being earned were climbing towards €0.78. Today's €0.87 has almost no lag. Setting an unlagged multiple against a distribution of lagged ones makes a growing company look cheap by construction. That is the rule the brief states for adjusted EPS (*"a current adjusted P/E does not create an adjusted history"*), and a fresher reported denominator raises the same problem. A like-for-like TTM history would settle it, but `earnings.json` holds EUZ quarters from Q1 2025 only. I logged that to [data-wishlist.md](../data-wishlist.md). The 04:26 run drew the same line: it quoted 16.67x on €0.87 *"with no recomputed percentile"* and used the stored rank as the filter.

**The licence is not in the TTM.** It was booked in Q1 2025, which subtracting 9M 2025 removes, so the €0.87 is licence-free. That makes the fresh figure cleaner than the annual one. It does not make the percentile comparable.

**P/OCF does not rescue it either way.** At the 33.7th percentile the cash multiple is a long way from the decile, and nine-month operating cash covered only 0.870x of parent profit (04:26 run, issuer report). On no reading are both multiples cheap.

**How far from the filter.** The 10th-percentile order statistic of the stored history is **18.68x**. On the FY2025 denominator that is about **€14.57**, €0.07 above the limit that just ended. A proposal at that limit would be the 04:26 order again, already 5.1% below the price and so ended under D109 the day it was written. That is no proposal, so none is written. €14.57 is a screening reference from a frozen snapshot, not fair value and not a buy limit.
