## 2. Thesis

Tradeweb is a tollbooth on electronic rates, credit, equities and money-market workflows, with a network that becomes more useful as clients automate more of the trade lifecycle. From 2022 to 2025, local filed revenue rose from $1.189bn to $2.052bn while operating margin increased from 35.9% to 41.2%, and Q2 2026 still delivered 9.0% revenue growth and a 54.4% adjusted EBITDA margin while ADV rose 18.2%. The market is underweighting the durability of client, geography and protocol adoption after volatility normalises, provided that Tradeweb can keep converting rapidly growing volume into revenue despite fee and product-mix pressure.

## 6. The case against

Tradeweb may be a high-margin tollbooth whose toll is quietly falling. In Q2, ADV rose 18.2% but
revenue rose only 9.0%; total variable fees per million fell 7.2%, with rates down 9.4% and credit
down 8.3%, because the growth tilted toward lower-fee products and protocols. Competitors and
clients can force further price and mix compression, while Q2's 22.6% market-data growth was partly
helped by an amended LSEG licence rather than purely organic adoption. If normalised volatility
slows volume while fee capture keeps declining, today's excellent margin can coexist with a much
weaker compounding engine—the exact failure mode the warning and break tests are designed to catch.

Why this is not the 7532.T mistake again: the order is not based on a capital-markets activity
headline alone. It is based on four filed years of revenue and operating-margin growth, current
cross-asset adoption, and an explicit test of whether volume actually converts to fees and market
share.
