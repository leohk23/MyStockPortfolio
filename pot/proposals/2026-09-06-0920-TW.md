## 2. Thesis

Tradeweb is a multi-asset electronic-marketplace network whose venues become more useful as
institutions, dealers, liquidity and trading workflows meet in one place. Four complete local
years show revenue rising from $1.189bn to $2.052bn and GAAP operating margin widening from 35.9%
to 41.2%, while Q2 2026 still produced 9.0% revenue growth and a 54.4% adjusted EBITDA margin in a
more normal volatility backdrop ([earnings.json](../../earnings.json),
[Q2 release](https://investors.tradeweb.com/static-files/e5696c92-ff61-4d32-85b0-678deedf8d9a)).
The market appears to treat lower fee capture as erosion; what it may be missing is that automated
protocols can deepen electronic adoption while transaction, subscription and market-data revenue
compound together.

## 6. The case against

**Dominant risk (`financial`):** Tradeweb's network may be adding notional trading volume faster
than economic value as product mix, volume discounts and client bargaining power compress fee
capture.

**Earliest observable:** The Q3 filing on **29 October 2026** will disclose transaction-fee growth,
ADV growth and variable fees per million against P3's 15-point and 10% thresholds
([Q2 filing and disclosure cadence](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000133/tw-20260630.htm),
[prices.json](../../prices.json)).

In Q2, ADV rose 18.2% but transaction fees grew only 8.3%; total variable fees per million fell
7.2%, cash-credit fees fell 11.4%, and growth skewed toward lower-fee short-tenor derivatives,
European credit and portfolio trading. August ADV still grew 13.7%, but its high-yield TRACE share
was 8.3% versus 8.5% in Q2, so the newest volume release does not settle whether economics are
compounding with activity. Impressive volume and share statistics can therefore coexist with
structurally weaker monetisation, while the two missing local capital-return measures make the
quality case less complete than MWA's
([Q2 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000133/tw-20260630.htm),
[August activity](https://investors.tradeweb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tradeweb-reports-august-2026-total-trading-volume-612-trillion)).

**Combined exposure:** TW is not in the human book, so the existing name weight is 0%; the order
adds another US financial-market infrastructure exposure to a book already **66.27% US** by market
value ([signals.json](../../signals.json)).

Why this is not the 7532.T mistake again: this is not a generic bet on electronic trading or
volatility. The case starts with four filed years of revenue and GAAP margin progression, admits
that two capital-return measures are unavailable, uses valuation only as a gate, and tests the
exact weakness in the latest filing—whether volume becomes transaction revenue while electronic
market share continues to rise.
