The FY2025 issuer statement reports operating profit of $13.743bn, a 23.40% margin, making the question whether reinvestment produces durable cash returns, not whether the business has yet become profitable ([annual release](https://www.astrazeneca.com/content/dam/az/PDF/2025/Q4-FY/Full-year-Q4-2025-results-announcement.pdf)).
