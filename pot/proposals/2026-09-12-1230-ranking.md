- **`earnings.json` carries no year-ago quarter.** It holds the trailing four or five quarters per name,
  so a quarter cannot be compared with the same quarter a year earlier. On 7532.T this is the difference

  Wishlisted.
- **PPIH's monthly sales history is not retrievable.** The company publishes the series and the current
  month renders, but prior months sit in PDFs that do not extract, so a trend cannot be built from the
  one reading available. Wishlisted.
- **The 00:32 run's `adjustedYears()` finding is closed** — `ownTtmEps()` now applies a current-year
  adjustment to the trailing multiple, and MWA's and TW's own bases have moved as a result. NVO's has
  not, which is stated in its P5 and is why it sizes at half.
- **`peWeeks` is under three years for four ranked names** — RSGN.SW 144, GME 129, AMSC 84, and on the
  excluded side GWRE 46 and MRVL 41 — while `peWindow` says "5y" for all of them. 7532.T's 207 weeks is
  over three years and its percentile is usable, but it begins **2022-09-30**, not five years ago, and is
  quoted that way above. Stated so the label is never read as a measurement.
