## 2. Thesis

I infer that part of the 39.3% discount to the historical reported-P/E median prices T2-style agent/open-banking substitution as well as credit-cycle risk; the repository cannot identify the market's motive. ## 6. The case against

**Theses:** T1 — cheaper inference can lower Experian's costs and those of its substitutes, with no quantified net benefit established; T2 — agent workflows and first-party underwriting can displace paid data/decision steps; T3 — identity and trusted decision records may gain demand, but no separately quantified compulsory AI-control revenue is established.

**Dominant risk (`competitive`):** Open-banking or agent underwriting replaces paid Experian data while price/mix keeps reported B2B revenue growing.

**Earliest observable:** A regulator's dated bureau-requirement implementation notice or a named lender's production replacement announcement, checked monthly from 20 October at the Fannie Mae/FHFA and lender sources in P3; Experian's next scheduled unit-disclosure opportunity is 18 November. No dependable external displacement series is scheduled, so Rule 4 halves the allocation.

Consumer performance is already weaker than B2B; [Q1 North American Consumer organic revenue fell 2% as breach contracts wound down](https://www.experianplc.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/trading-update--first-quarter-fy27). This is not proof of data displacement, but it shows why aggregate growth can hide weaker demand. Acquisitions and capitalised software consume real cash, and Benchmark exclusions are broader than independently identified one-offs. A three-bureau mortgage rule covers one use case and can change; it does not protect every Experian product. Continued growth today cannot certify the agent-era economics.

**Human-book overlap:** No EXPN/Experian holding in the public instrument list, hence **0% direct existing weight**. It adds a fresh name, but Experian's [Q1 North American share was 67% of group revenue](https://www.experianplc.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/trading-update--first-quarter-fy27), so a London listing is not an escape from the human book's 66.85% US exposure; Technology plus Communication Services already total 59.4% ([signals.book](../../signals.json)).
