## 2. Thesis

CME runs exchanges where customers trade futures and options to manage interest-rate, currency and commodity risks. It also clears trades, collecting collateral and standing between buyers and sellers, and sells market data.

The business case is recurring transaction and data income with low physical reinvestment, rather than a prediction about the direction of markets. FY2025 revenue rose 6.37%, operating profit rose 7.58% and operating margin reached 64.86%, while operating cash covered attributable earnings and capital expenditure was modest. The market may underweight the combination of a growing data franchise and broader trading activity, but whether extra activity still earns attractive fees is the unresolved test in P3.
