Now is the entry because the 28 August regular close of $218.28 values the freshly reported $7.91 trailing GAAP EPS at 27.60×, below the 38.50× point-in-time floor in the local history, while Q2 FY2027 revenue still grew 106% and gross margin was 75.0% ([local price](../../prices.json), [Q2 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).
The market may still be underestimating how quickly simultaneous demand from frontier labs, cloud providers and physical-AI workloads can turn the Rubin ramp into earnings, with management guiding Q3 revenue to $108.0bn ±2% even while assuming no China Data Center compute revenue ([Q2 results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).

This is not the 7532.T mistake again: the case is not a rebound narrative or a local-currency share
price. It is supported by four filed years of accelerating USD revenue and sustained high margins,
a current multiple below the company's own point-in-time range, and an explicit acknowledgement of
China, customer concentration and cyclicality rather than treating them as afterthoughts.

## 6. The case against

This may be the most dangerous point in the cycle to extrapolate NVIDIA. Two direct customers were
22% and 14% of FY2026 revenue, so a small number of buyers can turn today's shortage into tomorrow's
pause; export controls can also remove markets or strand inventory with little notice
([FY2026 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)).
The latest 75.0% gross margin and 117% Data Center growth may be peak economics produced by an
extraordinary hyperscaler build-out, while customers are simultaneously designing their own silicon
and regulators are scrutinising NVIDIA's position. Most importantly, this is not diversification:
the human book already owns 57 shares worth $12,441.96, or 7.08% of its locally priced value, so a
pot purchase doubles down on Leo's existing highest-conviction theme rather than exploring beyond
his radar ([holdings.json](../../holdings.json), [prices.json](../../prices.json)). The valuation is
cheap only against NVIDIA's short, boom-heavy P/E-band history—not in an absolute sense—and a
collapse in AI infrastructure returns could compress both earnings and the multiple at once.
