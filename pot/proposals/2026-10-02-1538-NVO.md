# NVO — Novo Nordisk A/S (NYSE ADR, 1 ADR = 1 B share)

semaglutide drugs for type 2 diabetes (Ozempic, Rybelsus) and obesity (Wegovy, as an injection and

wholesalers, pharmacies, insurers and national health systems, and the US is more than half of
sales. It earns the gap between a patent-protected price, after large US rebates, and the cost of

## 2. Thesis

Novo earns a 44.6% adjusted operating margin (H1 2026) and a 31% return on invested capital
(FY2025), and its obesity category is still growing in volume (+87% in the US, MAT May 2026). The
price is 9.4x trailing earnings, at the 1.6th percentile of 184 weeks and below its own floor, with
a 5.0% dividend yield. That prices Novo's earnings as shrinking for years. Management's own
ambition, which it says is not guidance, is 2026–30 revenue growth "in line with industry peers" at
a "broadly stable operating margin". The purchase is a bet that the decline priced in is deeper
than the business will deliver. **Whether volume growth actually outruns falling realised prices is
the question P3 tests. It is not assumed here (Rule 2).**

## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none. T1 to T3 cover AI model pricing, seat-priced software and agent governance, and
none of them bears on a drug maker's price or volume.

**Dominant risk (`competitive`):** Eli Lilly's tirzepatide and its oral successors, together with
US price concessions, push Novo's realised price down faster than its volume grows. Revenue then
shrinks inside a market whose volume is growing 80% or more.

**Earliest observable:** US Operations sales growth at CER and Wegovy pill sales in Novo's report on
**4 November 2026**, set against Lilly's own quarterly US tirzepatide revenue. Both come from
filings, both are dated, and both are within two quarters. P3's warning and break both test this.

- **The list-price cut lands in 2027.** From 1 January the US list price of Wegovy and Ozempic falls
  by about 50% and 35%. Net prices sit well below list after rebates, so the effect on revenue is
  smaller than the headline and is not quantified by Novo. But 2027 is the first full year exposed
  to it, which is why the break reads the 2027 outlook.
- **The trend is already against it.** US sales fell 4% at CER in H1 while US obesity volume grew
  87%. That is share and price being lost together. Q2's +4% is one quarter.
- **The margin is a question, not a comfort.** 44.6% is extraordinary for a manufacturer, and it
  lasts only while patents and constrained supply let Novo price above competition. ROIC has fallen
  from 57% in FY2023 to 31% in FY2025 as capacity spending arrived before the revenue did
  ([prices](../../prices.json) `capital.history`). The US semaglutide patent runs to 2032.
- **Pipeline setbacks.** Q2 carried DKK 6.3bn of pipeline impairments, DKK 4.0bn of it monlunabant
  ([6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000353278/000035327826000023/caq22026.htm)).
- **The cheap multiple may be the right price.** A drug maker six years from US patent expiry, with
  a list-price cut ahead, should trade low. At 9.4x and a 5% yield the market is wrong only if
  earnings hold roughly flat to 2030.
- **Combined exposure.** Leo's human book does not hold Novo ([signals](../../signals.json)
  `book`), so this adds no overlap there. Inside the pot it would be the **third healthcare name**,
  after AZN.L and EUZ.DE, so pharma pricing policy would hit two pot positions at once.

  This proposal stands on its own.
