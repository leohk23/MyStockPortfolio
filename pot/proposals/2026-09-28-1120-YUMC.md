## 2. Thesis

The restaurant network is producing improving operating earnings and cash while franchise openings spread part of the expansion cost across other investors. The completed Pizza Hut rights purchase supplies a specific new earnings source through saved licence payments, rather than relying on a broad Chinese consumer recovery. My investment inference is that a modestly growing cash-generative network merits a small position even without recovery to its historical multiple; whether acquisition financing and distributions consume those benefits is the unresolved test in P3.

This is not the 7532.T tourism mistake: the case rests on filed profit, cash after capital spending and measured capital productivity, not a rebound narrative. Supply-chain density and repeat transactions are plausible reasons for operating efficiency to persist, but delivery costs and discounting can still consume it. No permanently high margin is assumed.
