## 2. Thesis to test after results

**The business:** NVIDIA sells processors, networking and computing systems, with software used to train and run AI models. Cloud operators and other computing customers buy that capacity; revenue depends on equipment demand rather than a subscription to a single model.

**The engine:** [Local filed years](../../earnings.json) show revenue rising from $26.974bn in FY2023 to $215.938bn in FY2026. Operating margins were 20.7%, 54.1%, 62.4% and 60.4% across those years. This is rapid growth with a recent margin decline, not a smooth margin series. Q2's locally recorded operating margin is 66.2%. High returns attract substitutes; persistence remains a question.

**Why now:** the local price has fallen 29.28% over a month, both valuation filters pass, and a new results release is due today. The CEO transition and export-control change make the next disclosure unusually relevant.

**What the market is missing:** a possible opportunity is that lower model costs expand useful inference faster than compute prices fall. That is a hypothesis, not evidence that the discount is wrong. T1 could instead compress customers' economics and their ability to pay. This run does not certify a durable mispricing before the new figures.

**What has to happen:** operating growth must turn into customer cash without progressively greater NVIDIA-funded support, and the new outlook must survive the regulatory change. The balance-sheet checks below test that proposition; revenue growth alone cannot.

## 6. The case against

**Theses:** T1 bears directly because cheaper open models can change compute intensity and customer economics in either direction. T2 bears indirectly because agents change the workloads buying inference; seat displacement does not establish NVIDIA revenue. T3 is not a separate investment premise here.

**Dominant risk (`accounting`):** supported-customer demand and investment gains make reported earnings look more self-funding and repeatable than the underlying economics.

**Earlier risks:** `earlierRisks.NVDA` is absent, but the obsolete [31 August proposal](archive/2026-08-31-1526-NVDA.md) explicitly identified circular demand. That accounting risk is **still dominant**, and P3 retains its receivables/support tests. Its credit-loss consequence is financial, not a separate reason to count the same risk twice. The earlier draft's mixed-source adjusted TTM is not reused.

**Earliest observable:** the Q3 balance sheet and support disclosures, next expected with today's results/filing cycle; review by 20 November. H1 OCF was **$74.421bn versus $118.010bn net income**, or **63.06% conversion**. Q2 disclosed $99bn equity investments, $25bn equity commitments, $56bn cloud/third-party lease commitments and $108.5bn maximum guarantee exposure including the August agreement. These categories are not additive current cash liabilities. The major guarantee starts with future construction phases, first expected FY2029; eventual loss is a slower risk than current disclosure growth. [Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000075/nvda-20260726.htm).

Aggregate receivables and support can warn before default, but do not isolate sales to supported customers. Half allocation is the default for that unresolved visibility if a later proposal otherwise clears. Margin strength cannot rebut it.

Two secondary changes matter. The [12 November company announcement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition) confirms Huang's departure and Kress's interim appointment; the next outlook must be assessed under that leadership. The [16 November BIS announcement](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-issues-rule-addressing-remote-access-american-super-intelligence-technology) extends controls to remote access through third-country data centres. It supplies no NVIDIA revenue impact. I do not equate a China-free direct-compute guide with immunity to remote-access restrictions.

**Leo's total exposure:** The book overlap was set aside at his request.

**Why this is not the 7532.T mistake again:** the AI story and low historical multiple have not been accepted as sufficient. The case needs cash conversion, the quality of customer funding, a comparable earnings basis and a post-rule outlook.
