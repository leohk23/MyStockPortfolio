## 2. Thesis

**This includes a reported-profit recovery: H1 operating profit fell 2.6%, despite revenue growth, and I require FY2026 reported operating profit to return to growth over FY2025.** The business case is repeat purchases of consumer-health products: Q2 organic revenue grew 3.1%, including positive volume/mix, while Oral Health grew faster than the group ([H1 release](https://www.haleon.com/news/press-releases/financial/2026/2026-half-year-results)). ## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none.

**Dominant risk (`accounting`):** recurring restructuring and delayed cash payments make adjusted profit growth overstate the cash earnings ordinary shareholders can retain.

**Earliest observable:** new restructuring disclosures at the 29 October trading statement; the complete profit reconciliation and cash/provision movements at the FY2026 filing, checked by 5 March 2027. This is inside two quarters but cannot be cleared from a sales update. P3's already-active warning and the half-allocation acknowledge that limitation.

The high gross margin is vulnerable to private-label substitution and the cost of keeping brands visible. Positive volume/mix is evidence of current demand, not proof that future pricing will hold. Returns on all the capital remain far below the headline product margin; I do not remove goodwill to manufacture a better ROIC. Debt and buybacks can consume the cash the brands generate, while a GBP listing still carries substantial overseas operating exposure.
