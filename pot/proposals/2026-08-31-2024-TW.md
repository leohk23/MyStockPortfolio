## 2. Thesis

Tradeweb is a multi-asset electronic-marketplace network whose value grows as more institutions, dealers and workflows meet on the same venues. Why now: four locally filed years show revenue rising from $1.189bn to $2.052bn and GAAP operating margin widening from 35.9% to 41.2%, while Q2 2026 still produced 9.0% revenue growth in a more normal volatility backdrop ([earnings.json](../../earnings.json), [Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000132/tw-2026xq2xex991.htm)). The market appears to treat lower fee capture as erosion; what it may be missing is that lower-fee automated products can deepen adoption and market share while transaction, subscription and market-data revenue compound together.

## 3. Falsifier

**Warning:** At the Q3 2026 filing scheduled for **29 October 2026**, trigger a full re-read if transaction-fee revenue growth trails total ADV growth by **15 percentage points or more** while total variable fees per million also decline by **more than 10% year on year**. Q2 already showed the milder version—18.2% ADV growth, 8.3% transaction-fee growth and a 7.2% fall in total variable fees per million—so this threshold tests whether mix compression is worsening rather than merely existing ([Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000133/tw-20260630.htm), [local result date](../../prices.json)).

**Break:** At the FY2026 10-K, to be checked by **1 March 2027**, put the position under §5.1 if two consecutive quarters have both (a) flat or declining transaction-fee revenue despite positive ADV growth and (b) year-on-year declines in Tradeweb's fully electronic U.S. high-grade and high-yield TRACE shares. That combination would show that neither monetisation nor electronic market share is compounding, directly breaking the business case; Q2 reference shares were 19.0% in high grade and 8.5% in high yield, both up year on year ([Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000132/tw-2026xq2xex991.htm)).

## 6. The case against

Tradeweb's network may be adding volume faster than economic value. In Q2, ADV rose 18.2% but transaction fees grew only 8.3%; total variable fees per million fell 7.2%, cash-credit fees fell 11.4%, and some growth came from lower-fee short-tenor derivatives, European credit and portfolio trading. If that mix shift persists, impressive volume and share statistics can coexist with structurally weaker monetisation, while competition and client bargaining power stop margin expansion ([Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000133/tw-20260630.htm), [Q2 release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1758730/000175873026000132/tw-2026xq2xex991.htm)).

Why this is not the 7532.T mistake again: this is not a generic bet on electronic trading or volatility. The case starts with four filed years of revenue and GAAP margin progression, uses the multiple only as a gate, and tests the exact weakness in the latest filing—whether volume becomes transaction revenue while electronic market share continues to rise.
