## 2. Thesis

**The business:** Eckert & Ziegler supplies radioactive materials, components and manufacturing services to medical and industrial customers. It earns from isotope products, services and occasional licences rather than depending on one drug's clinical success.

**The engine:** Local annual operating margins were 20.7%, 18.9%, 22.7% and 24.1% over FY2022–25; these are vendor-field ratios, not a certified adjusted series. FY2025 ROIC is 20.0%. Qualified production and customer integration can support margins, but returns on additional investment must be demonstrated.

**Why now:** The [12 November release](https://www.ezag.com/press/detail/?newsid=9eed2a67-11a7-4433-b1a4-a8d3576b3b73) shows third-quarter sales growth of 6% and adjusted EBIT growth of 25%. The new cash statement also supplies the reading the earlier WAIT lacked.

**What the market is missing:** My inference is that the uneven timing of licences obscures progress in the supply business. The release's nine-month sales comparison improves from 2% to 4% without last year's licence revenue. That supports further investigation; it does not establish that expansion and related-party financing will leave cash for shareholders.

**What has to happen:** Growing operating earnings must produce cash after investment, leases and related-party funding. A positive medical-demand story without that conversion is insufficient. P3 tests the cash claim directly.

## Second reader

**Would I place it:** yes, because the one finding that bears on the dominant risk (the 12 November bond extension) is read by the FY2026 cash-after-advances test on 24 March 2027, and Pentixapharm's own cash makes a further draw before then unlikely.

### What I examined

The dominant risk is related-party financing, and the draft costs it from EUZ's side only: €5m drawn, €13.5m undrawn. It does not ask what the borrower is doing, or whether the facility's terms moved. I also checked whether the €80m adjusted-EBIT guide had been cut quietly, and whether the fourth quarter it now needs is unusual.

### What I found

- **The bond was amended on the day of the results** ([Pentixapharm ad hoc, 12 Nov 2026](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/ad-hoc-mitteilungen/pentixapharm-holding-ag-extends-drawdown-period-and-term-of-its-convertible-bond-with-eckert-und/70496502)). The term moves from 31 Dec 2027 to 31 Dec 2030. Repayment of the €5m drawn is deferred three years. The undrawn €13.5m stays callable by Pentixapharm until 30 June 2030. The coupon rises from 4.00% to 6.00% from 1 January 2027. Pentixapharm puts the amendment at about €3.7m, the higher maximum interest over the extended term if all 37 bonds are issued. EUZ's 9M report records the €5.0m loan receivable and €0.1m accrued interest, with no impairment or allowance stated ([EUZ 9M report](https://www.ezag.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/euz326_e_offen.pdf), pp. 15–16).
- **The borrower is better funded than the draft implies** ([Pentixapharm 9M 2026 report](https://www.pentixapharm.com/s/Pentixapharm_Q3_2026_e.pdf), pp. 4, 20–21). It held €21.9m of cash at 30 Sep 2026, against €4.6m at 31 Dec 2025. Its nine-month net loss was €7.7m and its operating cash outflow €7.3m. Its FY2026 loss guide is now about €13m, down from about €21.6m. Its directors state that resources cover at least twelve months, with no going-concern qualification, and that funding reaches into 2028.
- **The guide is confirmed, not cut.** About €320m of sales and €80m of adjusted EBIT, set on 26 March and confirmed on 13 August and 12 November ([EUZ release, 12 Nov](https://www.ad-hoc-news.de/boerse/news/corporate-news/eqs-news-eckert-und-ziegler-bereinigtes-ebit-im-dritten-quartal-25-prozent-prognose-2026-bestaetigt/70495599)). In FY2025 Q4 carried 34.1% of local operating income (€25.690m of €75.276m, [earnings.json](../../earnings.json)), so the guide needs no unusual quarter.
