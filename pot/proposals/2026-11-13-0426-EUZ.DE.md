**What the market is missing:** My inference is that the uneven timing of licences obscures progress in the supply business. That supports further investigation; it does not establish that expansion and related-party financing will leave cash for shareholders.

**What has to happen:** Growing operating earnings must produce cash after investment, leases and related-party funding. A positive medical-demand story without that conversion is insufficient. P3 tests the cash claim directly.
