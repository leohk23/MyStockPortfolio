## 6. The case against

Do not buy this if you want a predictable compounder. Filed revenue fell from $5.2728b in FY2023 to $3.6299b in FY2025, only the latest of those three years had positive GAAP operating income, and Q1's recovery was concentrated in collectibles while hardware fell 3.4% and software fell 13.0% ([FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326380/000132638026000013/gme-20260131.htm), [Q1 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326380/000132638026000025/gme-20260502.htm)). The headline 13.60× P/E flatters the business because $268.4m of Q1 profit was an unrealized eBay derivative gain; even the normalized 16.50× multiple rests on a 23.65× floor struck from only 30 positive-earnings weeks after years of losses, so this is a thin historical anchor, not proof of cheapness ([prices.json](../../prices.json), [Q1 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326380/000132638026000025/gme-20260502.htm)).

Most importantly, GameStop is becoming a concentrated capital-allocation vehicle: it held 4,710 Bitcoin, posted $983.3m collateral against eBay derivatives, remains committed to an eBay transaction after eBay rejected its $55.5b proposal, and has agreed to retire $1.4b of convertible debt for an as-yet-undetermined number of new shares ([Q1 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326380/000132638026000025/gme-20260502.htm), [eBay rejection](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1065088/000155278126000322/e26249_ex99-2.htm), [GameStop proposal](https://investor.gamestop.com/news-releases/news-details/2026/GameStop-Proposes-to-Acquire-eBay-at-125-00-Per-Share/default.aspx), [debt exchange 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326380/000132638026000042/gme-20260802.htm)). The strongest reason to reject the order is therefore that the apparent retail turnaround may be real but the shareholder outcome is increasingly dominated by Ryan Cohen's opaque, volatile investment and dilution decisions; £125 is sized as an experiment in that uncertainty, not as confidence that it has gone away.
