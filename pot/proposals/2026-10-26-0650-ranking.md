### Where I disagree and could not settle it

**Is Novo a WAIT or an EXCLUDE?** The draft and I both keep it at WAIT. The case for EXCLUDE today is that the issuer's own guide is for lower earnings, so §2.4b's recovery test is not met *on the company's own forecast*.
