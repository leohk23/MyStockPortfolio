## 2. Thesis

R&S Group supplies transformers required by grids, renewables, storage, industrial infrastructure and data centres, and FY2025 earnings quality is visible in 8.6% organic sales growth, a 20.9% EBITDA margin, CHF48.1m free cash flow and lower net debt ([FY2025 results](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/news/rands-group-publishes-annual-and-non-financial-reports-2025/f7d7c976-e100-48b1-953d-ea92e96928b1)).
Why now: H1 sales fell 13% and EBITDA fell 23% as utility inventories normalized and longer-cycle power-transformer deliveries slipped, yet backlog rose 17% to a record CHF357.9m, book-to-bill was 1.2× and deliveries extend into 2027–2028 ([H1 update](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/news/rands-group-provides-trading-update-for-h1-2026/bb41967c-ac88-4041-be50-200b3f26951b)).
The market may be treating the delivery delay as a transformer down-cycle when backlog mix and new Łódź capacity suggest part is timing and capacity conversion; converting signed orders while holding a 19%–21% margin range would restore growth without requiring a commodity-price surge.

This is not the 7532.T mistake again: the order does not rely on a rebound story, acquisition-
inflated headline growth or favourable currency. It separates 47% reported growth from 8.6%
organic growth, confronts current sales and order-intake declines, checks cash conversion, and
makes backlog conversion—not the share price—the falsifiable claim.

## 3. Falsifier

The thesis is false if R&S Group's FY2026 results report an **EBITDA margin below 19.0%**. That is

([H1 update](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/news/rands-group-provides-trading-update-for-h1-2026/bb41967c-ac88-4041-be50-200b3f26951b)).

## 6. The case against

The share may be cheap because earnings are already rolling over. H1 adjusted order intake fell
10%, sales fell 13%, EBITDA fell 23%, and FY2026 revenue guidance of CHF410m–CHF420m is roughly flat
with FY2025 and below the mid-term growth range. Utility customers have excess inventory and
installation bottlenecks, while the higher power-transformer mix takes longer to produce; backlog
can look reassuring for years before becoming cash, and factory delays would hurt revenue and
margins ([H1 update](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/news/rands-group-provides-trading-update-for-h1-2026/bb41967c-ac88-4041-be50-200b3f26951b)).

Much of FY2025's reported growth came from the first full year of Kyte Powertech, and this is a
short listed history of only 179 weeks. The strongest bear case is that the market correctly sees
peak transformer margins, acquisition-flattered growth and an expensive capacity build meeting
normalized utility demand; the 12.70× multiple is only a gate, not an answer.
