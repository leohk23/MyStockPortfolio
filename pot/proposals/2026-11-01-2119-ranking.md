## Wärtsilä: why WAIT, not PROPOSE

**The business.** Wärtsilä sells engines and propulsion systems for ships (Marine), and gas-engine power plants for utilities and, increasingly, US data centres (Energy). About half of its sales are service: spare parts, maintenance agreements and upgrades on the installed fleet. Service was **53.7% of Q3 2026 net sales (€802m of €1,493m)** ([Q3 interim](https://www.wartsila.com/media/news/27-10-2026-wartsila-s-interim-report-january-september-2026-3806530)). Energy Storage has been held for sale as a discontinued operation since June 2026.

**The engine is good and improving.** Comparable operating margin was **8.3% in 2023, 10.8% in 2024 and 12.0% in 2025** ([2023](https://www.wartsila.com/media/news/31-01-2024-wartsila-s-financial-statements-bulletin-january-december-2023-3396344), [2024](https://www.wartsila.com/media/news/05-02-2025-wartsila-s-financial-statements-bulletin-january-december-2024-3545975), [2025](https://www.wartsila.com/media/news/04-02-2026-wartsila-s-financial-statements-bulletin-january-december-2025-3713746) bulletins), and **14.2% in Q3 2026 against 12.8%**. Local filed operating income agrees in direction: €452m, €680m, €816m. Local Q3 2026 opinc is €212m, the comparable figure. ROIC was 12.60%, 21.86% and **37.66%** over FY2023–25, gross profit over assets 38.06% and turnover 0.816× (FY2025, `prices.json`). Q3 orders rose **33% to €2,368m** and the order book to **€9,876m (+22%)**, with book-to-bill 1.59. Prospects: *"We expect the demand environment over the next 12 months to remain at a similar level in both Energy and Marine to that of the past 12 months"* ([Cision release](https://news.cision.com/wartsila-corporation/r/wartsila-s-interim-report-january-september-2026,c4415636)).

**What the cheapness actually is.** The Scan fired on `cheapOn: cash`: P/OCF **9.48×**, at the **8.82nd percentile of 136 weeks from 2024-04-05**, against a 12.84× median. Under three years, so the percentile is **indicative**. The window starts there because FY2022 was a loss year (EPS −€0.11, operating cash −€62m), so this is not a missing `HISTORY_FROM` entry. The brief says that when cash is cheap and earnings are not, "find the non-cash charge, or the working-capital release". Here it is a release from customer advances. Operating cash over net income was **3.19× in 2023, 2.40× in 2024 and 2.55× in 2025** (`prices.json` capital cfo against `earnings.json` nic). Q3 2026 operating cash was €583m against €340m, on orders up a third. The same advances sit in current liabilities, and they shrink the invested capital behind the 37.66% ROIC: the average was only **€1,648.5m** on €8,474m of assets. Both the cash multiple and the ROIC therefore measure an order book that is still growing, and they reverse when it stops. On earnings, reported P/E is **24.9×** (€1.03 TTM) at the **11.03rd percentile** against a 35× median, outside the decile. Yahoo's recurring basis is at 7.41%, unsourced.

**The EPS also mixes in a business being sold.** Q3 EPS was **€0.21 against €0.31**, but **€0.28 against €0.29 from continuing operations** ([issuer release](https://www.wartsila.com/media/news/27-10-2026-wartsila-s-interim-report-january-september-2026-3806530)). So about €0.07 of Q3 was Energy Storage's discontinued loss, now recorded as non-arithmetic evidence in [adjustments](../adjustments.json). Continuing EPS was flat year on year while the comparable operating result rose 19%. Something below operating profit absorbed the growth, and I did not establish what.

**No venue could be shown to clear the fee gate.** Trading 212 shows only a CFD page, [WRT1VH.FI](https://www.trading212.com/trading-instruments/cfd/WRT1VH.FI). Its Invest URL redirects to the generic instrument list: strong, not conclusive, evidence that no real-share line exists. [IBKR's Europe schedule](https://www.interactivebrokers.co.uk/en/pricing/commissions-stocks-europe.php) has no Finland row. Finnish transfer tax does **not** apply to a listed share ([vero.fi](https://www.vero.fi/en/individuals/property/transfer-tax/corporate-stock/)). Without a cost, §4.1a cannot be shown to pass, and an IBKR minimum of about €1.25 or more on a £40–90 ticket would likely fail it. The brief says to "treat the cost as unknown rather than inventing one". A proposal whose own cost gate probably fails is not executable, so this is logged to the [wishlist](../data-wishlist.md).

**What would make it a PROPOSE, for the second reader or the next run.** Two things: a confirmed whole-share line at a known cost, and an answer to whether the valuation passes once the advances are set aside. On the second, continuing-operations EPS for the trailing four quarters would put the P/E on the business being kept. **Why this is not 7532.T again.** The order book and margins are in the accounts, not the story.
