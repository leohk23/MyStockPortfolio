## 2. Thesis

NVIDIA is a full-stack accelerated-computing platform—chips, systems, networking and software—whose economics improve as more AI workloads standardise around its ecosystem. Q2 FY2027 revenue doubled year on year to $96.2bn, Data Center revenue rose 117% to $89.0bn, and GAAP gross margin held at 75.0%, evidence that growth still has exceptional margin quality rather than merely exceptional volume. The market is underestimating how long the Rubin ramp and software/network lock-in can compound cash earnings, but the position is deliberately small because NVIDIA is also investing in and guaranteeing parts of the ecosystem that buys its products.

**Break:** By the FY2027 annual filing, checkable on **1 March 2027**, put the position under §5.1
if NVIDIA reports a material customer or partner default, a called guarantee, or a credit-loss
provision tied to these support arrangements. It also breaks if receivable days remain above 65
for two consecutive quarters **and** a support category continues growing faster than Data Center
revenue in both. NVIDIA does not disclose how much revenue comes from customers it invests in,
finances or guarantees, so the central circular-demand risk is **not fully falsifiable today**;

## 6. The case against

NVIDIA increasingly helps finance and underwrite the ecosystem that buys its products. At July
quarter-end it carried $99bn of equity investments, $25bn of equity commitments and $3.5bn of
AI-cloud guarantees; in August it added a guarantee capped at $105bn for an OpenAI-linked data
centre buildout, while receivables reached $63.1bn and some customers receive 90-day-to-one-year
terms. Revenue and gross margin can remain excellent while supported customers order equipment,
so those headline numbers do not falsify circular demand. If NVIDIA-supported financing is masking
end demand, credit losses, guarantee calls and a simultaneous earnings/multiple contraction can
arrive together; because supported-customer revenue is undisclosed, this risk cannot be fully
measured before damage appears.

cash-earnings quality, a current normalised valuation check and explicit balance-sheet observables,
not on the AI capital-expenditure narrative alone.
