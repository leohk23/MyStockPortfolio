**Why earnings look cheap and cash looks dear.** The stored P/E percentile is struck on annual
GAAP EPS. For FY2026 that is RMB44.00 per ADS, while the company's own non-GAAP figure was
**RMB26.80**, so GAAP is 64% higher. Net income of RMB103,592m against operating income of RMB59,665m
(`earnings.json`) is the same gap: the year's earnings included large investment gains. Operating
cash carries none of those marks. It reflects the operating business, which spent FY2026 subsidising
quick commerce, so operating income fell **59%** (147,076 → 59,665) and operating cash fell to
RMB76,213m from 163,509m. So the P/E's denominator is held up by non-cash gains, while the P/OCF's
falls with the operating business. **This quarter widens the gap.** Swapping Sep 2025 for Sep 2026 in
`earnings.json`'s quarters gives a trailing GAAP EPS of RMB45.94, so **12.09x** at $83.31 (12.02x on
the issuer's own quarterly figures). On the company's adjusted basis, the four quarters to September
(7.09 + 0.62 + 8.52 + 8.10) give RMB24.33, so **22.83x**. The September quarter alone, annualised,
gives 17.14x. On cash, trailing operating cash of RMB84,077m (76,213 − 30,771 + 38,635) gives about
**15.55x**, against a five-year P/OCF median of 9.23x and a tenth percentile of 6.10x. At this price,
the cash leg would need the price below about $32.66. Both are sensitivities on the stored per-ADS
basis at the stored CNY rate, not rebuilt percentiles. They ignore the August placing, which adds
about 3.8% more shares and so makes every per-ADS figure here slightly flattering.

**Consequence for the Scan.** The ceiling is struck on the published annual EPS, so it stays at

P/E will drop from 20.2x to about 12x on gains alone, and the name will keep firing §6.1. That firing
is the same artefact as today's, not new cheapness.
