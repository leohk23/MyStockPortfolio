## Safran: the percentile is an accounting artefact

The Scan has SAF.PA at the **3.6th percentile of 83 weeks from 2024-04-05**, an indicative window of
under three years. That figure divides the price by IFRS EPS ([earnings](../../earnings.json)), which
for FY2022–25 reads **−5.76, 4.70, −1.60, 17.17**. Safran hedges its dollar sales, and under IFRS the
fair-value change of those hedges runs through net income.

- The loss years void the multiple, which is why the window is only 83 weeks.
- The annual series changes earnings basis materially. The pipeline also rebases annual earnings
  to the latest share count (`normaliseEps`), so the stored FY2023 €4.70 is not the historical
  denominator unchanged: local net income and the FY2025 anchor imply approximately €8.24.
  Today's annual-basis multiple, €271.90 ÷ €17.17 = **15.8x**, must not be read as a clean
  recurring-earnings comparison.

From Safran's [FY2025 release, 13 February 2026](https://www.safran-group.com/download/press_release/file/2265697),
now opened directly by the second reader; the drafter had used a republication:

| Measure | FY2025 |
|---|---|
| IFRS EPS | €17.17, agreeing with local |
| Company-adjusted EPS | €7.60 |
| The gap, mainly | a €5,701m pre-tax favourable change in hedge fair value, plus purchase-price amortisation |
| Adjusted EPS, H1 2025 | €3.80 ([issuer, 31 July 2025](https://www.safran-group.com/pressroom/safran-reports-its-first-half-2025-results-2025-07-31)) |
| Adjusted EPS, H1 2026 | €4.63 ([issuer, 28 July 2026](https://www.safran-group.com/download/press_release/file/2274730)) |

Twelve months to June 2026 on the company's basis are €3.80 (H2 2025, derived as FY €7.60 less H1
€3.80) plus €4.63, which is **approximately €8.43, so 32.3x** at €271.90. This sums rounded
per-period EPS with different weighted share counts; it is a research approximation, not an
issuer-reported TTM. No comparable adjusted historical percentile has been established here.

The basis that survives the hedge noise is cash. P/OCF is **19.9x, at the 30.9th percentile of 188
weeks**, against a 21.3x median. It is outside the decile, but D71 says neither multiple overrules
the other. This is evidence against the bargain claim, not an automatic exclusion. §7.2a permits
an uncertified earnings basis at reduced size; the decision to wait rests on the business and
price argument in the second-reader section.

The business is good. ROIC was 15.9% in 2025, up from 10.0% in 2022, with gross profit over assets
of 23.7% and turnover of 0.50. The 2026 outlook was last raised on 28 July: recurring operating income
€6.4–6.5bn and free cash flow €4.7–4.9bn. That is not today's question. The Sweep's aftermarket
inference is untested, and it is not tested by price.

**Recorded:** the three half-years and FY2025 adjusted EPS under `normalizedEps.SAF.PA`, and the
€5,701m hedge gain as evidence (`arithmetic: false`, pre-tax) in [adjustments](../adjustments.json).

**Data-quality finding for §5.** Safran's derivatives do **not** qualify for hedge accounting;
its [FY2025 accounting notes](https://www.safran-group.com/download/press_release/file/2265697)
explain the profit-and-loss remeasurement. The drafter's generalisation to every IFRS hedger,
including unexamined MTU and Airbus, is withdrawn. The issue is recorded in the
[data wishlist](../data-wishlist.md); it is not a `HISTORY_FROM` case.
