## 2. Thesis

The completed Pizza Hut rights acquisition creates a specific change in unit economics through retained licence payments, so the next results can test an operational change already made ([completion notice](https://ir.yumchina.com/static-files/b860fd22-dbf0-42d4-adb1-50183734b945)). My inference is that the market's broad China-consumer discount underweights these opportunities to earn on an installed network, although the acquisition still has to earn its cost and the store economics below must survive competition.
