## 2. Thesis

**The business:** PDD runs the Pinduoduo and Temu marketplaces, charging merchants for advertising and transaction services. This is a recovery proposal: audited FY2025 operating profit **fell 14.1%**. It is not a claim that the latest full year was growing.

**The engine:** Filed operating margins were 23.7%, 27.5% and 21.6% in 2023–25: substantial but inconsistent. Current-method FY2025 ROIC is 26.9%. H1 2026 operating profit subsequently rose 13.0%, while operating cash exceeded net income. Those are separate tests of operating recovery and cash backing; neither proves that merchants will keep paying.

**Why now:** Two reported quarters show an operating recovery after the annual setback. The next quarterly disclosure can test its continuation before committing more capital. The opportunity is to own a recovering merchant-services business, rather than extrapolate the share-price fall or a Chinese consumer rebound.

**What the market is missing:** My inference is that the price allows little credit for the operating recovery because reported net profit is still falling. I assign value to that divergence, without treating the unexplained losses below operating profit as temporary or assuming PDD wins the subsidy contest.

**What has to happen:** Merchant-advertising revenue must remain growing, and FY2026 operating profit must exceed audited FY2025. The proposal fails if that recovery does not reach the filed accounts by March 2027.

Figures and their reconciliation are in P5. The merchant-retention question remains open and is tested in P3, not assumed here.
