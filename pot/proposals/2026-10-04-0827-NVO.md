## 2. Thesis

Novo sells established chronic-disease treatments with FY2025 reported operating margin of 41.3% and ROIC of 30.6%, although margin has retreated from 2024. The reason to buy a small position now is the commercial broadening of the obesity franchise: oral Wegovy had exceeded 265,000 weekly prescriptions by 17 July, and the issuer raised its full-year adjusted outlook in August ([company release](https://www.novonordisk.com/content/nncorp/global/en/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=916590)). My inference is that a medicine franchise with current volume uptake and international distribution deserves a small recovery position, without assuming it will restore its former dominance or margin.

## 6. The case against

**Theses:** none

**Dominant risk (competitive):** Lilly takes patients while lower realised prices prevent Novo's expanding treatment volume from sustaining earnings.

**Earliest observable:** Both companies' Q3 reports, checked by 5 November; US adjusted sales and the rival-product comparison in P3 directly address this risk.

The current high margin reflects an established franchise, manufacturing and distribution, but those advantages can coexist with share loss. The [September capital-markets presentation](https://www.novonordisk.com/content/dam/nncorp/global/en/investors/pdfs/capital-markets-day/2026/financials-presentation-cmd-2026.pdf) describes margin and long-term growth ambitions as ambitions rather than financial guidance; I give them no earnings credit. The low historical percentile also contains the very provision distortion the cash-sized order is meant to acknowledge. Cash cheapness is corroboration, not a clean owner-earnings calculation after all manufacturing investment.

**Combined exposure:** No Novo listing is in the human holdings, so existing direct company weight is 0%; Healthcare is 1.00% of that book. The pot already owns AZN.L, so this adds medicine-sector exposure despite being a fresh company. Dollar trading does not make its operating exposure purely American.

**Why this is not the 7532.T mistake:** Prescription uptake is insufficient on its own. The size is reduced for uncertain earnings; filed margin decay and capital returns are shown; the dated test asks whether volume survives competition and pricing, with DKK, USD and GBP kept distinct.
