# Questions for Leo

Gaps in the rules, found by the Deep dive's second reader (D106): a judgement `strategy.md` does not
settle, as distinct from a doubt about one name. Each is asked once. The examiner decides it for that
day and adds a row here, with `open` in the last column. Leo's answer replaces `open`, with the
decision number it became, and from then on it is a rule.
