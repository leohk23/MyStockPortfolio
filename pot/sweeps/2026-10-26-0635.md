## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The energy shock now sits beside, rather than instead of, a sovereign-financing
strain. Locally, the US 10-year yield is **4.988%** and the five-year **4.805%**, up 19.1% and
28.5% respectively this year ([local market data](../../prices.json)). The BIS's 29 September
bulletin says near-record government debt and a larger non-bank footprint have reshaped sovereign
debt markets; more public debt is expected to worsen liquidity and increase the risk of market
dysfunction
([BIS Bulletin 136](https://www.bis.org/publications/bulletin-136-high-public-debt-nbfis-and-risk-market-dysfunction)).
This is not merely Dalio's saved opinion. It is a separate institutional warning about the market
mechanism through which high debt can become unstable.

At the same time, the IMF forecasts global inflation at **4.7% in 2026**, with energy-importing
economies outside the technology value chain facing the hardest outlook
([October WEO](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO/Issues/2026/10/13/world-economic-outlook-october-2026)).
The IEA says diesel benchmarks remain nearly twice crude and pre-crisis levels, and that lost
inventories are unlikely to be rebuilt before the second half of 2027
([October Oil Market Report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-october-2026)). I went
beyond the local macro block for sovereign-market liquidity and the product-market detail; the
block has yields and WTI, not either of those mechanisms.

**Inference.** A high discount rate and a high physical-input bill arriving together is hostile to
leveraged, capital-intensive businesses whose 2026 profit depends on one exceptional spread. That
raises the burden of proof on yesterday's Kongsberg Maritime and LyondellBasell candidates rather
than creating a third one. Market infrastructure is the obvious second-order beneficiary of
volatility, but Leo already owns CME and Tradeweb and watches Euronext, Deutsche Börse and LSEG.
Adding another exchange would duplicate a thesis and a sector, not discover an exposure. The more
useful action this morning is to preserve cash and wait for this week's company results.
