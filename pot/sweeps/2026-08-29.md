### What I take from it

The useful hunt is not for another chip name. It is for infrastructure businesses where actual
orders have arrived but the share price still discounts failed conversion, and for value-facing
consumer businesses where weak real spending is a tailwind only if execution and margins hold.
That produces RSGN.SW, AMRC and BME.L below. The insurance candidate is a separate quiet divergence:
Lancashire's underwriting improved while Beazley's deteriorated, yet Lancashire's share price is
roughly flat over one year. WTI is up 30.35% over one year but down 9.46% over three months
([local macro data](../../prices.json)); that cooling move and the IMF's normalisation assumption
are why I did not turn the war or oil shock into a commodity candidate.

## 3. Candidates

### RSGN.SW — SIX Swiss Exchange — R&S Group

**Why:** The transformer maker's H1 backlog reached a record CHF357.9m, up 17%, with a 19.0% EBITDA
margin and 6.4% free-cash-flow margin, while the share is down 46.41% over one year and trades at
about 13.2 times trailing earnings
([company update](https://ir.the-rsgroup.com/news),
[price and valuation](https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/equities/tearsheet/summary?s=RSGN%3ASWX)).

**What would have to be true:** The adjusted 10% fall in H1 order intake must be temporary
normalisation rather than the start of a transformer down-cycle, and backlog must convert while
capacity investment preserves cash generation; otherwise the apparently low multiple is peak-cycle
earnings in disguise
([detailed update](https://www.eqs-news.com/de/news/ad-hoc/rs-group-provides-trading-update-for-h1-2026/bb41967c-ac88-4041-be50-200b3f26951b)).

### AMRC — NYSE — Ameresco

**Why:** The market value is down about 10% over one year while total project backlog rose 32% to
$6.73bn; Q2 added $1.8bn of awards, including $1.2bn of data-centre power work, making this a
less-loved route into the power bottleneck rather than another semiconductor
([company results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1488139/000162828026051825/amrc_20260630x8-kxexx9911.htm),
[market-value history](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/amrc/market-cap/)).

**What would have to be true:** Awards not yet signed must become contracted revenue, Q2's negative
$107.2m operating cash flow must reverse, and 3.2x corporate leverage must stay safely below the
3.5x covenant; the 41.8 times trailing P/E means backlog alone does not make it cheap
([company cash-flow detail](https://www.ameresco.com/ameresco-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results/),
[Yahoo valuation](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AMRC/)).

### BME.L — London Stock Exchange — B&M European Value Retail

**Why:** Weak real spending favours value retail, but B&M is not being credited with an easy win:
the share is roughly flat over one year, while Q1 France revenue grew 14.6% and like-for-like sales
5.3%; the UK business's 2.3% like-for-like decline keeps expectations grounded
([Q1 statement](https://www.bandmretail.co.uk/~/media/Files/B/Bmstores-Corp/documents/investors/reports-and-presentations/2026/q1-fy27-trading-update.pdf),
[price history](https://markets.ft.markitdigital.com/data/equities/tearsheet/historical?s=BME%3ALSE)).

**What would have to be true:** UK weakness must mainly reflect the garden-season comparison rather
than lost customer relevance, and the FMCG price investment and range reset must restore sales
without permanently sacrificing margin. This is the closest candidate to the 7532.T mistake: the
turnaround story is not evidence, so first-hand store observation and filed cash conversion matter
more than management's recovery language
([FY26 results](https://www.investegate.info/announcement/rns/b-m-european-value-retail-s-a-di---bme/fy26-preliminary-results-/9598419),
[strategy §2.2](../../strategy.md)).

### LRE.L — London Stock Exchange — Lancashire Holdings

**Why:** H1 profit after tax rose to $141.7m from $109.2m and the discounted combined ratio improved
to 80.7% from 87.4%, while the share is approximately flat over one year and trades near 6.3 times
trailing earnings
([company results](https://news.cision.com/lancashire-holdings-limited/r/lancashire-holdings---h1-2026-results%2Cc4378247),
[price](https://markets.investorschronicle.markitdigital.com/data/equities/tearsheet/historical?s=LRE%3ALSE),
[valuation](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/LRE.L/)).

**What would have to be true:** The low multiple must not be capitalising peak insurance pricing;
gross premiums already fell from $1.36bn to $1.32bn, so underwriting discipline must preserve the
combined ratio as rates soften, and catastrophe, marine and energy losses must stay within the
risk budget rather than make the current earnings base unrepeatable
([company results](https://www.lse.co.uk/rns/lancashire-holdings-h1-2026-results-g8xnrlvouf11jy5.html?mobile_view=desktop)).
