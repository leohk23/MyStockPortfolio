Income growth also included a one-time Medicare rebate. **Inference:** strong quarterly growth does not establish strong current consumer volumes, and the inflation figures give no basis for assuming imminent rate relief. For retailers already held or watched, transactions and volumes matter more than nominal sales growth.

The Australian evidence supports continuing physical investment, while the Korean decision shows financing can get dearer alongside it. Neither proves future returns on that investment, nor explains yesterday's individual chip-stock moves.
