## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** US labour demand weakened without yet becoming a broad contraction. September payrolls rose by 29,000, against an average of 45,000 over the prior year; July was revised to a 10,000 loss and July plus August were revised down by 60,000. Unemployment was 4.2%, participation 61.8%, and hourly earnings grew 3.0% over the year. Source: [Bureau of Labor Statistics, September Employment Situation](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm). **Inference.** The relevant fact is not a recession call but less labour income momentum. A candidate depending on volume growth from an unstrained consumer now needs stronger evidence than a resilient sales line.

**Observation.** August personal consumption expenditure rose 0.9% in one month while personal income rose 0.2%; the saving rate was 4.1%. At the same time, [local market data](../../prices.json), updated 3 October, has WTI at $91.11, up 47.69% over one year, and the US ten-year yield at 5.277%. Source: [Bureau of Economic Analysis, August Personal Income and Outlays](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-august-2026). **Inference.** Nominal spending is outrunning income while energy and capital remain expensive. That favours non-discretionary demand, but only where reported growth converts into cash; working-capital funding and leverage matter more than an earnings headline. The BLS and BEA releases are the material evidence beyond the repository's macro block.

## 3. Candidates

### Uniphar — `UPR.IR`, Euronext Dublin — new

**Why watch it.** Uniphar adds European Healthcare exposure to a book with only 1.0% in that sector. Its H1 filing reports 6.9% organic gross-profit growth, with organic growth in all three divisions; operating profit rose 8.6%, Medtech EBITDA rose 11.5%, and rolling ROCE was 14.7%. It is a healthcare distributor and services operator rather than a binary drug-development bet. Source: [Uniphar's H1 2026 interim results](https://uniphar.euwest01.umbraco.io/media/2z2lx5dr/uniphar-plc-june-2026-interim-results-final-pdf.pdf).

**What would have to be true.** The claimed working-capital timing unwind must reverse: H1 operating cash flow was negative €53.0m, free-cash-flow conversion was negative 77.1%, net bank debt rose from €171.1m to €275.7m, and leverage rose from 1.6x to 2.4x. H2 EBITDA acceleration is not enough unless cash conversion normalises and the delayed Irish distribution centre reaches its February 2027 launch without another capital or timing slip. This is the 7532.T check: do not mistake broad healthcare demand and adjusted-EPS growth for shareholder economics while working capital and facilities consume the cash. Source: [the same issuer filing](https://uniphar.euwest01.umbraco.io/media/2z2lx5dr/uniphar-plc-june-2026-interim-results-final-pdf.pdf).

No standing thesis drove it. It offsets the book's US and Technology/Communication Services concentration, although the cash-flow evidence makes it a watch rather than a conclusion. `UPR.IR` was added to [the watchlist](../../watchlist.json); price and valuation are deliberately left to the repository's independent fetch.
