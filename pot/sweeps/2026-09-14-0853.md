## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** Cancer treatment has acquired a new recurring diagnostic step. On 4 September the
FDA approved AstraZeneca's camizestrant with Guardant360 CDx for certain advanced breast-cancer
patients whose ESR1 resistance mutation is detected before radiographic progression. Guardant says
the approved pathway calls for another blood test every three months; in SERENA-6, switching after
that molecular signal reduced the risk of progression or death by **56%** and produced median
progression-free survival of **16.0 months versus 9.2 months**
([FDA](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-new-breast-cancer-treatment),
[Guardant](https://investors.guardanthealth.com/press-releases/press-releases/2026/Guardant-Health-Announces-Landmark-FDA-Approval-of-Guardant360-CDx-as-Companion-Diagnostic-for-AstraZenecas-ETCAMAH-camizestrant-in-Advanced-Breast-Cancer/default.aspx)).

**Inference.** The second-order story is more interesting than one drug: testing can move from a
one-off choice of therapy to repeated surveillance while the patient is still responding. But the
obvious pure play does not pass the valuation filter. Guardant closed at **$157.38** on 11 September,
with a **$21.12bn** market value and a provisional **17.4× trailing sales** multiple while still
loss-making ([Yahoo, provisional](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GH/)). Its Q2 revenue rose **44%**
to **$335.0m**, but net loss was **$120.1m** and free-cash-flow use was **$69.5m**
([Q2 release filed with the SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1576280/000157628026000036/gh-06302026xexhibit991.htm)).
That is the 7532.T mistake in a better industry: a powerful narrative does not make an entry price.
AstraZeneca is the candidate because this approval lands inside an already profitable, diversified
oncology business, not because camizestrant's economics are yet proven.

**Observation.** A quieter earnings release shows a different sort of operating leverage. UNFI's
fiscal 2026 sales fell **2.0%** to **$31.15bn**, while adjusted EBITDA rose **27%** to **$701m**, free
cash flow reached **$323m**, net debt fell **$295m**, and year-end net leverage was **2.2×**. The
company says fill rate, on-time delivery and throughput improved for a fourth consecutive quarter.
For fiscal 2027 it guides to **$31.2bn–$31.8bn** sales and **$275m–$325m** free cash flow
([SEC-filed results](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1020859/000102085926000022/f26q4earningsrelease.htm)).
**Inference.** Cost and network execution have repaired the cash engine before sales have recovered.
That earns observation, not belief: the midpoint of next year's sales range is only 1.1% above 2026,
and reported EPS of **$1.34** was barely half adjusted EPS of **$2.65**. At **$44.45**, the provisional
multiple is **32.9× reported earnings** and **13.1× forward earnings**
([Yahoo, provisional](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/UNFI/)). The decisive next fact is whether the
service gains bring durable customer volume rather than another year of shrinking sales.
