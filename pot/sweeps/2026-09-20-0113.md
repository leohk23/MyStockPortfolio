## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The energy shock is now visible in both prices and behaviour, but it has not yet become a broad consumer collapse. Local WTI is **$96.08**, up **53.38%** over one year ([prices.json](../../prices.json)). Euro-area inflation rose to **3.2%** in August; energy prices were **14.3%** higher and contributed **1.29 percentage points**, while inflation excluding energy and unprocessed food was **2.1%** ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-euro-indicators/w/2-17092026-ap)). In Britain, retail volumes still rose **0.9%** over the three months to August and **2.4%** year on year, but fuel volumes fell as retailers reported that motorists were making fewer journeys, delaying refills or only partly filling tanks in response to higher prices ([ONS](https://www.ons.gov.uk/businessindustryandtrade/retailindustry/bulletins/retailsales/august2026)).

**Observation.** The physical transition has not paused. Renewables supplied **54.1%** of EU electricity generation in Q2; solar supplied **41.6%** of renewable generation, up from **37.0%** a year earlier ([Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20260918-1)). This goes beyond the local macro block, which carries oil and market prices but not electricity-generation mix, retail volumes or the inflation contribution by component.

**Inference.** The cleaner second-order exposure is not another bet on the oil price or a headline renewable developer. More intermittent generation and more expensive energy increase the value of measurement, control, safety and trusted operating data. The constraint is capital: the local US 10-year yield is **4.998%** ([prices.json](../../prices.json)), so a long-duration project story without cash conversion is less attractive than a business already earning on the installed system. That led to Halma; a separate pass through Leo's T1 found LSEG as a model-neutral owner and distributor of licensed data rather than another frontier-model vendor.
