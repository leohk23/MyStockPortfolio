## 3. Candidates

### THEON.AS — Theon International — Euronext Amsterdam

**Why it deserves work:** H1 order intake rose 38.5%, revenue rose 35.4% including 24.7% organic
growth, and adjusted EBIT margin was 26.2%. The €1.46bn soft backlog is 2.4 times the top of 2026
revenue guidance, giving this European night-vision and optronics supplier a company-specific case
beyond the broad defence-spending theme. Source: [THEON H1 trading
update](https://theon.com/en/news/2026/theon-announces-q2-2026-h1-2026-trading-update).

**What would have to be true:** Soft backlog and options must convert into firm orders, revenue and
statutory cash; the unusually high margin must be supported by durable product advantage and asset
turnover; and acquisition-funded expansion must preserve returns while pro-forma leverage falls
from the stated 3.0×. The full H1 report due after today's close must reconcile the trading-update
adjustments. Otherwise this is the 7532.T mistake again: extrapolating a fashionable demand story
without proving the forward economics or valuation.

This is a candidate, not an order. It has been added to the watchlist so the normal fetch can test
its price, earnings and valuation history independently.
