### VYLR — NYSE — Vylor (new)

- **Why:** Corteva's former seed segment (Pioneer) has listed on its own. In the same Brazil quarter that broke its crop-protection sister, it held price/mix **+6%** against volume −9%. Its Q3 net sales were **$938m, −1%**, and year-to-date sales are **$8.53bn, +4%**. It reaffirmed FY2026 adjusted pro forma operating EBITDA of about **$2.8bn** and operating EPS of **$2.25–2.35** ([Q3 release, 11 Nov](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002128626/000212862626000009/a3q_2026xearningsxnewsxr.htm)). It began trading on **1 October 2026** ([Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002128626/000119312526402928/ck0002128626-ex99_1.htm)).
- **What would have to be true:** trait and germplasm pricing has to hold up while farmer credit is tight. And the new licensing arm, "Vylor One", guided to about **$500m** of *gross* licensing income in 2027 (same release), has to show up as net profit and operating cash in the first standalone 10-K, not just as a target.

**Exposure:** offsets the sector concentration with agricultural inputs (Yahoo will likely file it under Basic Materials), but it adds to the US geography. It does **not** reduce the 64%: it beats the diversifying alternatives on business quality, not on geography. No thesis drove it.

**Valuation, provisional:** Yahoo puts VYLR at **$72.06** on 12 November ([chart endpoint](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/VYLR?range=3mo&interval=1wk), provisional until the pipeline fetches it). Its first weekly close after listing was $67.26. On the guided EPS midpoint that is about **31x**, my arithmetic on an *adjusted pro forma* basis. That is a forward, company-adjusted figure, not a filed trailing one. Nothing here is cheap by §6.1, and §6.1 cannot test it yet anyway: six weeks of trading is not a five-year history. It will sit on the watchlist a long time before the Scan can say anything. That is the cost of watching a spin-off.

**7532.T test:** the trap here is the opposite of tourism, the "defensive franchise" story. Volume fell 9% in the quarter and price did the work. §2.4b applies if full-year volume is flat, and the Deep dive would have to say when volume growth resumes. The release also gives no net debt figure, and a spin-off's opening balance sheet is where a parent leaves its liabilities. The Form 10 has to be read before anyone calls this a quality compounder.
