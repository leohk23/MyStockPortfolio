## 3. Candidates

### BNZL.L — Bunzl — London Stock Exchange

**Why it deserves work:** Bunzl is an unglamorous distributor of consumables into grocery,
foodservice, cleaning, safety and healthcare. First-half underlying revenue grew 3.2%, adjusted
operating margin rose from 7.0% to 7.3%, 78% of orders were processed digitally and own-brand
products supplied about 30% of revenue. The release itself supplies the caution: much of the
margin benefit from inflation is expected to be temporary, and management still prioritises
bolt-on acquisitions while launching a £500m buyback. The provisional Yahoo close was 2,658p on
4 September, up 28.0% in 2026 but only 4.1% over one year, so the operational recovery is partly
priced without having erased the longer disappointment. Sources: [Bunzl half-year
report](https://www.bunzl.com/newsroom/half-year-report-2026/), [provisional Yahoo
quote](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/BNZL.L/) and [local book](../../signals.json).

**What would have to be true:** North America volume growth must persist after the inflation
benefit fades; digital ordering and own-brand penetration must produce durable customer retention,
cash conversion and returns on capital; and acquisitions plus adjusted measures must not hide weak
asset productivity. The repo's independent earnings and multiple history must also pass Leo's
valuation filter. Otherwise this is the 7532.T mistake again: extrapolating a plausible recovery
without proving the forward economics and price.

This is a candidate, not an order. It has been added to the watchlist so the normal fetch can test
its price, earnings and valuation history independently.
