the Treasury cancelled its fixed-rate and inflation-linked bond auctions for 27 and 29 October,

[Suno report](https://www.suno.com.br/noticias/circuit-breaker-ibovespa-b3-reeleicao-lula-26102026-go/)

**Observation: elsewhere.** Locally, the S&P 500 rose 0.70% and the Nasdaq 0.82% on the day. WTI is
at $101.36, still up 76.8% this year, and the US 10-year yield is at 4.986%
([prices.json](../../prices.json) `macro`). The press tied the US move to Anthropic filing for an
IPO and to chipmakers ahead of this week's results and the Fed
([Bloomberg wrap](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-25/us-equity-futures-climb-as-crude-oil-price-dips-markets-wrap)).
Fair Isaac fell 13.2% to $708.07 (Yahoo, provisional), having been $1,659.53 a year ago. The cause
is regulatory: from 1 October Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac price loans scored with VantageScore 4.0
on the same grid as Classic FICO, after opening VantageScore to every approved lender on 9 September
([Fannie Mae Lender Letter LL-2026-06, reissued 30 Sep](https://singlefamily.fanniemae.com/media/48071/display)).
I worked from the letter's search summary because the PDF itself refused the fetch.

**Inference.** In Brazil, the obvious trade was priced within the same session. The exporters a
weaker real helps, Embraer and WEG, *rose*. The domestic franchises took the whole fall. That is a
reason to look at the domestic side rather than at the exporters, and it is also a warning: a
domestic Brazilian bank earns in reais, and a falling currency is exactly the risk Leo names in
his 7532.T regret. On FICO, a monopoly losing its pricing power to a regulator is a broken moat,
not a de-rating. It would also add to the crowded US Technology line. It is not a candidate.

**Within what Leo owns.** VantageScore is owned jointly by the three credit bureaus, and Experian
is held. It is the obvious beneficiary of FICO losing its mortgage monopoly. Even so, EXPN.L closed
down 6.7% at 2,900p today, after +15.2% over the month, and its P/E sits at the 6th percentile of
its five-year range ([prices.json](../../prices.json)). I found no company announcement that
explains Monday's fall, so I am not offering one. Its interim results are on 18 November; the
figure to look for is how much of US mortgage scoring revenue the half-year attributes to its own
scores, if it discloses that at all. META also fell 1.9% after announcing it would raise money for
AI infrastructure.

**Disclosure.** This lane is running on an Anthropic model, so I am not commenting on Anthropic's
IPO beyond the fact that it was filed. For T1, the useful fact is that a frontier-model seller's
registration statement, once public, is a filed source for the margin T1 says will compress. The
Deep dive should read it as a source, not as an opinion of mine.

## 3. Candidates

### ITUB — NYSE ADR (home listing ITUB4.SA, B3) — Itaú Unibanco — new

- **Why:** Brazil's highest-return private bank fell 20.4% in one session on a political shock,
  while the exporters rose. It reported a recurring managerial result of BRL 12.4bn for 2Q26, a
  recurring ROE of 24.3% and CET1 of 12.3%
  ([2Q26 release, SEC 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001132597/000113259726000215/eng_pressreleasex2t26.htm)).
  It offsets geography: 0% Latin America against 65.6% US. It adds to Financial Services (8.1%)
  rather than to the tech-and-communications half of the book. No thesis drove it.
- **What would have to be true:** ROE has to stay above about 20% through a weaker real and
  whatever rate response follows, and the 3Q26 and 4Q26 releases have to show credit costs holding.
  Then a sterling holder's return still has to survive the currency. That last point is the 7532.T
  question asked directly: any proposal has to price the real, not just the bank. The same release
  already cut 2026 guidance for fees and insurance to +2% to +5% on market volatility, which was
  *before* this shock.
- **Why not today:** it is day one and still loud. The brief prefers a story that has gone quiet.
  Adding it now lets the pipeline carry its price, filed earnings and history, so a Deep dive can
  look once it settles. The 7.70 price is provisional until then.

No other candidate. The exporters already priced their gain in the same session. Another exchange
would duplicate CME, Tradeweb and three European exchanges already watched or held. FICO is
argued against above.
