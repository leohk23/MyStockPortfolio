## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The energy shock is now a refined-product and inventory problem, not merely a high crude-price headline. The IEA says global observed oil inventories have fallen by 507 million barrels since February, August refining margins reached records in the Atlantic Basin, and Gulf plus Russian diesel exports were 1.6 million barrels a day below February. It also forecasts 2026 oil demand down 2.5 million barrels a day, showing that demand destruction is already part of the balancing mechanism. **Inference.** The apparent beneficiaries are easy to name, but the more durable question is who keeps the economics when routes normalise and emergency buffers stop falling. Peak refinery and tanker earnings are not automatically cheap earnings. Source: [IEA September Oil Market Report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-september-2026).

**Observation.** In [local market data](../../prices.json), WTI is $94.27, up 51.15% over one year, while the US ten-year yield is 5.24%. The S&P 500 is down only 1.04% over seven days and remains up 15.35% over one year. **Inference.** Markets are pricing an expensive-input, expensive-capital world without broad panic. That makes externally financed growth and theme exposure less attractive than contracted revenue, asset availability and per-share cash conversion. The higher bar is especially relevant because the existing book already has abundant AI and US growth exposure.

## 3. Candidates

### Gresham House Energy Storage Fund — `GRID.L`, London — new

**Why watch it.** This is physical UK power infrastructure and a rare addition to the book's 1.17% Utilities exposure, not another US technology supplier. The fund had 1,072MW operating at June, lifted contracted revenue to 61% from 35%, and had financed and started construction on the first 397MW of a larger pipeline. Source: [GRID H1 2026 RNS](https://www.investegate.co.uk/announcement/rns/gresham-house-energy-storage-fund--grid/interim-results-for-period-ended-30-june-2026/9785600).

**What would have to be true.** Contracting and asset availability must turn construction into per-share cash rather than just appraised value. Annualised revenue per operational MW fell to £63,200 from £75,100, the NAV increase was driven mainly by moving construction projects from cost to discounted-cash-flow valuations, and net debt was £171.4m. The watch case needs operating revenue per MW to stabilise, the 2027–28 projects to commission on budget, and financing to avoid material dilution or an uneconomic rise in leverage. This is the check against repeating the 7532.T mistake: a real theme does not establish shareholder returns.

`GRID.L` was added to [the watchlist](../../watchlist.json) so the normal pipeline can establish its quote and fund record independently.

Its Q2 release also contains large vessel-sale gains, convertible-note issuance and rapid fleet changes. The shortage is real; chasing peak-cycle earnings here would be the 7532.T error in a different industry. Sources: [Scorpio Tankers Q2 results](https://www.scorpiotankers.com/scorpio-tankers-inc-announces-financial-results-for-the-second-quarter-of-2026-and-the-declaration-of-a-dividend/), [local valuation record](../../prices.json).
