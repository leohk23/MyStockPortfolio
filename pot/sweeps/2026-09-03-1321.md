become a broad European factory-price spiral, while US manufacturers are producing more without a
matching labour-cost increase. That is evidence for selective efficiency investment, not for
buying every company that calls itself an automation or energy-transition beneficiary.

**Observation beyond the local macro block.** July US capital-goods imports rose $14.4bn, including
$6.9bn of computers and $6.6bn of computer accessories

**Inference.** The AI build-out is visible in physical trade, but the supplier trade is already
loud. A new name needs operating leverage and cash evidence, plus an explicit test against customer
concentration and an independently fetched valuation.

### IMI.L — IMI — London Stock Exchange

**Why:** IMI is a less obvious way to test the energy-efficiency and physical-productivity thesis:
first-half organic revenue rose 5%, organic adjusted operating profit rose 8%, adjusted operating
cash flow rose 32% to £208m, and process-automation orders rose 12% organically, including strength
in nuclear, power and LNG
([company half-year results](https://www.imiplc.com/en/news-insights/half-year-results-2026/)).

**What would have to be true:** its small-but-critical fluid and motion controls must keep earning
pricing power and aftermarket revenue across cycles, the current nuclear, data-centre and energy
orders must convert without acquisition-led dilution, returns on capital must support the margin,
and the independently fetched valuation must pass Leo's filter; one strong cash half and management's
megatrend framing are not enough.

### CIEN — Ciena — New York Stock Exchange

**Why:** today's result converts part of the crowded AI-network story into reported operating
evidence: fiscal-third-quarter revenue rose 37% to $1.67bn, GAAP gross margin expanded from 41.3% to
45.4%, and GAAP operating margin rose from 6.1% to 18.0%
([company release](https://investor.ciena.com/news/news-details/2026/Ciena-Reports-Fiscal-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)).

**What would have to be true:** growth must broaden beyond the two customers that supplied 41.7% of
