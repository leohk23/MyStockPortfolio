**Observation.** The IEA's new ASEAN review says Southeast Asian energy demand per person has risen almost 30% in a decade, regional crude production is 40% below its 1990s peak, and electricity demand is projected to grow about 5.4% a year from 2026 to 2030. Sources: [IEA ASEAN Energy Security Review](https://www.iea.org/reports/asean-energy-security-review), [ASEAN Centre for Energy and IEA storage paper](https://aseanenergy.org/publications/accelerating-battery-energy-storage-system-for-vre-integration). **Inference.** This is a real physical-investment need, but not a new stock idea by itself.
