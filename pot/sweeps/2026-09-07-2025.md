## 3. Candidates

### ENX.PA — Euronext — Euronext Paris

**Why it deserves work:** Q2 underlying revenue and income rose 16.9%, or 9.9% like for like at
constant currencies; non-volume revenue was 58% of the total and covered 170% of underlying
operating costs excluding depreciation and amortisation. Adjusted EBITDA margin reached 66.1%,
while the mix spans trading, clearing, custody, settlement and data rather than relying on one
volatile product. Sources: [Euronext Q2 results](https://www.euronext.com/en/media/15989/download)
and [August volume-release record](https://www.euronext.com/en/investor-relations/financial-information/news/euronext-announces-volumes-for-august-2026).

**What would have to be true:** Like-for-like growth and recurring-revenue coverage must persist
without unusually high volatility; Admincontrol, Athens and the securities-depository expansion
must earn acceptable returns rather than merely enlarge adjusted revenue; and the 57.3% operating
cash-flow conversion from Q2 must either improve or reconcile to clearing working capital. The high
margin also needs a durable network-effect explanation under rules designed to increase competition,
and the repo's independent multiple history must pass Leo's valuation filter. Otherwise this is the
7532.T mistake again: mistaking a favourable policy story and acquired growth for durable forward
economics at the price paid.

This is a candidate, not an order. It has been added to the watchlist so the normal fetch can test
its price, earnings and valuation history independently.
