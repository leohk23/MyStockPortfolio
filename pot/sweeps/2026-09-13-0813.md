## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** Producer inflation is now visible across regions, not only in the oil ticker. US
final-demand PPI rose **0.4% in August and 5.4% over a year**; goods rose 1.1% in the month while
services rose 0.1% ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/ppi.nr0.htm)). China's industrial PPI
rose **3.8% year on year**, while producers' purchase prices rose 5.8%
([NBS](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202609/t20260910_1965274.html)); manufacturing
PMI improved but remained fractionally contractionary at **49.8**
([NBS](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202609/t20260901_1965170.html)). In Europe,
August inflation reached **3.3%**, including 14.3% energy inflation, and the ECB raised all three
policy rates by 25 basis points on 10 September
([ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/press_conference/monetary-policy-statement/2026/html/ecb.is260910~6a45359cfc.en.html)).
The local block agrees with the direction: WTI is **$100.05, +74.55% year to date**, and the US
5-year yield is **4.791%, +28.14% year to date** ([prices.json](../../prices.json)).

**Observation.** Physical investment and trade are still moving despite the price shock. The US
goods-and-services deficit widened from **$71.2bn to $88.6bn in July** as exports fell 2.1% and
imports rose 2.8%; the import increase was led by capital goods, including computers, accessories
and semiconductors
([BEA](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/us-international-trade-goods-and-services-july-2026)). The
ECB also reports AI-related activity in digital services, business investment and exports, while
noting that AI financing has spread from equity into bonds and private credit. That is observation,
not proof that another chip supplier is attractive. It makes the financing and cash return on the
spending more important.

**Inference.** The less crowded questions are one step downstream. First, can a logistics network
turn changing trade routes into profit without buying empty volume? Second, can a data owner charge
for better decisions and trust when agents compress software seats and alter distribution? The book
already owns ample US technology exposure, so the candidates below are an Asian physical network
and an established data business, not another AI-infrastructure supplier. Higher input costs and
yields make cash conversion and returns on capital the admission test for both.
