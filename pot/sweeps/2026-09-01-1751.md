Meanwhile, only 14 of 43 country markets are within 5% of their all-time high and the median market
needs a 14% rise to recover it, so this is not indiscriminate global euphoria
([local signals](../../signals.json)). **Inference.** Activity is holding up, but supply and price
pressure are selective. That favours a distributor with inventory, customer reach and pricing
power, while the uneven country tape leaves room to watch a strong domestic franchise in a weak
market. Neither works without cash conversion and balance-sheet evidence.

## 3. Candidates

### WCC — WESCO International, NYSE

**Why:** This is a second-order way to investigate the component and electrical-equipment squeeze:
Q2 sales grew 13%, organic sales 12.6%, data-centre sales 45%, and backlog roughly 60%, with
adjusted EBITDA margin rising to 7.3% from 6.7%
([WESCO Q2 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/929008/000092900826000021/wcc-2q2026earnings.htm)).

**What must be true:** Backlog must convert without working capital swallowing the earnings, demand
must remain broader than hyperscale data centres, leverage must keep falling, and the independently
fetched valuation must pass the strategy's filter.

### BBCA.JK — Bank Central Asia, Indonesia Stock Exchange

**Why:** Indonesia is among the weaker country markets in the local breadth data, but BCA reported
8% loan growth, a 10.2% rise in low-cost CASA balances, an 84.3% CASA mix, a 1.9% NPL ratio and a
4.9% loan-at-risk ratio. It is a quality-franchise candidate inside a weak market, not a call to
buy the country index ([local market data](../../prices.json),
[BCA half-year results](https://www.bca.co.id/en/tentang-bca/media-riset/pressroom/siaran-pers/2026/07/29/07/10/pertama-kali-kredit-tembus-rp1000-triliun-bca-komit-dukung-perekonomian-nasional)).

**What must be true:** The CASA funding advantage and asset quality must survive oil, currency and
rate pressure; loan growth must translate into earnings after margin pressure; and the local data
pipeline must confirm a valuation that compensates for those risks.

Both names were added to the watchlist. This is candidate discovery only; no order is implied.
