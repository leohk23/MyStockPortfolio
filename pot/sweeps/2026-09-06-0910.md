## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** There has been no new trading session since Friday. The repo still has WTI at
$91.48, up 6.7% in seven days and 59.6% in 2026, while the US 10-year yield is 4.784%. The latest
IMF market monitor describes the same collision: energy stayed elevated during renewed US-Iran
strikes and Friday's stronger payroll release pushed Treasury yields higher. **Inference.** This is
not a fresh Sunday signal. It is still a demanding test of business quality: revenue growth that
requires cheap fuel, cheap finance or ever more inventory deserves less weight than orders that
turn into cash. Sources: [local market snapshot](../../prices.json) and [IMF Global Markets Monitor,
4 September](https://www.imfconnect.org/content/imf/en/gmm.html).

**Observation beyond the local macro block.** WTO data show first-quarter merchandise-trade volume
up 3.2% year on year, but the strength was narrow: the value of AI-enabling trade rose more than
40%, while Middle East imports and exports were already down 11.9% and 9.7%. Separately, WTO-IMF
trade-policy activity averaged almost twice its 2024 level in January-May 2026 and about one-quarter
above the 2025 average. **Inference.** Headline trade resilience hides both concentration and more
friction. The under-covered second-order opportunity is not another chip maker or a bet on one
shipping route; it is the independent testing, inspection and certification needed when suppliers,
routes and rules change. Sources: [WTO first-quarter trade
release](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/rese_31jul26_469_e.htm) and [WTO-IMF trade-policy
activity update](https://www.wto.org/english/news_e/news26_e/rese_23jul26_463_e.htm).

**Observation from the book.** The main book is 66.27% US, and Google, Microsoft, Apple and Nvidia
together are 35.51%; `holdings.json` has no sector field, so the technology concentration comes
from the names rather than `byGeography`. **Inference.** An ex-US compliance-services candidate
earns a meaningful diversification preference over another American software name, provided its
capital returns and valuation survive the independent fetch. Source: [local book](../../signals.json).

## 3. Candidates

### BVI.PA — Bureau Veritas — Euronext Paris

**Why it deserves work:** Bureau Veritas is a tollbooth on complexity rather than a bet on which
trade route wins. In the first half, organic revenue grew 5.0%, accelerating to 5.5% in Q2;
adjusted operating margin rose 29 basis points at constant currency and organic free cash flow rose
3.2%. That is operating evidence behind the WTO lead, not proof of a moat. The shares closed at a
provisional €27.20 on Yahoo, down 5.0% over one month but up 4.3% over one year, so the price move
is enough to watch, not evidence that it is cheap. The candidate adds formal European exposure and
avoids the book's much larger US megacap-technology concentration. Sources: [Bureau Veritas H1
release](https://group.bureauveritas.com/newsroom/delivering-our-commitments-higher-sequential-organic-growth-q2),
[provisional Yahoo quote](https://fr.finance.yahoo.com/quote/BVI.PA/) and [local book](../../signals.json).

**What would have to be true:** Organic growth must remain broad after the oil, petrochemical, coal
and government-services exits; recurring inspection and certification demand must support cash
returns without acquisitions or adjusted measures hiding weak asset productivity; and the repo's
independent earnings and multiple history must pass Leo's valuation filter. Otherwise this is the
7532.T mistake in a smarter costume: noticing a real world trend without proving fundamentals,
forward economics and price.

This is a candidate, not an order. It has been added to the watchlist so the normal fetch can test
its price, earnings and valuation history independently.
