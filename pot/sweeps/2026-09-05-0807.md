thesis on its own. Sources: [European Commission rail-financing

## 3. Candidates

### KBX.DE — Knorr-Bremse — Xetra

**Why it deserves work:** Knorr-Bremse sells safety-critical braking and related systems into rail
and commercial vehicles, a less direct and more globally diversified way to investigate transport
efficiency than adding another railway operator. In the first half, revenue rose 3.1%, operating
EBIT rose 13.7% and free cash flow rose 82.9% to €294m; the €7.887bn order book was a record. The
counter-evidence matters: total order intake fell 1.2%, rail order intake fell 7.7%, and management
is selling the train-HVAC business while presenting new 2030 targets. This candidate adds formal
European geography against a book that is 66.3% US. The repo has no sector field, but its largest
names show that the more relevant concentration is US megacap technology, which this also avoids.
Sources: [Knorr-Bremse first-half
release](https://www.ir.knorr-bremse.com/nachrichten/knorr-bremse-liefert-starke-ergebnisse-im-zweiten-quartal-und-startet-wachstumsoffensive-growth-beyond/a5984b77-0570-4e9d-9667-f54d87f91c00)
and [local book](../../signals.json).

**What would have to be true:** Rail intake must stabilise, the backlog must convert without margin
or working-capital reversal, and cash conversion and ROCE must improve after separating operational
progress from restructuring, acquisitions and the HVAC disposal. Most importantly, the independent
repo fetch must show that the current multiple passes Leo's valuation filter. Until then the price
is an unknown, not a reason to turn a candidate into an order.

This is a candidate, not an order. It has been added to the watchlist so the next fetch can supply
its price, earnings and valuation history through the normal pipeline.
