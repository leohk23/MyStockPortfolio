## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The late-session local data still show the same oil-and-rates squeeze, but at a
slightly higher oil level than the prior sweep: WTI is $90.46, up 8.3% in seven days, while the US
ten-year yield is 4.796% ([local market data](../../prices.json)). The immediate catalyst is renewed
US-Iran fighting rather than evidence of stronger end demand
([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/61f03262bb5dfa1c414c4fb7be40772e)). **Inference.**
This is a supply-risk and discount-rate shock together. It raises the bar for long-duration shares
and for oil consumers, but it does not by itself make an oil producer or contractor a good idea.

**Observation beyond the local macro block.** China's official August manufacturing PMI improved
to 49.8 and new orders crossed into expansion at 50.6, but the split remained uneven: large firms
were at 50.6 while small firms were at 47.9
([National Bureau of Statistics of China](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202609/t20260901_1965170.html)).
**Inference.** Chinese industrial demand may be stabilising, but it is not yet broad. A company
exposed to it needs its own order, cash-conversion and return-on-capital evidence; country-level
recovery is not enough and would risk repeating the 7532.T mistake.

### ANDR.VI — ANDRITZ — Vienna Stock Exchange

**Why:** ANDRITZ is a less obvious supplier to several real-capex cycles rather than one headline
trade: first-half order intake rose 25.2% and backlog reached a record €12.60bn, up 20.5% from
year-end, with growth led by hydropower and supported by pulp-and-paper and metals demand
([company half-year release](https://www.andritz.com/newsroom-en/news/2026-07-30-results-h1-2026-group)).

**What would have to be true:** the backlog must convert into cash at acceptable returns without
fixed-price project losses, service and diversified end markets must soften capital-project
cyclicality, and the independent valuation must pass Leo's filter; first-half growth was Q1-heavy
and Q2 hydropower orders fell 26.7% year on year, so the record headline cannot be extrapolated
blindly ([company half-year release](https://www.andritz.com/newsroom-en/news/2026-07-30-results-h1-2026-group)).
