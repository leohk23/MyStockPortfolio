# Sweep — 31 August 2026

## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** India has supplied the clearest new macro fact since this morning's sweep: real
GDP grew 7.8% in the April–June quarter and nominal GDP grew 10.3%
([Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2304949&lang=1&reg=48)).
That growth is arriving into a harsher external price environment. Locally, WTI is $86.17, up
50.3% year to date, and the US ten-year yield is 4.72%, up 12.7% year to date
([prices.json](../../prices.json)). **Inference.** Strong aggregate growth does not remove the
7532.T risk: with fuel, funding and execution costs elevated, the useful exposure is a business
whose signed work converts to cash and margin, not an index-level growth story.

**Observation.** High rates and heavy issuance are also creating activity for the plumbing around
capital markets. Tradeweb's second-quarter average daily volume rose 18.2% year on year to $3.0
trillion, while revenue rose 9.0% and international revenue rose 13.9%
([Q2 results](https://investors.tradeweb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tradeweb-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results));
July volume then rose 23.3% year on year
([July volumes](https://investors.tradeweb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tradeweb-reports-july-2026-total-trading-volume-675-trillion-and/)).
**Inference.** The quieter second-order idea is not to forecast the next rate move, but to test
whether electronification and workflow share keep compounding after volatility normalises.

## 3. Candidates

### LT.NS — Larsen & Toubro, NSE

**Why:** India's growth is translating into signed engineering work: L&T's June order book was
₹7.79 trillion, up 27% year on year, with slow-moving orders below 1%
([Q1 transcript](https://investors.larsentoubro.com/upload/Analysttrans/FY2027AnalysttransL%26T%20Q1%20FY27%20Earnings%20Call%20Transcript.pdf)).

**What would have to be true:** the book must convert into cash and acceptable project margins;
that is the real test because 52% is international and 71% of the international book is in the
Middle East. The independently fetched valuation must also clear Leo's filter.

### MWA — Mueller Water Products, NYSE

**Why:** replacement and measurement of municipal water networks is a quiet infrastructure need;
third-quarter sales rose 4.1% and adjusted operating margin reached 24.0% from 19.6% even though
overall volumes were slightly lower
([Q3 filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1350593/000135059326000034/exhibit991pressreleasefy26.htm)).

**What would have to be true:** the margin gain must survive without tariff refunds and lower
incentive compensation, Water Flow volumes must recover, and foundry investment must turn into
durable free cash flow rather than merely sustaining old capacity.

### TW — Tradeweb Markets, Nasdaq

**Why:** it is a tollbooth on the growing electronic fixed-income workflow rather than a bet on
bond direction; second-quarter volume grew 18.2%, revenue 9.0%, and the adjusted EBITDA margin
held at 54.4%
([Q2 results](https://investors.tradeweb.com/news-releases/news-release-details/tradeweb-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results)).

**What would have to be true:** volume growth must reflect durable client adoption and market
share rather than one volatile rate cycle, revenue must keep following volume despite the slower
growth seen this quarter, and the local valuation history must clear the filter.

No order, price target or position size is implied. `LT.NS`, `MWA` and `TW` were added to the
watchlist so the normal pipeline can supply price, earnings and valuation history.
