**Observation.** China's August production data are much stronger than its broad demand measures. Industrial value added rose 5.2% year on year, equipment manufacturing 12.1% and high-tech manufacturing 16.7%; industrial-robot output rose 34.6%. Automation suppliers deserve a look, but only where company orders, service revenue and cash returns show that the volume is durable rather than subsidy, pre-buying or a short equipment cycle.
