## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The Federal Reserve is now describing delegated agent payments as an infrastructure problem rather than only an AI use case. Governor Waller says the largest barriers are authentication, liability and fraud: the system must prove that an agent was authorised, record what the buyer intended and recalibrate fraud controls that were built around human behaviour. He also says machine-speed micropayments favour rails with lower flat fees, while the market has not settled whether standards will be platform-specific or interoperable. Source: [Federal Reserve, Payments in the Age of AI Agents](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/waller20260928a.htm). **Inference.** This is direct evidence for T3's control-layer need arriving through payments before regulation makes it compulsory. It is not evidence that any identity or security vendor is winning: the investable fact to seek is disclosed agent-payment volume, authentication revenue or fraud performance, not a product announcement. Given the book's 60.60% Technology and Communication Services exposure, I did not turn the theme alone into another US software candidate.

**Observation.** Bank of England MPC member Catherine Mann says the September forecast has UK inflation rising above 4% in Q1 2027. Her financial-conditions decomposition says nominal conditions tightened after the Middle East conflict, but much of the move reflects inflation and policy-uncertainty premia; real conditions initially loosened. She has voted for a 25-basis-point Bank Rate increase, which is her view rather than a collective MPC decision. Source: [Bank of England, Financial conditions: what's priced in?](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/speech/2026/october/catherine-mann-nomura-investor-conference). [Local market data](../../prices.json), updated 6 October, has WTI at $89.82, up 45.50% over one year, and the US ten-year yield at 5.311%. **Inference.** A higher nominal yield is not automatically equivalent to weaker real demand when inflation compensation is doing part of the work. The candidate bar remains growth in real volumes and cash returns after working capital and reinvestment, not a multiple that looks cheaper because discount rates rose.

## 3. Candidates

### Terumo — `4543.T`, Tokyo Stock Exchange — new

**Why watch it.** Terumo adds Healthcare exposure to a book with only 1.0% in that sector through recurring cardiovascular, hospital-care, blood and cell-technology products rather than a single drug outcome. Q1 revenue grew 9.4% excluding currency, with constant-currency growth across Cardiac and Vascular of 7.3%, Medical Care Solutions of 8.5%, and Blood and Cell Technologies of 6.1%. Operating cash flow rose to JPY74.5bn from JPY32.6bn, against JPY12.6bn of PP&E purchases and JPY6.2bn of intangible purchases. Source: [Terumo's 7 August Q1 filing](https://www.terumo.com/system/files/document/2026-08/FinancialResults_27Q1_E.pdf).

**What would have to be true.** Broad constant-currency growth and cash after reinvestment must persist, and capital returns must improve without acquisition spending becoming the hidden denominator. Q1 gross profit included a US tariff refund, operating profit included settlement proceeds, and management raised full-year profit guidance for those two items; they are not evidence of durable operating acceleration. The newly announced Arsenal Medical acquisition is for a clinical-stage product, with the purchase price and financial impact undisclosed. This is the 7532.T check: do not buy the 19.9% reported-revenue and 60.1% operating-profit headlines as a Japanese rebound when the filing itself says underlying revenue growth was 9.4% and identifies the one-offs. Sources: [the Q1 filing](https://www.terumo.com/system/files/document/2026-08/FinancialResults_27Q1_E.pdf), [Terumo's Arsenal Medical acquisition presentation](https://www.terumo.com/system/files/document/2026-09/Presentation_260930_AcquisitionofArsenalMedical_E.pdf).

No standing thesis drove it. It offsets the book's US and Technology/Communication Services concentrations and adds Healthcare, although it increases an existing 8.28% Japan exposure. `4543.T` was added to [the watchlist](../../watchlist.json); price and comparable valuation are deliberately left to the repository's independent fetch.
