## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The US consumer release now completes the producer-price picture from the earlier
sweep. August CPI rose **0.4% in the month and 3.4% over a year**. Energy rose **2.1% in the month
and 16.3% over a year**, while inflation excluding food and energy was 0.3% in the month and eased
to 2.4% over a year ([BLS CPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.nr0.htm)). Real average hourly
earnings fell **0.1% in August and 0.3% over a year**; longer hours left real weekly earnings 0.3%
higher over the year ([BLS real earnings](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/realer.nr0.htm)). These
are reported facts: the energy shock has reached household prices, but it has not yet broadened
into accelerating annual core inflation.

**Observation.** The IEA's September report puts scale on the physical squeeze. It estimates that
August global oil production fell **1.6mb/d month on month**, observed inventories fell **95m
barrels**, and cumulative draws since February reached **507m barrels**. Atlantic Basin refinery
margins reached records as diesel tightened, even while the IEA cut 2026 demand to a **2.5mb/d
decline** because high prices are destroying consumption
([IEA](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-september-2026)). The local market block agrees
with the direction rather than supplying those balances: WTI is **$100.05, +74.55% year to date**,
and the US 5-year yield is **4.791%, +28.14% year to date** ([prices.json](../../prices.json)).

**Inference.** This is a margin-and-cash-conversion regime, not a licence to capitalise spot
profits forever. Refiners, tankers, storage, fertiliser, discount retail and fuel-efficiency
suppliers are the obvious second-order screens. They are also already represented in the tracked
universe by STNG, VPK.AS, CF, BME.L, ROST, SPM.MI and several industrial-efficiency names. Adding
another integrated oil company because current refining margins are records would be the
**7532.T mistake** in commodity form: noticing the world correctly without proving mid-cycle
returns or the valuation filter. T1–T3 do not drive this conclusion.
