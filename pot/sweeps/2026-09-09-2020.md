### YUMC — Yum China — New York Stock Exchange — new

**Why:** KFC/Pizza Hut's China operator is a more specific lead than consumer recovery: Q2
same-store transactions rose **5%**, same-store sales **1%**, and restaurant margin held at
**16.1%** despite greater delivery costs. Source:
[30 July results](https://ir.yumchina.com/news-releases/news-release-details/yum-china-reports-second-quarter-2026-results).

**What would have to be true:** Traffic must remain profitable as delivery-subsidy comparisons
toughen, and the **$1.2bn** Pizza Hut China brand purchase completed **7 August** must earn a
return after financing and the capital committed. The next quarterly results should separate
operating improvement from purchased royalty savings. Sources: same results,
[completion 8-K, pages 2 and 77](https://ir.yumchina.com/static-files/b860fd22-dbf0-42d4-adb1-50183734b945).

The 7532.T mistake would be calling EPS accretion evidence of improved capital returns.
The acquisition uses bridge financing; refinancing belongs in the valuation. Geography is
**China**, despite the US listing. Neither T1 nor T2 is required for this candidate.

### METSO.HE — Metso — Nasdaq Helsinki — new

**Why:** It sells mineral-processing equipment and the parts and service that keep it running;
Q2 aftermarket sales were **57% of sales**, while reported pre-tax ROCE reached **17.3%**.
This tests ongoing mine productivity rather than requiring a correct call on the next metal price.
Source: [24 July half-year results](https://www.metso.com/corporate/media/news/2026/7/metsos-half-year-report-january-1--june-30-2026/).

**What would have to be true:** Cash generation must support the earnings improvement without
continually expanding working capital: H1 operating cash flow **before financial items and
taxes** fell to **€285m from €343m**, despite higher adjusted EBITA. Check conversion and
aftermarket demand at the **22 October** results. Sources: same results,
[company reporting calendar](https://www.metso.com/corporate/media/news/2025/10/metsos-financial-reporting-and-annual-general-meeting-in-2026/).

The 7532.T mistake would be extrapolating equipment orders through a commodity cycle or treating
management's adjusted EBITA as distributable cash. ROCE above is not our after-tax ROIC.
This overlaps the industrial theme already watched in Epiroc and Konecranes; neither T1 nor T2 drives it.

### ALFA.ST — Alfa Laval — Nasdaq Stockholm — new

**Why:** Heat exchangers, separation and marine equipment address operating efficiency;
Q2 organic orders grew **29%** and operating cash flow reached **SEK2.413bn**, versus
**SEK2.159bn**. Source: [21 July company release](https://news.cision.com/alfa-laval/r/alfa-laval-ab--publ--interim-report-1-april---30-june-2026,c4376242).

**What would have to be true:** Capacity investment must convert orders into returns:
reported ROCE fell to **21.7% from 24.4%**, and management expects sequentially lower Q3
demand. Watch conversion, leverage and the service mix at the next results. Sources: same release,
[Q2 report, pages 1–2 and 7](https://mb.cision.com/Main/905/4376242/4199107.pdf).

The 7532.T mistake would be buying the energy-efficiency story while overlooking acquisition
capital and a falling return on it. The report also names data centres and semiconductors as
growth drivers: a Swedish listing does not remove Leo's existing AI-capex exposure. T1 raises a
question about customer spending economics, not proof that physical cooling demand will decline;
T2 does not directly drive this equipment business.
