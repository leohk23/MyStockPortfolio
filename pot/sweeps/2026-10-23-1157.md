## 1. Where the world is

**Observation, inside the book.** Meta reported its third quarter this week, and the shares fell on 22 October. Revenue grew 26% to $64.6bn, but
capital expenditure of $34.91bn (including finance-lease principal) outran operating cash flow of
$32.79bn. Free cash flow was −$2.12bn for the quarter, against $11.05bn for the nine months. The
quarter also carried a $9.89bn charge for the teen-harm settlement with 52 state attorneys general,
which totals up to about $18.59bn. Full-year capex guidance narrowed to $135–145bn, with no 2027
figure. The board created a **Class C non-voting share**, to be issued as a one-for-one stock
dividend effective January 2027, which works as a two-for-one split
([Meta Q3 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/meta-reports-third-quarter-2026-results-302915964.html)).
Locally META closed at $648.85, down 6.98% on the day and 12.8% over the month. The Nasdaq fell
2.60% and the S&P 500 1.21% ([local market data](../../prices.json)). The press tied the fall to AI
spending, and puts the Philadelphia semiconductor index down 6.3% on the day
([Yahoo Finance market report](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/nasdaq-futures-drop-over-1-093849296.html);
press figure, not checked against an index provider).

**Inference.** For a book that is 58.76% megacap tech, this is the largest event of the week. It
is the T1 margin question arriving through the cost line rather than through price: demand is
fine (+26%), but the cash a dollar of revenue returns has gone to zero for a quarter. That is not
a sell signal. It is a reason not to add more of the same exposure through the pot. The Class C
dividend is also a **data event for this repo**: an effective 2:1 that Yahoo may or may not
restate cleanly (see section 4).

**Observation, beyond our macro block.** WTI is $100.94 locally, up 76.1% this year
([local market data](../../prices.json)). The Strait of Hormuz has been largely shut since late
February. On 22–23 October Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia and a Libyan pipeline closure added to
it ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/10/23/oil-prices-rise-as-houthis-launch-another-wave-of-attacks-on-saudi-arabia/);
narrative only). The binding shortage is middle distillates rather than crude: the IEA's October
report has global diesel exports 700,000 barrels a day lower year on year, as recorded in the
[20 October sweep](2026-10-20-0534.md).

**Inference.** Eight months in, the first-order winners are priced: STNG is up 65.9% and WDS.AX
38.9% this year, both already on the watchlist. The second-order question is who sells a
diesel-equivalent product without buying Gulf crude. That leads to renewable diesel and
sustainable aviation fuel, below.

## 3. Candidates

### NESTE.HE — Nasdaq Helsinki — Neste — new

**Why watch it.** Neste is the world's largest renewable diesel and SAF producer, and it makes a
diesel-equivalent product from waste fats rather than Gulf crude. In Q2 2026 the Renewable Products
comparable sales margin was **USD 1,223/ton against 361 a year earlier**. Segment comparable EBITDA
was EUR 859m against 174m, and group comparable EBITDA EUR 1,203m against 341m. The leverage ratio
fell from 34.3% to 29.9% in six months
([Neste half-year report](https://www.neste.com/news/nestes-half-year-financial-report-for-january-june-2026)).
The CEO credits "exceptionally wide middle distillate cracks", but also Germany adopting RED III
and higher US biofuel mandates. Those two are regulatory and outlast a war. Neste would fill an
empty slot: Energy (nothing in the book above the tail) and Europe at 7.13%. No thesis drove it.

**What has to be true.** The mandate-driven part of the margin has to be large enough that the
business earns well at a **normal** distillate crack. The 2025 margin of USD 361/ton is the
warning: that is what the business earned before the war. Q3 results on **29 October 2026** are
the first test ([Neste results date](https://www.neste.com/news/neste-to-publish-its-third-quarter-results-on-29-october-2026)).
**7532.T check: this is the closest a candidate has come to that mistake.** A record margin
created by an external shock is a tourist rebound in a different industry. The valuation filter
must be run on mid-cycle earnings, not on 2026's. If the only cheap number is the one with the
war in it, it is not cheap.
