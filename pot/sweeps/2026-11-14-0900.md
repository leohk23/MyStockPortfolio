### WALMEX.MX — Bolsa Mexicana — Walmart de México y Centroamérica (new)

- **Why:** Mexico's largest retailer, and a defensive name in a country the book does not touch. It is out of favour on margin, not on sales. Provisional Yahoo price **MXN 54.67** on 13 November, against a 52-week range of 44.80–63.38. Its weekly closes run from **65.58** (May 2025) down to **45.49** (September 2026), and it is about **−11%** over a year ([chart endpoint](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/WALMEX.MX?range=2y&interval=1wk)). Its Q3 2026 release, dated 27 October ([PDF](https://files.walmex.mx/upload/files/2026/EN/Quarterly/3Q26/Walmex%203Q26%20Earnings%20Release.pdf)), gives:
  - consolidated revenue **+3.0%**; Mexico same-store sales **+1.7%**, with ticket up 1.6% and traffic "broadly flat"
  - gross margin flat at 24.7%, with "investments to improve our price gap" paid for by Commerce Solutions; price perception improved 260bp
  - SG&A up 40bp to 17.4% of sales; EBITDA **+0.4%**, margin down 30bp to 10.2%; Q3 EPS **MXN 0.642**
  - nine months: net income **−1.7%**, EPS **MXN 2.012**
- **What would have to be true:** the price investment has to be buying share that converts to margin later, not marking a permanent reset. Mexico grew on ticket alone with flat traffic, which is the weak version of growth. Its SSS beat the industry body ANTAD by 230bp, by the company's own account, and that is the evidence for share gain. The same release says nine-month SG&A was "driven by growth investments and labor cost".

**Exposure:** adds **Consumer Defensive and Mexico**, and offsets the US concentration. The book has nothing in Latin America. **Context:** in July the US declined to renew USMCA in its current form, which moves the treaty to annual reviews through 2036 while its terms stay in force ([White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/usmca-2026-joint-review-united-states-declines-extend-agreement-triggering-annual), a law firm's summary, narrative only). My inference: a domestic grocer is less exposed to that than an exporter, but it is not immune, because the peso and Mexican wages both run through the same channel. No thesis drove this.

**7532.T test:** partly. The price is already **+20%** off its September low, and nothing in the release says margins have turned. The mistake would be buying "Walmart" as a brand without looking at a business whose traffic is flat. I have not computed a P/E: annualising nine months would overstate it, because the fourth quarter is the seasonal peak. The pipeline will strike one on reported EPS within the hour.

### LGEN.L — London Stock Exchange — Legal & General (new)

- **Why:** the UK's largest pension-risk-transfer writer, and the second-order beneficiary of the yield move in §1. Its half-year results on 5 August ([release](https://group.legalandgeneral.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026/8/2026-half-year-results-simpler-more-focused-lg-delivers-11-core-operating-eps-growth)) gave:
  - core operating profit **£918m, +7%**; core operating EPS **+11%**, with FY26 expected "above the top end of our 6–9% target range"
  - interim dividend **6.24p, +2%**; a £1.2bn buyback, about £450m done by end-July; more than **£5bn** of shareholder returns over 2025–27
  - Solvency II coverage **201%**, against a 160–190% target range; Solvency II capital generation £790m, +3%
  - **£5.7bn** of global PRT written or exclusive, with "strict pricing discipline"

  Provisional Yahoo price **314.7p** on 13 November, against a 52-week range of 217.2–318.1p, and **+33%** over a year ([chart endpoint](https://query1.finance.yahoo.com/v8/finance/chart/LGEN.L?range=2y&interval=1wk)). It publishes its first-ever quarterly trading update on **Monday 16 November**, according to [Hargreaves Lansdown's week-ahead note](https://www.hl.co.uk/shares/share-research/next-week-on-the-stock-market-16-11-2026) (a broker note, so it supplies the date only).
- **What would have to be true:** this is my inference, not the company's. Long gilt yields have to stay high enough that defined-benefit schemes keep reaching full funding and coming to market. L&G also has to keep pricing discipline while the volume grows, because "strict pricing discipline" is itself a sign that competition is thick. Solvency above the top of its range is the cushion behind the capital-return promise, so a fall back into the range would be the early warning.

**Exposure:** adds **Financial Services and the UK**, and offsets the US. Leo already holds gilts directly, so a fall in yields would hurt this name while it helped those holdings, a partial hedge. **Caveat for the pipeline:** H1 IFRS profit before tax of **£1,997m** includes the gain on disposing of the non-retained US business (same release). Any reported P/E or trough the pipeline strikes on 2026 will therefore be flattered by a one-off. The Deep dive must use core operating EPS, and must say that it has.

**7532.T test:** this is the risk. The price is within 1% of its 52-week high after a 33% year, so any rerating has already been paid for. The watchlist entry is for Monday's update and for the multiple history the pipeline will fetch, not for chasing.
