([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_09112026.htm)). The repository now has WTI at

target range ([Fed calendar](https://www.federalreserve.gov/monetarypolicy/fomccalendars.htm),
[AP narrative](https://apnews.com/article/f0ea070bd8d0d6bfa88203b62c33fed7)).

**Observation.** This is not only a bank story. Deutsche Börse said in July that Q2 net revenue
excluding its Treasury result rose **9%** to **€1.412bn**, while EBITDA on the same basis rose
**13%** to **€775m**. It also raised its 2026 Treasury-result expectation to more than **€0.7bn**
because of the changed global rate outlook and higher cash balances
([Q2 release](https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/media/news-stories/press-releases/Q2-2026-Broad-Based-Secular-Growth-and-Operating-Leverage-in-the-Second-Quarter-5397468)).

**Inference.** Higher rates and geopolitical volatility reward businesses paid on both cash and
market activity, but the useful candidate is one whose core operation was growing before today's
decision. That points to market infrastructure rather than a directional bank. It also creates the
right 7532.T question: the rate headline is not a substitute for valuing the core exchange and the
pending Allfunds acquisition, so Deutsche Börse is a watchlist addition only.
