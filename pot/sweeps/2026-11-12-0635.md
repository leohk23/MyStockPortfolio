### RUI.PA — Euronext Paris — Rubis (new)

- **Why:** energy distribution with observable volume growth offers a way to investigate the supply shock beyond yesterday's producer candidate: Q3 distribution volume grew **7%**, while fuel volume grew **2%** and fuel gross margin **13%** ([3 November update](https://www.rubis.fr/en/communique/rubis-q3-9m-trading-update-another-quarter-of-strong-operational-performance-2026-guidance-reaffirmed/)).
- **What would have to be true:** at FY2026 results on **11 March 2027**, fuel volumes and margin per unit must remain resilient and operating cash must recover as inventory funding eases; the economics must survive lower oil prices without depending on inventory gains.

The group explicitly passes oil-price changes to customers. Its **44% Q3 revenue growth is not 44% demand growth**: LPG volume was flat, and bitumen's **82%** increase amplified the total volume result. The proposed governance conversion still requires approvals; no rerating from it is assumed. All from the same Q3 release. H1 shows the risk already: net income attributable to the group rose **17%**, but reported operating cash fell **42% to €159m**; the company's adjusted operating cash was **€223m**, excluding a **€64m competition fine**, and still fell **19%**. Higher working-capital needs accompanied higher oil prices ([H1 results](https://www.rubis.fr/en/communique/rubis-h1-2026-results-strong-performance-leading-way-to-upgrade-of-2026-guidance/)).

**Exposure and 7532.T test:** adds Energy with African and Caribbean operating exposure, reducing dependence on software and chips if eventually bought. No standing thesis drove it. The mistake would be calling oil pass-through growth, or treating a fine-adjusted cash figure as money available to shareholders. The next reader must separate sustainable distribution profit, inventory effects and capital tied up. Current price and both valuation percentiles remain **–** pending the independent fetch. Yahoo confirms the [RUI.PA listing](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/RUI.PA/); its quote is not used as local evidence.
