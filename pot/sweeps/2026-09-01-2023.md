**Observation.** The oil-and-rates squeeze from the previous sweep has intensified rather than
resolved: WTI is $90.79, up 10.4% in seven days, while the US ten-year yield is 4.796% and the
S&P 500 is down 0.7% today ([local market data](../../prices.json)). **Inference.** That remains a
poor setting for buying a loud theme on revenue growth alone. Cash conversion, returns on capital
and the durability of customer savings matter more while both input costs and the discount rate
are rising.

**Observation beyond the local macro block.** July US job openings were little changed at 7.3
million; hires were 5.1 million and quits 3.1 million, also little changed
([BLS JOLTS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/jolts.nr0.htm)). Beneath that stable aggregate, Dallas
Fed research using task-level Claude usage and Lightcast postings estimates that generative-AI
automation exposure reduced Texas online job postings by 2.6% in 2025, with the heaviest exposure
in computer, clerical, managerial and other white-collar work. The authors caution that online
postings under-cover several physical occupations and that this is Texas, not the whole US
([Dallas Fed](https://www.dallasfed.org/research/economics/2026/0901)). **Inference.** AI is
beginning to show up as customer economics, not just capital spending. The useful second-order
names are incumbents that own trusted data and sit inside the workflow being automated; they still
have to prove that AI expands revenue and returns on capital rather than commoditising the product.

## 3. Candidates

### INTU — Intuit, Nasdaq

**Why:** this is an accounting-workflow incumbent already turning automation into paid products:
fiscal-2026 QuickBooks Online Accounting revenue grew 23%, TurboTax Live grew 37%, and GAAP
operating income grew 20% against 14% total revenue growth, while the Dallas Fed now finds early
real-world displacement in white-collar postings
([Intuit FY2026 results](https://investors.intuit.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000896878-26-000029/fy26q4earningspressrelease.htm),
[Dallas Fed](https://www.dallasfed.org/research/economics/2026/0901)).

**What would have to be true:** AI must deepen retention, attach and customer outcomes rather than
let a general model bypass Intuit; the 2% fall in total TurboTax units must not become structural;
and independently fetched valuation and capital-return measures must pass Leo's filter.

### REL.L — RELX, London Stock Exchange

**Why:** RELX owns verified professional data and embeds it in decisions rather than selling a
generic model. First-half underlying revenue grew 7% and adjusted operating profit 9%; Legal,
where the company attributes growth to adoption of its AI-enabled research and analytics platform,
grew underlying revenue 10% and adjusted operating profit 13%
([RELX interim results](https://www.relx.com/media/press-releases/year-2026/first-half-2026)).

**What would have to be true:** AI-enabled products must produce incremental renewals and usage
rather than merely defend the base; RELX must retain rights to the data that make its answers
trusted; development spending and 2.3× net debt/EBITDA must still leave attractive returns on
capital; and the independently fetched valuation must clear the strategy's filter.

Both names were added to the watchlist. This is candidate discovery only; no order, target price
or position size is implied.
