- **Why:** a bank whose net interest income rises when rates do. It cuts the book's US and AI weight, and it pays in the situation that hurts the rest of the book, a Fed that hikes. ### RMV.L, London Stock Exchange: Rightmove, new

- **Why:** UK property listings, in sterling, driven by UK housing transactions and not by AI capex. Earnings kept growing while the share price fell by a third, which is the same pattern as Burry's HSTECH point in Leo's reading. Here the question is whether the cause is sentiment or structure. Yahoo is expected to file it under Communication Services (unconfirmed), so it would add a little to that sector while lowering the book's US and AI dependence.
- **What would have to be true:** agency branch membership must not shrink as estate agents use AI to run fewer branches, and Rightmove's share of portal time must hold while consumers start property searches in general AI assistants. This is **T2** head-on: revenue per advertiser is priced per branch. It is also a test of **T1**: cheaper models are as available to Rightmove as to anyone trying to bypass it.

From the [H1 2026 RNS](https://plc.rightmove.co.uk/content/uploads/2026/07/2026.07.31-Rightmove-final-RNS-H12026-unsigned.pdf), 31 July. I decoded the text myself from the PDF's font maps. A few words came out garbled, and I use none of those:

| Measure, H1 2026 | Figure | H1 2025 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue | £225.8m (+7%) | £211.7m |
| Underlying operating profit | £155.1m (+3%) | £151.3m |
| Underlying basic EPS | 15.6p (+6%) | 14.7p |
| Basic EPS | 14.8p | 14.1p |
| ARPA, total | £1,726 (+7%) | £1,609 |
| Agency branches, 30 June | 16,591 | 16,382 |
| New Homes developments, 30 June | 2,766 | 2,941 |

That is T2's early-warning pattern: revenue grows **through price** (ARPA +7%) while customer count is flat (total membership 19,357 against 19,323). For Rightmove that is also how the business has always worked, so the pattern alone does not prove T2. The margin is the newer fact: revenue grew 7% but underlying operating profit only 3%, which the company attributes to technology and AI investment. The [6 November trading update](https://plc.rightmove.co.uk/content/uploads/2026/11/261106-Trading-update.pdf) is a PDF with no text layer I could decode. Press accounts say it narrowed 2026 revenue growth to 6–7% and cut ARPA guidance, but I carry no figure from them.

**Price, provisional (Yahoo):** **534p** intraday on 25 November, **34.3%** below the 812.2p weekly close in the week of 27 July 2025, **32.2%** above the 404p low in the week of 10 May 2026, and level with the 540.2p of a year ago.

**7532.T test:** the opposite risk. 7532.T was buying a rebound without checking the fundamentals. The danger here is reading a structural change as a sentiment de-rating because the reported earnings still look fine. The falsifier is concrete: the agency branch count and the share-of-time figure, which the company reports every half-year. Both fail first if AI search takes the consumer.
