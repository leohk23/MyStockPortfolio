## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The energy shock has reached monetary policy rather than remaining a commodity story. US CPI was 3.4% year on year in August, with energy up 16.3%; the Federal Reserve then raised its target range by 25 basis points to 3.75%–4.00%. The ECB also raised its deposit rate to 2.50% and projects 2026 inflation of 3.0% against 0.9% growth. Locally, WTI is $92.41, up 45.64% over one year, and the US ten-year yield is 5.184%. **Inference.** A long-duration recovery story now has to clear both expensive funding and pressure on household purchasing power. Reported revenue growth is not enough: cash conversion, leverage and the amount of capital needed to grow matter more while both major central banks are tightening. Sources: [US Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/cpi_09112026.htm), [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/monetary20260916a.htm), [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2026/html/ecb.mp260910~314e508016.en.html), [local market data](../../prices.json).

**Observation.** The EU customs reform is now law. It makes a non-EU e-commerce platform the importer responsible for formalities and duties, removes the old duty exemption for parcels below €150 and introduces an EU-wide handling fee from November; more than 90% of e-commerce parcels entering the EU came from China. **Inference.** This is a second-order competitive change, not merely customs administration. It narrows the landed-cost and compliance advantage of direct-from-China marketplaces, while favouring a European platform only if enforcement is real and the fee does not fall equally on its own partner parcels. Sources: [Council of the EU](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2026/09/03/eu-customs-council-greenlights-landmark-reform/), [European Commission](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/eu-customs-reform_en).

## 3. Candidate

### Zalando — `ZAL.DE`, Xetra — new to the watchlist

**Why it is here.** The EU customs reform creates a specific test of whether a Europe-based fashion platform can take share as direct-import platforms lose part of their price and compliance advantage. Zalando's Q2 group GMV rose 20.7% to €4.915bn and revenue rose 20.8% to €3.424bn, but the cleaner lead is B2B: revenue grew 27.6% to €334.7m and adjusted EBIT margin expanded to 12.2% from 4.3%. The partner business reached 36.9% of stand-alone B2C GMV, moving more volume toward a capital-light model. This adds European consumer and commerce-infrastructure economics rather than another US megacap or semiconductor exposure. Sources: [Zalando Q2 2026 results](https://corporate.zalando.com/en/financials/zalando-q2-2026-results), [EU customs reform](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/customs/eu-customs-reform_en).

Yahoo's provisional off-universe history has the shares at €22.06 on 23 September, against €25.84 on 15 September 2025. The price is provisional and no outside multiple is adopted; the normal pipeline should now establish the comparable earnings and valuation record. Source: [Yahoo Finance, provisional](https://de.finance.yahoo.com/quote/ZAL.DE/history/).

**What must be true.** Stand-alone customer growth and B2C economics must improve after stripping out the ABOUT YOU consolidation; B2B margin gains must survive integration and convert to group cash; and the customs reform must actually disadvantage direct non-EU rivals without imposing the same parcel economics on Zalando's partner business. The company already guided 2026 GMV and revenue growth to the lower half of its prior range, while Q2 net income fell to €73.8m from €96.6m, so acquisition growth and adjusted profit cannot substitute for owner earnings.

**Is this the 7532.T mistake again?** It would be if a regulatory headline and reported acquisition growth were mistaken for better underlying economics. The candidate survives only as a measurable share, margin and cash-conversion test, with valuation still to be established locally.

**Action.** Added `ZAL.DE` to [`watchlist.json`](../../watchlist.json) for the independent quote, earnings and valuation pipeline. No order proposed.
