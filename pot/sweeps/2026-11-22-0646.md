- **What would have to be true:** UK contracts must become profitable installations and recurring service, with improving reported per-share earnings in the next annual results; rollout volume alone is insufficient.

Its [23 October Q3 report, pages 5–8](https://media.tomra.com/download/1fad059ecf6d11f195fddabb6db84e3a) gives revenue **€366m, +20%**, operating cash flow **€112m against €64m**, but gross margin **43.0% against 44.1%**. Nine-month reported EPS stayed **€0.17**, while adjusted EPS rose to **€0.20 from €0.16**. The gap matters: stronger sales have not yet produced higher year-to-date reported EPS. The [issuer's July UK announcement](https://news.cision.com/tomra-systems-asa/r/tomra--appointed-majority-supplier-to-leading-retail-chain-in-uk,c4375490) records an appointment to supply approximately **1,200 machines** to a retail chain. That is evidence of procurement, not installed revenue.

Watch contract economics and reported earnings before calling this cheap. ### ROCK-B.CO, Nasdaq Copenhagen: ROCKWOOL, new

- **Why:** the second-order beneficiary of expensive energy is the insulation that reduces consumption; this company has disclosed volume growth rather than just an efficiency slogan.
- **What would have to be true:** the next nine-month update, discussed at the issuer-confirmed **26 November results call**, must show that pricing is catching energy costs without sacrificing volume; subsequent capacity spending must restore cash generation and capital returns ([issuer call notice](https://www.rockwool.com/group/about-us/investors/financial-reports/)).

The [19 August H1 report, pages 1–6](https://ne-cdn.eurolandir.com/press-releases-attachments./4168087/se-2026-22-en.pdf) shows Q2 sales **€1,000m, +10%**, driven by volumes. The qualification is substantial: H1 EBIT **fell to €249m from €266m**; company-defined rolling ROIC fell to **15.7% from 19.9%**; free cash flow was **−€151m**. Full-year investment guidance is about **€750m**, excluding acquisitions. Comparatives exclude Russia following deconsolidation; that change must be reconciled before interpreting any historical multiple. A good product can still be a poor investment when capacity absorbs the cash.

**7532.T test:** do not assume a European building recovery or count a weaker currency as operating improvement. Actual volume growth earns monitoring, while falling profit and cash keep this a recovery question. It adds **Industrials**, specifically building products ([Yahoo classification and listing identity](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/quote/ROCK-B.CO/)), rather than another AI supplier.
