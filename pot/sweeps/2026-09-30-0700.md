## 3. Candidates

### Kemira Oyj — `KEMIRA.HE`, Nasdaq Helsinki — new

**Why watch it.** Kemira adds European water-treatment chemicals rather than more US technology exposure, and therefore adds chemical/materials economics to a book dominated by Technology and Communication Services. The attraction is not a generic water-scarcity story: Q2 revenue was flat only because acquisitions offset a 2% organic decline, but Water Solutions revenue grew while the group's operative EBITDA margin fell to 16.1% from 19.0% as energy-linked raw-material and logistics costs rose. That makes this a measurable pricing-power test rather than an obvious thematic winner. Source: [Kemira's half-year report](https://www.kemira.com/news-and-stories/newsroom/releases/kemira-oyjs-half-year-financial-report-2026-profitability-weakened-by-cost-inflation-performance-improvement-measures-continue/).

**What would have to be true.** Water Solutions growth must become organic rather than acquisition-led, delayed price increases must recover more of the approximately €100m full-year cost inflation, and returns on the enlarged capital base must stabilise. Kemira expected to recover only about half of that cost inflation, while 2025 operative ROCE had already fallen to 16.5% from 20.8%; the counterweight is first-half operating cash flow rising to €150.9m from €118.9m. Sources: [half-year report](https://www.kemira.com/news-and-stories/newsroom/releases/kemira-oyjs-half-year-financial-report-2026-profitability-weakened-by-cost-inflation-performance-improvement-measures-continue/), [Kemira financial information](https://www.kemira.com/investors/financial-information/).

This fails the 7532.T check if “water demand” substitutes for evidence of organic growth, pass-through and returns on capital. No standing thesis drove it. It is a geography and sector diversifier whose valuation is deliberately left to the repository's independent fetch.
