# Sweep — 31 August 2026

## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The oil shock is no longer just a spot-price story. Local WTI is $85.13, up
29.8% over one year but down 7.6% over three months ([prices.json](../../prices.json)); meanwhile,
the IEA says observed inventories fell 69 million barrels in July and 410 million barrels from the
start of the war, with Atlantic Basin refining margins at record highs as product supply lagged
crude supply ([August Oil Market Report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-august-2026)).
Renewed US-Iran fighting lifted oil again on 31 August
([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/00f872327d65e5330598054a234dc25a)). **Inference.** A
simple oil-producer chase risks buying the loudest trade near peak scarcity. The quieter question
is which owners of scarce, efficient real assets can preserve pricing power while fuel costs and
high interest rates damage their customers.

**Observation.** China's August manufacturing PMI improved by 0.6 points to 49.8, but the split is
uneven: large firms were at 50.6, medium firms at 49.4 and small firms at 47.9; new orders improved
to 50.6 ([National Bureau of Statistics](https://www.stats.gov.cn/sj/zxfbhjd/202608/t20260831_1965154.html)).
Locally, the Hang Seng is down 0.9% over one year and 3.6% year to date while copper is up 45.3%
over one year ([prices.json](../../prices.json)). **Inference.** Demand is improving at the top of
the industrial system without becoming a broad recovery. That argues for globally diversified
capital-goods and service businesses with visible orders, not a blanket China-rebound thesis.

## 3. Candidates

### AER — AerCap Holdings, NYSE

**Why:** aircraft supply remains constrained just as expensive fuel raises the value of efficient
fleets; AerCap reported $1.5 billion of quarterly operating cash flow, 18% adjusted return on
equity and $119.21 book value per share
([Q2 results](https://www.aercap.com/news-media/press-releases/detail/643/aercap-holdings-n-v-reports-strong-financial-results-for)),
while the provisional 28 August price of $147.49 is 1.24 times that book value
([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AER/)).

**What would have to be true:** lease rates and sale premiums must remain durable, airline credit
must withstand high fuel costs, and the 2.05-to-1 adjusted debt-to-equity ratio must not turn
asset scarcity into a refinancing problem.

### KSB3.DE — KSB SE & Co. KGaA preference share, Xetra

**Why:** pumps, valves and installed-base service are a less crowded way to follow energy, water
and industrial investment; first-half order intake rose to €1.849 billion from €1.698 billion even
as major end markets remained difficult
([H1 results](https://www.ksb.com/en-global/media/press-releases/uebersicht/ksb-reports-strong-second-quarter-performance-3131140)).

**What would have to be true:** the large Energy order must lead to repeat demand rather than a
one-off spike, order growth must convert into revenue and cash, and service economics must offset
weakness in smaller industrial customers. The independently fetched valuation must also clear
Leo's filter.

No order, price target or position size is implied. `AER` and `KSB3.DE` were added to the
