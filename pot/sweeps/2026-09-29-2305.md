## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The first energy shock was absorbed with inventories and government support, but those buffers are thinner while capital is dear. The [OECD's September outlook](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-economic-outlook-interim-report-september-2026_f751d02b-en/full-report.html) says first-half global growth slowed to a 2.6% annualised rate, inventories and alternative supply softened the initial Gulf disruption, and renewed energy pressure is now lifting business costs. In [local market data](../../prices.json), WTI is $89.53, up 43.55% over one year, and the US ten-year yield is 5.255%. **Inference.** This is not broad panic: it is a squeeze on companies that need both cheap inputs and cheap refinancing. Contracted or regulated revenue is worth investigating, but only where the allowed return stays above the marginal financing cost.

**Observation.** A second supply risk is becoming concrete outside the repository's 17-series macro block. [NOAA's 10 September diagnostic](https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.html) puts the chance of a very strong El Niño above 90% for the Northern Hemisphere autumn and winter, while warning that event strength does not guarantee any particular regional impact. The OECD separately identifies extreme weather and food prices as downside risks. **Inference.** Water resilience and agricultural productivity deserve attention, but “El Niño beneficiary” is not an investment case. Regional rainfall, farmer income, order conversion and capital returns have to bridge the weather event to shareholder cash.

## 3. Candidates

### United Utilities Group — `UU.L`, London — new

**Why watch it.** This adds a UK regulated-water business to a book with only 1.17% Utilities and reduces reliance on US technology. Ofwat has provisionally allowed £958m of United Utilities' 2026 cost-change claim, while the company says it remains on track for about £2bn of 2026/27 investment, £11.5bn across AMP8, roughly 10% compound asset-base growth and 10–11% regulatory returns. Sources: [Ofwat's draft decision](https://www.ofwat.gov.uk/funding-set-to-unlock-further-improvements-for-water-company-customers-communities-and-the-environment/), [United Utilities' 25 September update](https://corporate.unitedutilities.com/globalassets/documents/corporate-documents/trading-update-and-2026-cost-change-draft-decision-response.pdf).

**What would have to be true.** Ofwat's December final decision must preserve the economics, delivery must avoid penalties, and the return on incremental capital must outrun financing and dilution. FY2026 net debt was £9.943bn, the effective interest rate rose to 4.2% from 3.8%, and the expanded programme required an £800m equity raise. The provisional Yahoo quote is 1,409p, up 26.09% over one year, so this is not presented as an undiscovered bargain. Sources: [FY2026 results](https://corporate.unitedutilities.com/globalassets/documents/corporate-documents/2025-26-preliminary-full-year-results-rns-final.pdf), [provisional Yahoo quote](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/UU.L/).

`UU.L` was added to [the watchlist](../../watchlist.json) so the normal pipeline can establish its quote and valuation history independently.

**Screened out, not added:** Valmont Industries (`VMI`) is an intuitive irrigation and grid-infrastructure response to the weather and power themes, but its filing does not yet show the weather translating into agricultural demand. First-half Agriculture sales fell 15.4%; North American volumes remained soft and international sales fell 30.6%, primarily amid the Middle East conflict. A forecast weather event cannot substitute for farmer purchasing power or orders. Without a regional demand inflection this would be the 7532.T mistake again. Source: [Valmont's Q2 2026 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/102729/000010272926000044/vmi-20260627x10q.htm).

No standing thesis drove `UU.L`. T1 is a risk to the data-centre portion of its proposed growth programme if AI infrastructure demand disappoints, but housing, wastewater capacity, asset replacement and resilience are separate drivers. The candidate is primarily a sector and geography diversifier, not a thesis confirmation.
