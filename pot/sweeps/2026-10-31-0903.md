### SAN.PA — Euronext Paris — Sanofi — new

- **Why it might belong.** Q3 sales grew 14.3% at constant exchange rates, business EPS grew 19.6%, free cash flow was EUR3.269bn and 2026 guidance was raised. It adds European Healthcare to a book with only 1.02% Healthcare and reduces both the sector and geographic concentration in US growth shares. The source is [Sanofi's Q3 release](https://dotcom.prod.accelerator.sanofi/en/media-room/press-releases/2026/2026-10-30-06-30-00-3392028).
- **What would have to be true.** The launch portfolio must grow quickly enough to replace Dupixent economics before loss of exclusivity, rather than today's 34.8% Dupixent growth masking the cliff. Vaccines must recover from the 6.6% decline, and reported earnings and cash must support the business-EPS presentation. The repo's valuation history must then show a price that clears Leo's filter. **The 7532.T test:** this is a product-concentration and pipeline case, not “pharma is defensive.”

### CL — New York Stock Exchange — Colgate-Palmolive — new

- **Why it might belong.** Q3 organic sales grew 3.6%, with both volume and pricing, while base-business gross margin rose 90 basis points. Nine-month operating cash flow was $2.913bn, and toothpaste and manual-toothbrush market shares were 41.4% and 33.1%. It adds Consumer Defensive exposure, currently 3.52% of the book. The figures come from the [Q3 issuer release](https://investor.colgatepalmolive.com/news-releases/news-release-details/colgate-palmolive-announces-3rd-quarter-2026-results) and [prepared remarks](https://investor.colgatepalmolive.com/static-files/245e4f40-58c2-4174-950c-dceece2f3fb3).
- **What would have to be true.** Volume must keep growing after the pricing cycle, advertising must defend the category shares, and asset turnover and return on capital must justify the margin rather than the margin being treated as the answer. Local valuation still has to pass the filter. **The 7532.T test:** brand familiarity and a defensive label do not substitute for capital productivity or price.

### GD — New York Stock Exchange — General Dynamics — new

- **Why it might belong.** Q3 revenue rose 12.9%, EPS rose 15.7% and operating cash flow was 139% of net earnings. Backlog ended at $220.3bn, giving the business visible demand while adding Industrials and defence rather than another software or semiconductor name. The figures come from [General Dynamics' Q3 release](https://www.gd.com/Articles/2026/10/30/general-dynamics-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results).
- **What would have to be true.** The backlog must convert into deliveries, margin and cash without schedule slippage or cost overruns. The 6.8-to-1 book-to-bill was driven by large Virginia- and Columbia-class submarine awards, so it cannot be treated as a recurring growth rate. The local valuation must show the procurement cycle is not already priced. **The 7532.T test:** this is not “defence spending is rising”; it is whether funded work earns durable returns.
