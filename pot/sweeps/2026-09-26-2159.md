## 3. Candidate

### H.B. Fuller — `FUL`, NYSE — new to the watchlist

**Why it is here.** H.B. Fuller's 23 September result is a live test of pricing power in a cost shock, and it adds specialty-materials economics rather than more software or semiconductors. Q3 revenue rose 5.2% to $938m and organic revenue rose 4.4%; price added 7.4%, more than offsetting lower volume. Adjusted gross margin rose 120 basis points to 33.5%, and adjusted EBITDA margin rose 80 basis points to 19.9%. The useful fact is not that revenue grew, but that price exceeded the 6.2% rise in the BLS adhesive-and-sealant index while margin expanded. Sources: [H.B. Fuller Q3 release](https://investors.hbfuller.com/news/news-details/2026/H-B--Fuller-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx), [BLS August PPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/ppi_09102026.htm).

Yahoo's provisional off-universe quote was $50.06 at the 25 September close, down 16.2% over one year and inside a $46.70–$68.63 52-week range. Its provisional trailing P/E was 15.15. These market figures are not yet local and should be replaced by the normal pipeline now that the name is on the watchlist. Source: [Yahoo Finance, provisional](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/FUL/).

**What must be true.** Price increases must hold without continued unit contraction. The balance sheet must also convert the accounting margin into owner cash: net working capital rose to 18.5% of annualised revenue from 17.0%, receivable days rose to 63 from 58, and inventory days to 88 from 78. The planned £715m purchase of Advanced Medical Solutions is 12.9 times forecast 2026 EBITDA before synergies and below 8 times only if the full run-rate synergies arrive, so integration and deleveraging must work without another round of price-led volume erosion. Sources: [Q3 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/39368/000143774926031145/ful20260831c_10q.htm), [AMS acquisition terms](https://investors.hbfuller.com/news/news-details/2026/H-B--Fuller-Announces-Offer-to-Acquire-Advanced-Medical-Solutions/default.aspx).

**Is this the 7532.T mistake again?** It would be if a topical inflation story or a cheap-looking multiple excused falling physical demand, working-capital absorption or acquisition debt. The candidate survives only because the next filings can distinguish price, volume, cash and leverage separately.
