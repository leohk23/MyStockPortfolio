# Sweep — 30 August 2026

## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The local market picture is expensive in places but not one-way euphoric. The
S&P 500 is 1.3% below its all-time high and up 20.2% over one year, while the Hang Seng is down
0.1% over one year and 2.9% year to date; only 16 of 43 country markets are within 5% of their
highs ([signals.json](../../signals.json), [prices.json](../../prices.json)). US five- and ten-year
yields are 19.7% and 10.4% higher than a year ago. WTI is up 27.2% over one year but down 9.5% in
three months, while the World Bank's July indices show energy down 1.1% month on month, metals down
2.8%, and European gas up 19.1%
([World Bank Commodity Markets](https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets)).
**Inference.** The shock is easing unevenly, not disappearing. High-duration equities still face
a demanding discount rate, and a commodity label is too coarse: exposure to European gas, oil,
or industrial metals now means three different things.

**Observation.** China's July production data split sharply by industry. Industrial value added
rose 4.5% year on year and high-tech manufacturing rose 16.9%, but motor-vehicle output fell 0.1%,
cement fell 11.6%, and crude processing fell 15.8%
([NBS industrial production](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202608/t20260818_1965071.html)).
January–July industrial profit rose 17.6%; electronics profit rose 110.0% and non-ferrous metals
91.8%, while auto manufacturing profit fell 20.4%. Revenue rose 6.5%, inventories 10.8%, and
receivables 8.5%
([NBS industrial profits](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202608/t20260828_1965134.html)).
**Inference.** This is a selective manufacturing recovery with working-capital accumulation, not
a broad industrial boom. It strengthens the case for testing each Chinese holding's cash
conversion and end-market position instead of buying the headline growth rate.

**Observation.** US value retail is winning traffic even while the aggregate consumer signal is
mixed. Ross Stores' second-quarter sales rose 13% and comparable sales 10%, primarily from
traffic; even excluding a tariff refund, operating margin improved 205 basis points
([Ross Stores filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/745732/000074573226000038/q226exhibit991.htm)).
Walmart US comparable sales excluding fuel rose 2.6%, global e-commerce grew 23%, and US
transactions rose 1.5%
([Walmart filing](https://stock.walmart.com/sec-filings/all-sec-filings/content/0000104169-26-000145/earningsreleasefy27q2.htm)).
**Inference.** Consumers are still spending, but value, convenience and treasure-hunt formats are
taking share. That supports researching an off-price operator; it does not justify paying any
price after a strong release.

### Michael Burry, 26 February — VIEs

The saved article's useful claim remains about sizing, not forecasting: a VIE is an enforceability
risk that should change the maximum position, and a non-VIE Hong Kong share is only a relative
hedge. Since publication, the locally tracked Hang Seng Tech ETF `3067.HK` has fallen 9.2%,
Alibaba 19.7%, and BYD 3.2% through 28 August ([history.json](../../history.json)). The risk is no
longer as richly priced, but lower prices do not repair the legal structure. The thesis therefore
remains relevant and unproven; the practical consequence is still smaller VIE exposure, not a
blanket China call.

### Michael Burry, 11 March — common stocks and uncommon value

The Hang Seng Tech ETF is down 8.2% since publication, BYD 6.3%, and Haidilao 26.3%
([history.json](../../history.json)). PDD's provisional close fell from $102.94 on 11 March to
$85.69 on 28 August, down 16.8%
([historical price](https://www.financecharts.com/stocks/PDD/summary/price),
[current price](https://finance.yahoo.co.jp/quote/PDD/history)). All three named stocks are
cheaper; only price, not thesis quality, improved automatically.

BYD is the material change. At HK$91.95 it remains 23% above the article's HK$75 full-position
level ([prices.json](../../prices.json)), while its new interim filing shows first-half revenue
down 7.1%, attributable profit down 20.5%, and EPS down 21.1%. Operating cash flow improved to
RMB37.3bn, but bills payable rose to RMB53.4bn from RMB22.5bn at year-end
([BYD interim filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0828/2026082801633.pdf)).
That neither confirms nor clears the saved article's supplier-financing concern: cash generation
held up, but the bill-financing balance expanded. The operating thesis weakened before the price
reached the author's valuation level, so this is a re-look, not an add.

Haidilao's first-half filing still supports a narrower self-help thesis: total revenue rose 7.9%,
core operating profit 4.4%, and delivery revenue 121.2%, while core restaurant revenue fell 4.0%
and same-store sales 1.3%
([HKEX interim filing](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0825/2026082501119.pdf)).
It remains on the watchlist from the previous sweep; nothing new today improves yesterday's
falsifiers. PDD's second-quarter revenue and operating profit rose 8% while net income fell 12%,
but the release still lacks a geography or Pinduoduo/Temu split
([PDD release](https://investor.pddholdings.com/static-files/92cc7e1f-611b-4c0e-8cb5-5f5c7819e1cc)).
The disclosure objection survives the lower price, so PDD is again rejected.

### Ivan Li, 14 April — convexity

This remains a method article rather than a ticker source. Since publication, local prices put
Nvidia up 10.7%, VOO up 10.8%, Amazon up 7.0%, Meta down 12.8%, Netflix down 23.1%, and Tesla down
4.2% ([history.json](../../history.json)). The dispersion supports the article's point that a few
winners can drive long-run returns, but four months cannot establish a multi-decade law. For this
pot, patience still ends at a written falsifier, thesis exhaustion, or portfolio-limit breach;
there is no mechanical stop and no forced candidate from the article.

## 3. Candidates

### 1211.HK — BYD, Hong Kong — re-look, already held

**Why:** the share is only 6.3% below the saved article date and still above its stated valuation
level, while the new filing shows revenue and earnings contracting and bills payable expanding.

**What would have to be true:** overseas and higher-end growth must offset domestic auto pressure,
operating cash conversion must persist without further dependence on supplier financing, and
margin deterioration must prove cyclical rather than structural.

### BBW — Build-A-Bear Workshop, New York

**Why:** a fresh Burry lead and a 27 August earnings repricing exposed a contested, simple business:
second-quarter revenue fell 7.2% and first-half adjusted pre-tax income fell 18.4%, but it ended
the quarter with no revolver borrowings and 674 global locations
([company results](https://ir.buildabear.com/news-releases/news-release-details/build-bear-workshop-reports-fiscal-second-quarter-2026-results)).

**What would have to be true:** declining retail and e-commerce demand must stabilise, franchise
and commercial growth must represent durable brand monetisation, and earnings power must work
without the $7m tariff refund or buyback-supported EPS.

### ROST — Ross Stores, Nasdaq

**Why:** 10% traffic-led comparable sales on top of a 2% prior-year gain, a 205-basis-point
underlying margin improvement, and higher store-opening plans suggest share gain rather than mere
inflation; the local pipeline can now test whether the post-results price already pays for it.

**What would have to be true:** new and existing customer traffic must persist through harder
comparisons, merchandise availability and markdown discipline must protect the margin gain, and
the thesis must remain attractive after removing the tariff refund.

No order, price target or position size is implied. `BBW` and `ROST` were added to the watchlist so
the normal pipeline can supply prices, history and valuation; `1211.HK` was already in the book.
