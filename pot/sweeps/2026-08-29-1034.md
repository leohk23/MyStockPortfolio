# Sweep — 29 August 2026

## 1. Where the world is

### What is true

The world is absorbing a genuine increase in electricity use, not just an AI-market narrative. The
IEA expects global electricity demand to grow 3.6% in 2026 and 3.8% in 2027, versus 3.0% in 2025;
it also expects renewables to overtake coal generation in 2026 and says grid expansion and
modernisation are critical to integrating that supply
([IEA mid-year update](https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-mid-year-update-2026/executive-summary)).
The local data agree that the physical inputs have moved: copper is up 46.95% over one year, while
WTI is up 30.35% over one year but down 9.46% over three months
([local macro data](../../prices.json)).

That investment boom sits beside a less comfortable macro picture. The IMF forecasts 3.0% global
growth in 2026 and says disinflation has stalled, with technology momentum offsetting the drag from
war ([IMF July update](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/07/08/world-economic-outlook-update-july-2026)).
Markets show the same split: Nasdaq is up 23.06% over one year and the S&P 500 19.37%, while the
Hang Seng is down 0.13%; US five- and ten-year yields are up 21.14% and 11.66% respectively over
one year ([local macro data](../../prices.json)).

### What I take from it

Power demand is real, but the first-order trade is crowded enough that a good backlog is no longer
sufficient. GE Vernova, Caterpillar and Hammond Power Solutions all reported exceptional demand,
but their obviousness is the reason not to promote them here
([GE Vernova](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1996810/000199681026000147/gevpressrelease2q26.htm),
[Caterpillar](https://www.caterpillar.com/en/news/corporate-press-releases/h/2q26-results-caterpillar-inc.html),
[Hammond Power Solutions](https://asia.hammondpowersolutions.com/news/2026/july/Quarter-2-2026-Financial-Results)).
The useful candidates are businesses
where reported orders or cash generation improved while the price lagged, and where the next check
can distinguish conversion from backlog theatre. That produces AMSC and OSIS below.

The stalled-disinflation backdrop also argues for not making this a fourth consecutive list of AI
infrastructure names. Novo Nordisk is the counterweight: a familiar, profitable consumer-health
franchise where the market is pricing a material deterioration in realised price and competitive
position. NVIDIA appears only as a re-look because the newly revised local valuation scan fired on
it after results; it is not being rediscovered by the Sweep.

## 3. Candidates

### AMSC — Nasdaq — American Superconductor

**Why:** Revenue rose 30% to a record $94.1m, orders exceeded $130m, twelve-month backlog exceeded
$300m and operating cash flow nearly quadrupled to $16m, yet the share price was down 43.40% over
one year by 26 August
([company results](https://ir.amsc.com/news-releases/news-release-details/amsc-reports-first-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial-results),
[price source](https://www.marketbeat.com/stocks/NASDAQ/AMSC/chart/)). This is a quieter grid,
mining-power and resiliency route than the large turbine and transformer winners.

**What would have to be true:** Acquired and organic revenue must turn into durable per-share
earnings: first-quarter non-GAAP net income fell to $7.6m from $11.6m even as revenue grew, and the
company warns that backlog may not convert and that suppliers and government funding matter
([company results](https://ir.amsc.com/news-releases/news-release-details/amsc-reports-first-quarter-fiscal-year-2026-financial-results)).

### OSIS — Nasdaq — OSI Systems

**Why:** Fiscal-2026 revenue rose 4% to $1.786bn, non-GAAP EPS rose 11% to $10.35, operating cash
flow reached a record $276m and backlog a record $1.9bn, while the shares were down 10.18% over one
year at 28 August
([company results](https://investors.osi-systems.com/news-releases/news-release-details/osi-systems-reports-fiscal-2026-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year),
[price source](https://www.foxbusiness.com/quote?stockTicker=OSIS)). Security scanning,
optoelectronics and healthcare make it a second-order infrastructure/security name rather than a
single-theme defence bet.

**What would have to be true:** Security deliveries delayed by the Middle East conflict must
convert rather than roll forward indefinitely, and the fourth-quarter 4% revenue decline must be
timing rather than lost demand. Management's fiscal-2027 target of 5.0–8.1% revenue growth and
7.5–11.0% non-GAAP EPS growth must show up in GAAP cash earnings, not only adjusted guidance
([company results](https://investors.osi-systems.com/news-releases/news-release-details/osi-systems-reports-fiscal-2026-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year)).

### NVO — NYSE ADR / Nasdaq Copenhagen home listing — Novo Nordisk

**Why:** Q2 adjusted sales rose 7% and adjusted operating profit 11% at constant exchange rates,
and guidance was raised, yet the ADR trades at 11.86 times trailing earnings and has returned only
1.78% over five years
([company announcement](https://www.novonordisk.com/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=916589),
[price and valuation](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NVO/)). It is a profitable healthcare
franchise outside the portfolio's technology-heavy centre, with pessimism visible in the multiple.

**What would have to be true:** GLP-1 volume must offset lower realised prices and intensifying
competition without sacrificing earnings quality. The raised full-year range still calls for
adjusted sales and operating profit to be flat to down 6%, and Q2 adjustments exclude a DKK6.3bn
pipeline-asset impairment and 340B provision effects
([company announcement](https://www.novonordisk.com/news-and-media/news-and-ir-materials/news-details.html?id=916589),
[half-year filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353278/000035327826000023/caq22026.htm)).

### NVDA — Nasdaq — NVIDIA — re-look (held)

**Why:** The revised local Scan now puts trailing P/E at 27.50, the lowest observation and 0th
percentile across 261 weeks, while Q2 revenue rose 106% to $96.2bn, data-centre revenue rose 117% to
$89.0bn and GAAP gross margin was 75.0%
([local signal scan](../../signals.json),
[company results](https://investor.nvidia.com/news/press-release-details/2026/NVIDIA-Announces-Financial-Results-for-Second-Quarter-Fiscal-2027/default.aspx)).

**What would have to be true:** The earnings denominator must be durable: a five-year-low P/E is
not the same as a cheap business if hyperscaler capital spending or 75% gross margins normalise.
The market still values NVIDIA at $5.51tn and 18.36 times trailing sales, so the P/E signal needs a
fresh cash-flow and customer-concentration check before it means value
([market data](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NVDA/)).
