# Sweep — 31 August 2026

## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** Germany's August inflation split is now visible: headline CPI is provisionally
2.9% year on year, energy is up 10.5%, and inflation excluding food and energy is 2.4%
([Destatis](https://www.destatis.de/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2026/08/PD26_311_611.html?nn=2110)).
That agency release goes beyond the repo's macro block. **Inference.** This is still primarily an
energy shock rather than broad demand inflation. It squeezes customers before it creates a general
pricing boom, so the more useful second-order exposure is paid energy reduction and plant
availability, not simply another producer of the commodity that has already moved.

**Observation.** The late market reaction is consistent with that distinction: local WTI is
$85.92, up 3.0% on the day and 49.9% year to date, while the Euro Stoxx 50 is down 1.0% on the day
and the US ten-year yield is 4.758% ([prices.json](../../prices.json)). Renewed US-Iran fighting was
the immediate narrative catalyst
([Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/00f872327d65e5330598054a234dc25a)). **Inference.**
Higher energy and discount rates are arriving together. A candidate therefore has to show
recurring work, cash conversion and pricing discipline; a thematic backlog alone risks repeating
the 7532.T mistake.

**Why:** it sells the less glamorous work of keeping buildings, grids and industrial assets
efficient; second-quarter organic growth rebounded to 3.1% and first-half EBITA margin rose to
6.2%, while technical-facility-management demand and contract renewals remained strong
([H1 results](https://www.spie.com/en/regulated-information),

**What would have to be true:** organic growth and renewals must remain durable after the energy
shock, bolt-on acquisitions must not substitute for underlying growth, and margin expansion must
convert into cash without leverage drifting; the independently fetched valuation must also clear
Leo's filter.

No order, price target or position size is implied. `SPIE.PA` was added to the watchlist so the
normal pipeline can supply price, earnings and valuation history.
