## 3. Candidate

### Technip Energies — `TE.PA`, Euronext Paris — new to the watchlist

**Why it is here.** The OECD's evidence says the first response to the Hormuz shock has been supply diversification and alternative routes. Technip Energies sells the engineering and project delivery behind LNG and other energy infrastructure rather than owning the commodity. Its H1 adjusted order intake was €12.7bn and adjusted backlog reached €25.0bn, up 57% from year-end and equal to 3.5 times FY2025 revenue. The company says about 75% of awards over the preceding 24 months came from outside the Middle East. This name was screened but not added on 2 September; the OECD's new evidence that diversification is already carrying the shock, while buffers are depleting, is the change that brings it forward now. It adds a European Energy business to a book with almost no direct Energy exposure, rather than another US technology name. Source: [Technip Energies H1 results](https://investors.technipenergies.com/news-releases/news-release-details/technip-energies-h1-2026-financial-results).

**What must be true.** The backlog must convert into statutory profit and owner cash at acceptable project margins. H1 adjusted revenue was almost flat at €3.653bn, recurring EBITDA fell 33% to €212.3m, and attributable net profit fell 50% to €95.9m. Management cut Project Delivery EBITDA-margin guidance to above 5.0% from 6.5%–7.5%. Reported free cash flow was €957m, but €774m came from working-capital and provision movements; excluding those, free cash flow was €183m. The next result must show that customer advances and provisions are not hiding weak underlying conversion and that Middle East disruption does not consume the economics of the non-Middle-East awards.

**Is this the 7532.T mistake again?** It would be if the energy-security story or the record backlog excused collapsing margins. The candidate survives because signed work and a diversified award mix are observable; it fails if execution, provisions and working capital prevent that work from earning a return. No external price or multiple is used. The normal pipeline must establish valuation independently.

**Action.** Added `TE.PA` to [`watchlist.json`](../../watchlist.json). No order proposed.
