## 1. Where the world is

**Observation beyond our macro block.** The US August employment release reports 162,000 added
jobs and unemployment unchanged at 4.1%. Food services and local government education account
for much of the increase, while information employment fell by 23,000. **Inference.** This is
evidence against treating the economy as uniformly weak, but also against reading the headline
as broad corporate acceleration. Information job losses alone cannot establish that AI caused
them or that seat-priced software is losing customers. Source: [BLS, 4 September](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_09042026.htm).

**Observation.** Locally, WTI is $93.22, up 13.50% over one month; the US ten-year yield is
4.784%. Beyond that block, Konecranes describes strong orders but delayed delivery conversion,
and expects stronger deliveries in the second half. **Inference.** The useful second-order
question is who can make existing industrial assets cheaper to operate and keep them working,
while collecting cash. An order book or an energy-efficiency story does not settle that.
Sources: [local macro](../../prices.json), [Konecranes management Q&A, 10 August](https://investors.konecranes.com/half-year-financial-report-2026-qa).

### KCR.HE — Konecranes — Nasdaq Helsinki — new

**Why:** Maintenance and material handling offer exposure to industrial productivity: Q2 orders
rose **13.4% at comparable currencies**, while the service agreement base grew **4.2%** on that
basis. Reported rolling-twelve-month ROCE was **20.6%**; this is the company's ROCE, not our
after-tax ROIC. Source: [24 July results](https://investors.konecranes.com/press/konecranes-plcs-half-year-financial-report-january-june-2026-strong-orders-second-quarter).

**What would have to be true:** Orders must convert into cash without eroding capital returns:
H1 free cash flow fell to **€14.4m from €177.1m**, and Q2 comparable EBITA margin fell to
**12.7% from 14.3%**. The **23 October** results provide the next company-scheduled check.
Source: [same results](https://investors.konecranes.com/press/konecranes-plcs-half-year-financial-report-january-june-2026-strong-orders-second-quarter).

Price and local valuation: **–**, pending independent fetching. The filing restates per-share
comparatives for this year's **three-for-one split**; the fetched EPS/history must agree before
using a percentile. The 7532.T mistake here would be capitalising backlog as earned profit and
ignoring working capital, acquisition costs and the purchase multiple. Added to the watchlist.
Source for the split: [same filing](https://investors.konecranes.com/press/konecranes-plcs-half-year-financial-report-january-june-2026-strong-orders-second-quarter).

### 6690.HK — Haier Smart Home — Hong Kong Stock Exchange — new

**Why:** Test whether regional supply chains and HVAC integration can overcome weak appliance
demand: the release reports Smart HVAC revenue growth of **6.1%** and segment profit growth of
**26.8%**, with shared products and channels. These are segment figures, not group growth.
Source: [company release, 27 August](https://www.haier.com/ph/about-haier/news/20260828_294395.shtml).

**What would have to be true:** Integration must improve group cash generation and capital
returns without depending on supplier funding or temporary benefits. The Hong Kong filing has
H1 revenue down **2.8%**, attributable profit down **14.3%**, and operating cash flow of
**RMB9.752bn versus RMB11.139bn**. Its overseas margin discussion includes a one-off tax rebate.
Source: [filed results, pages 1, 33 and 63](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0827/2026082702357.pdf).

**A source discrepancy matters:** the release says gross margin **27.2%**; the HK filing says
**26.8%**, consistent with RMB40,714m / RMB152,109m. I use the filing and leave reconciliation
open. Price, comparable local EPS and valuation: **–**. Yahoo access was blocked or stale, so no
quote is borrowed. Added to the watchlist. The 7532.T mistake would be mistaking a quarterly
bounce for durable growth while ignoring tariffs, currency, capital needs and valuation.
Sources: [release](https://www.haier.com/ph/about-haier/news/20260828_294395.shtml),
[filing, pages 1 and 33](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0827/2026082702357.pdf).
