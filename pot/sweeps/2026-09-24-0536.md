## 3. Candidate

### KWS SAAT — `KWS.DE`, Xetra — new look

**Why it is here.** KWS is a crop-seed developer rather than another software or semiconductor name. The issuer reported FY2025/26 sales of €1.627bn, adjusted EBITDA of €314.1m and net debt of only €8.7m. EPS rose to €4.80 from €4.24, but the company says this was partly helped by finance and investment income; it is not evidence that the operating business accelerated. Free cash flow was €122.5m, almost unchanged, while operating cash flow fell to €172.6m from €227.7m and proceeds from the North American corn sale helped bridge the difference. Sugarbeet sales fell 2% and its EBITDA margin declined to 41.9% from 45.5%; vegetable sales fell 6.4% and the segment lost €25.8m at EBITDA. Those details keep the apparent balance-sheet and EPS improvement honest.

Yahoo's provisional off-universe quote was €71.50, down 9.03% on the day but still up 12.60% over one year, within a €61.30–€80.60 52-week range. Its provisional trailing P/E was 15.02. These market figures are not yet local and should be replaced by the normal pipeline now that the name is on the watchlist. Sources: [KWS results](https://www.kws.com/corp/en/media-press/news-2026/stable-business-performance-in-a-challenging-market-environment-kws-publishes-results-for-the-2025-2026-fiscal-year/), [KWS on seed adaptation](https://www.kws.com/corp/en/innovation/our-innovation-areas/seed-of-the-future/), [Yahoo Finance, provisional](https://de.finance.yahoo.com/quote/KWS.DE/).

**What must be true.** Management's FY2026/27 forecast of about 3% organic sales growth and a 19–20% EBITDA margin must be delivered without another asset sale doing the work of operations. Gross margin needs to stabilise, cash conversion needs to recover, and the vegetable investment must move toward profitability. Research spending at 21% of sales must eventually produce pricing, share gains or royalty economics that earn more than its cost. The 7532 mistake would be to let the climate-adaptation story excuse falling sales or margins; the shares' one-year gain also means this is not an untouched dislocation.
