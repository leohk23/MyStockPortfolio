## 3. Candidates

### 2914.T — Tokyo — Japan Tobacco (new)

- **Why.** This is a news event whose price effect has not yet been settled. The share fell 19.3% in one day to ¥6,269, provisional from Yahoo. Its weekly high was ¥7,539 in the week of 18 October 2026, and its five-year weekly low was ¥2,012 in March 2022, the month after Russia invaded Ukraine. So the share tripled with the Russian business still inside it. The press says Russia is about 10% of group revenue and about 25% of operating profit, citing company figures ([Tobacco Journal](https://www.tobaccojournal.com/news/russia-seizes-jti-operations/), via Reuters). **This lane could not verify that.** jt.com refused every request with 403, including its Q3 presentation of 29 October, so the figure is press-attributed and must not be used as a fact. The question is whether a one-day fall of 19% removed more or less than the Russian business was worth to a JT shareholder. Its profit may never have been distributable. The candidate adds Consumer Defensive (3.3% of the book) and offsets Technology and Communication Services. It adds to Japan (8.3%), where Leo already holds 7532.T, 8001.T and 5332.T. `strategy.md` §3.2 excludes no sector, so tobacco is in the universe unless Leo says otherwise.
- **What would have to be true.** The rest of JT, meaning Japanese domestic tobacco, the other international markets and the Ploom heated-tobacco business, has to be worth ¥6,269 a share on its own. That has to be measured from JT's own segment figures. If jt.com stays closed, they are in its securities reports on EDINET. The seizure has to be the end of the damage. Watch for cash held in Russia, any guarantee or liability left behind, and any sign that another government copies it. On 29 October JT said it was "unable to reasonably estimate the outlook" for Russia and was evaluating "the potential transfer of ownership" ([Tobacco Reporter](https://tobaccoreporter.com/2026/10/29/japan-tobacco-raises-2026-earnings-forecast-after-nine-month-profit-growth/), quoting the company). So a write-off may come with the full-year results, which last year were published on 12 February ([JT](https://www.jt.com/investors/results/forecast/pdf/2025/Full_Year/20260212_06.pdf)). **The 7532.T test:** this is not a tourism story, but it is a familiar name bought on a sharp fall. That is close enough to the 7532.T mistake to state plainly: a 19% fall does not make it cheap. Valuation is the filter, and only the ex-Russia earnings, from JT's own filing, can pass it. Cigarette volumes are also in long decline, so growth has to come from price and from Ploom. The repo has no P/E record for JT yet. Adding it is what gives one.
