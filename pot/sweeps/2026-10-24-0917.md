## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** The two dominant shocks remain energy and AI capital spending. The IEA says global
diesel exports were 700,000 barrels a day lower year on year in September, world oil demand is
forecast to fall 2.6m barrels a day in 2026, and 330m barrels of emergency stocks had been released
by 15 October, with more than 20m barrels of diesel to be accelerated
([IEA October market report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-october-2026),
[IEA Governing Board statement](https://www.iea.org/news/iea-governing-board-statement-on-energy-markets-and-security)).
The IMF now forecasts 3.1% global growth and 4.7% headline inflation in 2026, identifying
energy-importing economies outside the technology value chain as the hardest hit
([IMF October WEO](https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WEO/Issues/2026/10/13/world-economic-outlook-october-2026)).
Inside the book, yesterday's Meta result showed the other side of the divide: revenue grew 26%, but
quarterly capital spending including finance-lease principal exceeded operating cash flow, as
recorded in the [previous sweep](2026-10-23-1157.md).

**Inference.** The world is not simply risk-off: scarce energy and AI investment are transferring
income between sectors and countries. Leo already has the AI beneficiaries and yesterday's sweep
added Neste for the energy dislocation. The less crowded search is for profitable or near-profitable
European healthcare businesses whose demand is not created by either shock. That also addresses two
gaps in the actual book rather than adding another US technology name. I went beyond the local macro
block for the IMF outlook and the IEA's product-market and stock-release detail.

## 3. Candidates

### BAVA.CO — Nasdaq Copenhagen — Bavarian Nordic — new

**Why watch it.** Bavarian Nordic adds European Healthcare, both scarce in the book. On 8 October it
raised 2026 Travel Health revenue guidance from about DKK 3.0bn to DKK 3.4bn after stronger rabies
vaccine demand; the separate Public Preparedness assumption remained about DKK 2.5bn, of which
DKK 2.4bn was already secured in orders
([company guidance](https://www.bavarian-nordic.com/investor/news/news.aspx?news=7611)). This is not a
pre-profit biotech: 2025 revenue was DKK 6.244bn, EBIT DKK 1.804bn, net profit DKK 1.375bn and
operating cash flow DKK 2.722bn
([2025 annual report](https://www.bavarian-nordic.com/media/380028/bn_annual_report_2025.pdf)).

**What has to be true.** Travel-vaccine demand must remain a repeatable franchise rather than a
one-year rabies outbreak, and earnings must remain attractive when volatile government preparedness
orders normalise. The 7532.T check is explicit: a demand spike is not structural growth merely
because it appears in guidance. The next accounts need to separate volume and price, show the travel
portfolio growing outside rabies, and preserve cash conversion.

### RDC.DE — Xetra — Redcare Pharmacy — new

**Why watch it.** Redcare is a second European Healthcare candidate, driven by Germany's shift to
electronic prescriptions rather than drug-development risk. It raised 2026 group revenue growth
guidance to 16–18% and German prescription revenue to EUR 730–760m after preliminary third-quarter
German prescription growth of 56%; full Q3 figures arrive on 29 October
([company guidance](https://ir.redcare-pharmacy.com/en/news/redcare-pharmacy-raises-full-year-2026-revenue-guidance-again-driven-by-strong-rx-momentum-in-germany/1ed5c44d-9182-480e-bc2c-a0e03c44f64c)).
The quality is not yet proven: 2025 revenue rose 24.1% to EUR 2.941bn, but adjusted EBITDA margin was
only 2.0%, reported net loss was EUR 39.4m, operating cash flow EUR 31m and capital expenditure
EUR 118.6m ([2025 results](https://ir.redcare-pharmacy.com/en/news/redcare-pharmacy-strong-growth-in-2025-surpassing-eur-1bn-in-rx-revenue-adj-ebitda-up/4d756ba7-ebd1-4b54-8610-bcb06263b44d)).

**What has to be true.** Prescription growth must create operating leverage and cash, not just a
larger low-margin retailer. Q3 must support the 2.5–3.0% adjusted EBITDA margin guide, the
International segment must continue towards break-even, and peak automation spending must fall
without weakening fulfilment. **7532.T check:** the adoption story is visible in sales, but it is
not investable until the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement converge.
