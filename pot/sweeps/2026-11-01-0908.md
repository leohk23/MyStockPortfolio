## 3. Candidates

### EHC — New York Stock Exchange — Encompass Health — new

- **Why it might belong.** Q3 net operating revenue grew 8.9%, but the useful operating evidence is underneath it: same-store discharges grew 3.0%, total discharges 5.7% and operating cash flow 33.7%. Adjusted free cash flow rose 51.2%. Encompass added 189 beds through four new hospitals and 85 beds at existing hospitals in the first nine months. This adds Healthcare, only 1.02% of the book, and its demand is patient rehabilitation rather than AI capital spending. It does add another US listing, so the case wins on sector diversification, not geography. The figures come from the [Q3 issuer release](https://www.encompasshealth.com/press-releases/encompass-health-reports-results-for-third-quarter-2026).
- **What would have to be true.** Same-store discharge growth must persist while labour cost per patient and payer mix leave real cash after the capital needed for new beds. The 4.0% increase in net patient revenue per discharge must reflect durable reimbursement and case mix rather than price alone, and new hospitals must earn an adequate return on their beds and working capital. Local valuation still has to clear Leo's filter. **The 7532.T test:** an ageing population is not the thesis; patient volumes, reimbursement, staffing and capital returns are.
