### HEN3.DE, Xetra: Henkel preferred shares, Germany, new

- **Why:** a testable pricing-power case in adhesives and household products: sales can grow under input inflation, but the volume and margin bridge determines whether shareholders benefit.
- **What would have to be true:** price increases must retain customers and cover costs; the next full-year accounts must show cash returns on the enlarged capital base, not merely acquired revenue.

The [29 October preliminary release](https://www.henkel.com/press-and-media/press-releases-and-kits/2026-10-29-henkel-achieves-very-strong-organic-sales-growth-in-the-third-quarter-and-updates-outlook-for-2026-2272318) splits Q3 organic growth into **5.4% price and 1.1% volume**. Adhesives were **7.2% price, only 0.7% volume**; Consumer Brands **3.2% price and 1.6% volume**. The [10 November release](https://www.henkel.com/press-and-media/press-releases-and-kits/2026-11-10-henkel-posts-very-strong-sales-growth-in-the-third-quarter-2274738) confirms the growth and says higher raw-material costs constrained the earnings outlook. This is not broad volume acceleration. The adhesives segment's sub-1% volume deserves the §2.4b recovery scrutiny even though group volume clears that threshold.

**7532.T test:** do not buy a headline recovery built mostly on price. Group adjusted margin guidance is **around 15%**; direct-material prices are expected **around 10% higher**, before countermeasures. Compare statutory earnings, restructuring charges and returns on capital before calling the pricing durable.

**Price, provisional:** [Yahoo's history page](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/HEN3.DE/history/) shows **€85.88 at the 23 November close**, against **€77.88 on 28 October**, before the preliminary release: **+10.3% by division**. The improvement has already attracted a repricing. Add for monitoring; no historical-cheapness claim follows from these prices.

### SBS, NYSE: Sabesp ADS, Brazil, new

- **Why:** expansion of essential water and sanitation access can add paying customers and regulated assets; it is a different demand source from the book's technology spending.
- **What would have to be true:** completed investment must enter the remunerated asset base promptly, and incremental tariff revenue must exceed financing, depreciation and power costs without an unsustainable funding gap.

The company's [5 November Q3 release filed on EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1170858/000129281426005156/sbspr3q26_6k.htm) reports **adjusted service revenue +9.0%** and **adjusted EBITDA +13.8%**, but **adjusted net income −13.8%**. Total billed volume rose only **1.1% in Q3 and 0.6% over nine months**; tariff and mix effects matter. Investment was **R$4.5bn in Q3**. The accounts show **R$2.127bn operating cash after interest and tax**, against **R$5.108bn spent on contract assets, intangibles and property**. That is a funding requirement, not cash generation available to shareholders.

**7532.T test:** a national infrastructure programme is not itself an equity return. The latest net-income decline and sub-1% nine-month volume require a dated recovery case under §2.4b before any proposal. Construction revenue and company-adjusted service revenue must stay distinct; a USD receipt price must not be divided by BRL common-share EPS. Validate the split and ADS basis independently.
