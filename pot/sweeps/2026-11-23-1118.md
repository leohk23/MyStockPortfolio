### 4507.T, Tokyo Stock Exchange: Shionogi, new

- **Why:** a Japanese drug maker on about **12.5×** its own guided EPS. That is ¥3,121 at Friday's
  close (Yahoo, provisional) over ¥249.67, the full-year EPS it raised from ¥246.79 on 29 October.
  It also raised the dividend from ¥76 to ¥86 ([forecast revision](https://www.shionogi.com/global/en/news/2026/10/20261029_22.html)).
- **What would have to be true:** earnings must outlive the HIV royalty. The newer businesses and
  ViiV's long-acting regimens have to replace dolutegravir's income as its patents expire. The
  organic growth rate, excluding acquisitions, must be shown before anything is proposed.

The revision calls HIV "a key pillar of Shionogi's earnings" and expects royalty income and
equity-method profit from ViiV to rise. It gives no figure for either, so the size of the
dependence is not established here.

Search previews of Shionogi's own [March 2023 HIV presentation](https://www.shionogi.com/content/dam/shionogi/global/investors/ir-library/presentation/2022/E20230330.pdf)
put dolutegravir's patent expiry around **2028 in the US and 2029 in Europe**. The PDF would not
decode, so those dates are **unverified**.

Part of this year's growth is bought. The revision credits Torii-associated products and the
RADICAVA business transfer. That makes it a §2.4b case: the organic rate must be stated, not
inferred from the headline.

On price: −14.8% from February's ¥3,664 weekly high, and +16.4% over a year (provisional). That is
not a de-rating.

**7532.T test:** this is the closest to it of anything today, for two reasons.

- A low P/E built on a royalty with a known end date may simply be the market pricing the cliff
  correctly.
- The royalties are earned in dollars and sterling and reported in yen. At ¥159.1 to the dollar
  (`prices.json`), a weak yen flatters them, which is the currency trap Leo named.

Both have to be checked in the filing before this is called cheap. It adds **Healthcare**, the
book's smallest real sector, and Japan.
