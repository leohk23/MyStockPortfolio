# Sweep — 4 September 2026

## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** Japan's Statistics Bureau reports that July real household consumption fell
3.6% year on year, while real income for workers' households fell 3.8%. Against that, the repo's
11:48 UTC snapshot has the Nikkei 225 up 51.2% over one year. **Inference.** Japanese equity
strength is not evidence of a healthy household. I looked for businesses that benefit when people
trade down or recirculate goods, while requiring proof that domestic unit economics—not tourism,
store openings or a weak yen—drive the return. Sources: [Japan's Family Income and Expenditure
Survey](https://www.stat.go.jp/english/data/kakei/156.htm) and [local markets](../../prices.json).

**Observation.** Eurostat's release today says July euro-area retail volumes fell 0.6% month on
month; non-food volumes fell 1.4%, and Germany's total fell 3.4%. The repo simultaneously has WTI
up 46.7% over one year and the Euro Stoxx 50 up 20.1%. **Inference.** Household demand and input
costs are weaker than the headline index suggests. Reuse, repair and price-led formats merit work,
but a strained customer is not by itself a thesis and a broken retailer is not automatically
cheap. Source: [Eurostat retail trade](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/en/web/products-euro-indicators/w/4-04092026-ap)
and [local markets](../../prices.json).

**Observation.** US factory orders rose 0.9% in July, shipments rose 0.8% and unfilled orders rose
0.6%; the August employment report is still scheduled for 12:30 UTC, after this sweep's cutoff.
**Inference.** There is enough realised industrial activity to investigate tools that improve
safety and productivity without making a pre-release bet on rates. The distinction matters: a
workflow product must still show organic demand and cash conversion, not merely acquisition-led
growth. Sources: [US Census manufacturing orders](https://www.census.gov/manufacturing/m3/current/index.html)
and the [BLS employment release page](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm).

## 3. Candidates

### 2681.T — GEO Holdings (to become 2nd RETAILING) — Tokyo Stock Exchange Prime

**Why it deserves work:** 2nd STREET reuse sales rose 23.6% year on year in the June quarter,
while group sales rose 19.7% and operating profit rose 32.8%. This is direct evidence that
household thrift is reaching a business, not a forecast from weak spending alone. Source:
[GEO's first-quarter filing](https://www.geonet.co.jp/english/geo_wp/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2681_20260806_financial_results_1Q_E_Ax7pk5.pdf).

**What would have to be true:** Domestic reuse store productivity and inventory turns must fund
the expansion; the result must not depend on 38 net store additions, Switch pricing or the
95.2% rebound in reuse-luxury sales. Cash conversion and valuation also have to pass independently,
because the quarterly filing provides no cash-flow statement and borrowings rose.

### BRC — Brady Corporation — New York Stock Exchange

**Why it deserves work:** Fiscal-2026 organic sales grew 5.3% and operating cash flow rose 34.7%
to $244.1m; its identification, safety and productivity products are a second-order way to own
factory activity and operating efficiency. Source: [Brady's SEC-filed annual-results
release](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/746598/000074659826000043/exhibit991-financialsx2026.htm).

**What would have to be true:** The newly acquired Honeywell productivity business must deliver
the guided $0.80 of adjusted EPS accretion without integration add-backs, leverage or working
capital consuming the benefit, while the legacy identification business sustains roughly 5%
organic growth. The independently fetched valuation must allow for an acquisition miss.

These are candidates, not orders. Powell Industries was checked but not advanced because one
$400m-plus data-centre award dominates its record order quarter; FuelCell Energy was rejected
because most of the eye-catching $2.4bn awarded-capacity figure is optional rather than contracted;
and GEO's smaller reuse peers were not advanced without equally accessible current filings.
