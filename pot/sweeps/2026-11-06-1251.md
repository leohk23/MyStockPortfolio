## 3. Candidates

### 105560.KS — Korea Exchange — KB Financial Group (new)

- **Why.** It is Korea's largest bank holding company, and its earnings come from domestic lending and fees, not from semiconductors. Its [Q3 2026 preliminary results (Form 6-K, 29 October)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001445930/000119312526436880/d182035d6k.htm) show profit attributable to controlling interest of ₩1,907.5bn for the quarter, up 13.1%, and ₩5,792.1bn for nine months, also up 13.1%. The filing says these are unaudited. The share closed at ₩151,000 on 6 November, provisional from Yahoo. That is 22% below its 52-week high of ₩194,500 and against ₩75,200 at the April 2025 low. It fell 3.8% on a day the Kospi fell more than 8%. It adds Financial Services (8.5% of the book) and Korea (2.4%, all SK Hynix today), and it offsets Technology and Communication Services rather than adding to them.
- **What would have to be true.** Korean credit has to hold: household debt and property are the risks for any Korean bank, and today's sell-off does nothing to them. The 6-K carries no capital ratio, dividend or buyback figure. Reports of the earnings call describe record shareholder returns, but this lane did not verify them from a filing, so it states none. The government's "value-up" policy, pushing listed companies to raise payouts, has to survive. **The 7532.T test:** it is not a tourism-style story, but the price has doubled since April 2025 on that policy, so part of the case may already be priced. Valuation is the filter, and the repo values companies on P/E, which says little about a bank. A Deep dive would need price-to-book against return on equity, from the company's own disclosure, before it could call this cheap.

### 2802.T — Tokyo — Ajinomoto (re-look, already watched)

- **Why.** It fell 12.4% today and 19.4% over the month (`prices.json`) on no news of its own, three days before its results on 9 November. Most of its sales are seasonings and frozen food. The share was sold as an AI stock because of its chip-packaging film. It sits at the 30th percentile of its five-year P/E (`prices.json` `pePctile` 0.296).
- **What would have to be true.** The 9 November results have to show the film business is still growing, or that the food business carries the group without it. If the film business slows with data-centre spending, today's fall was not overdone, just early. Nothing should be done before that release, and this lane has no figure from the company's last results to quote. Leo will know the brand at first hand, which is the familiarity `strategy.md` §2 asks for, but knowing the brand is not knowing which half of the business the price is following.
