# Sweep — 29 August 2026

## 1. Where the world is

**Observation.** Markets are broad but not uniformly euphoric. The local country monitor has 16
of 43 live markets within 5% of their all-time highs, while 11 still need at least 50% to recover;
the median market is 13% below its high. The S&P 500 is 1.3% below its high, while Brazil,
Indonesia and Malaysia remain among the deepest long-duration laggards
([signals.json](../../signals.json)). **Inference.** This is not one global risk-on trade. The
opportunity is more likely to be in a specific business whose economics are improving than in
buying a depressed country simply because its chart is depressed.

**Observation.** The US has an awkward mix rather than a clean soft landing. July payrolls fell
23,000, May and June were revised down by 103,000 in total, and unemployment was 4.1%
([BLS employment report](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.htm)). CPI was still 3.4% year on
year, with energy up 14.7%, while real average hourly earnings were 0.2% lower than a year earlier
([BLS CPI](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/cpi.htm),
[BLS real earnings](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/realer.nr0.htm)). July personal consumption
rose only 0.2%: services spending increased by $86.2bn while goods fell by $49.9bn
([BEA](https://www.bea.gov/news/2026/personal-income-and-outlays-july-2026)). Chair Warsh called
the 2% target firm and said rates remain the main tool
([Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/speech/warsh20260828a.htm)).
**Inference.** Weak employment does not yet guarantee cheaper money. I would not build a candidate
on a near-term rate cut or a broad consumer rebound; self-help and supply discipline matter more.

**Observation.** The physical oil system is more stressed than the crude quote alone shows. Local
WTI is up 27% over one year but down 10% in three months ([signals.json](../../signals.json)); the
IEA says Gulf output was still 8.3m b/d below its pre-war level in July, seaborne product trade was
down 3.8m b/d year on year, and Atlantic Basin refining margins reached records
([IEA Oil Market Report](https://www.iea.org/reports/oil-market-report-august-2026)). In China,
July retail sales grew only 0.6% year on year and catering revenue 1.4%, although January–July
catering was up 2.6% ([National Bureau of Statistics of China](https://www.stats.gov.cn/english/PressRelease/202608/t20260819_1965078.html)).
**Inference.** The investable oil fact is the rerouting and product bottleneck, not a forecast of
tomorrow's crude price. China's consumer is still weak enough that restaurant growth must be
company-specific rather than assumed from macro recovery.

### Michael Burry, 11 March — common stocks and uncommon value

Haidilao is the part that became more interesting. The article stated about HK$17; the delayed

PDD has also become cheaper: Yahoo's delayed six-month measure is -17.7%

([PDD Q2 release](https://investor.pddholdings.com/static-files/92cc7e1f-611b-4c0e-8cb5-5f5c7819e1cc)).
Cheaper is not clearer, so PDD is not a candidate from this sweep.

## 3. Candidates

### 6862.HK — Haidilao International, Hong Kong

**Why:** a non-VIE China consumer name from Leo's saved work has fallen roughly 28% while table
turnover and core operating profit improved; delivery and new formats are now producing growth
without requiring a broad consumer recovery.

**What would have to be true:** delivery and secondary brands must add durable profit rather than
hide erosion in the core restaurants, same-store sales and spend must stabilise, and related-party
economics must remain fair.

### STNG — Scorpio Tankers, New York

**Why:** this is the second-order oil story: rerouted refined products and longer voyages lifted
second-quarter average daily TCE revenue to $52,661 from $25,569 despite a smaller fleet, while the
company sold older vessels rather than simply expanding into the spike
([company Q2 results](https://www.scorpiotankers.com/scorpio-tankers-inc-announces-financial-results-for-the-second-quarter-of-2026-and-the-declaration-of-a-dividend/)).

**What would have to be true:** longer routes and disciplined fleet supply must outlast the
war-driven rate spike, and management's 2029–30 newbuild commitments must not turn today's windfall
into tomorrow's overcapacity.

No order, price target or position size is implied. `3067.HK` and `1211.HK` are re-looks already in
the book; PDD failed the disclosure test.
