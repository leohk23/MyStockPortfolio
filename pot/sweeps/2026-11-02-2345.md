## 3. Candidates

### ULVR.L — London Stock Exchange — Unilever — new

- **Why.** Q3 underlying sales grew 5.2%: 2.9% from volume, 2.3% from price. In emerging markets they grew 9.5% with 6.0% volume, while developed markets fell 0.9% with volume down 1.7%. India grew 15% with 7% volume. Asia Pacific Africa and Latin America together were €8.0bn of the €13.3bn quarter, and full-year volume guidance was raised to at least 3% ([SEC 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000217410/000021741026000061/a2026-10x28xunileverq320.htm)). It adds Consumer Defensive (3.55% of the book) and emerging-market consumer demand that the book barely has, through a non-US listing, so it offsets both concentrations. It covers the same gap as CL (added 31 October), but CL is US-listed; the Deep dive should price the two against each other rather than carry both.
- **What would have to be true.** Emerging-market volume has to hold while price increases speed up, which the company says will happen for the rest of 2026. The developed-market decline must not widen, and Foods (volume down 1.1%) must stop dragging. The "modest improvement" in operating margin has to show up as return on capital, not just margin: this is a goodwill-heavy balance sheet, which is the point of the Djukanovic piece. **The 7532.T test:** the risk is buying a turnaround story built on new management and a reshaped portfolio. The defence is that the test here is reported volume, quarter by quarter, not the story.

### 4519.T — Tokyo Stock Exchange — Chugai Pharmaceutical — new

- **Why.** For January to September, core revenue was ¥1,068.1bn (+17.2%), core operating profit ¥536.1bn (+19.0%) and core net income ¥384.7bn (+20.2%). Royalty and other revenue grew 37.7% to ¥161.3bn, and the company says full-year results will beat its forecast ([Chugai Q3 release](https://www.chugai-pharm.co.jp/english/news/detail/20261027170000_1297.html)). Lilly's [Q3 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000059478/000005947826000108/lly-20260930.htm) confirms Chugai earns tiered royalties, from mid single digits to low teens, on worldwide net sales of orforglipron, now sold as Foundayo. That is a second-order way into oral GLP-1: neither of the two marketers (NVO is already watched), but a royalty with no selling cost attached. It adds Healthcare (1.03% of the book) in Japan, outside tech.
- **What would have to be true.** The base business (Alecensa, Hemlibra, Enspryng and exports to Roche) has to carry the valuation on its own, with Foundayo royalties as an option on top. Hemlibra has to hold against newer haemophilia drugs. Chugai is Roche-controlled and the release puts its overseas growth down to exports, which go to Roche, so part of its margin is set by a related party (the share of sales is not in the release and should come from the annual report); ROG.SW has been on the watchlist since 23 October and the overlap needs stating. US drug-pricing policy is the outside risk. **The 7532.T test:** buying the obesity-pill excitement would be the mistake again. The royalty is not broken out by product (see section 4), so it cannot yet be the case.
