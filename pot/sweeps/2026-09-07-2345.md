ten-year yield at 4.784%. **Inference.** Energy and financing costs still make unfunded expansion
stories difficult. The useful search is for work customers must eventually undertake and can
actually finance, with proof that suppliers collect cash. Higher oil alone does not establish
pricing power for an industrial company. Source: [local macro block](../../prices.json).

**Observation beyond our macro block.** Japan's infrastructure problem has progressed from an
accident headline to inspection and procurement work. MLIT's April update identified 748 km
requiring measures among 4,692 km with survey results; its March legislative proposal addresses
diagnosis, maintenance disclosure and coordinated renewal. These are dated policy observations,
not fresh September statistics. NJS subsequently announced a Bengaluru water-expansion study on
27 August. **Inference.** Engineering, inspection and utility operations deserve attention as
second-order beneficiaries, but a study award is not a construction order and a repair need is
not a funded backlog. Sources: [MLIT survey update](https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/interview/daijin260421.html),
[MLIT proposal](https://www.mlit.go.jp/report/press/mizukokudo13_hh_000722.html) and
[NJS study award](https://www.njs.co.jp/ja/investors/news/auto_20260827527049/pdfFile.pdf).

**Observation beyond our macro block.** Hannover Re's 7 September renewal outlook describes
abundant capital and softer prices, especially on loss-free catastrophe business, while expecting
terms and retentions largely to hold. This is an underwriter's assessment, not an agency index.
**Inference.** More demand for protection need not mean better shareholder returns: cheaper
outward cover can help an insurer while competition erodes its inward pricing. That makes
Lancashire's net retained economics more useful to examine than another headline catastrophe
growth story. Source: [Hannover Re's renewal outlook](https://www.hannover-re.com/en/news/2026/hannover-re-sees-profitable-growth-opportunities-in-an-increasingly-challenging-market-environment/).

### LRE.L — Lancashire Holdings — London Stock Exchange — re-look

**Why:** Test whether selective underwriting and outward protection preserve returns as pricing
softens. H1's undiscounted combined ratio was 90.8%, but the renewal price index was 92%.
Source: [Lancashire H1 results, pages 1–3](https://www.lancashiregroup.com/content/dam/lancashire/corporate2020/Media/Press-Releases/2026/LHL-results-for-the-six-months-ended-30-June-2026.pdf.downloadasset.pdf).

**What would have to be true:** Net underwriting returns must survive lower renewal rates and
a less favourable claims year without relying on discounting or reserve releases. Buying more
outward protection is not proof of lower total cost: allocated reinsurance premiums rose 14%,
partly because more proportional protection was purchased. Source: [same filing, page 3](https://www.lancashiregroup.com/content/dam/lancashire/corporate2020/Media/Press-Releases/2026/LHL-results-for-the-six-months-ended-30-June-2026.pdf.downloadasset.pdf).

only **128 weeks** starting 5 April 2024. This is not the cheapest-decile signal, nor a complete
five-year history. It stays a monitoring candidate; no duplicate watchlist entry is needed.
The 7532.T mistake here would be capitalising favourable-cycle earnings as permanent.
