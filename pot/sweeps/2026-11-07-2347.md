## 3. Candidates

### 1378.HK — Hong Kong — China Hongqiao Group (new)

- **Why.** Profits rose and the share price halved, and the company is buying back its own shares at the lower price. From its [interim results](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/0821/2026082101077.pdf) for the six months to 30 June 2026:

  | RMB '000 | H1 2026 | H1 2025 |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | Revenue | 87,505,895 | 81,039,092 |
  | Gross profit | 27,526,337 | 20,805,191 |
  | Change in fair value of financial instruments | 953,072 | (2,105,114) |
  | Profit before tax | 25,153,648 | 17,763,982 |
  | Attributable to owners | 17,210,307 | 12,361,046 |

  The headline +39.2% is flattered by that fair-value line, which swung by RMB3.1bn. Take it out and pre-tax profit rose 21.8% (RMB24.20bn against RMB19.87bn, this lane's arithmetic on the filed figures). The gross margin rose to 31.5% from 25.7%. Aluminium alloy volume fell 3.3% to 2.811m tonnes, and the alumina it sells fetched 28.3% less, RMB2,327 a tonne. Cash was RMB46.1bn against RMB51.2bn at December. There was no interim dividend. Between 23 and 30 October the company bought back 107,941,000 shares, 1.1% of the total, at an average HK$19.5566, and cancelled them on 5 November. That leaves 9,711,456,841 shares ([next-day return](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1106/2026110600095.pdf)). The controlling shareholders own about 62.67% ([buyback notice](https://www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/sehk/2026/1023/2026102301109.pdf)). At HK$20.40 the market value is about HK$198bn (Yahoo price, provisional). Yahoo records a HK$1.65 final dividend paid in May, 8.1% of today's price, provisional. The filing shows RMB14.07bn of dividends recognised in the half. **What it does to the book:** it adds Basic Materials, which is too small to have its own line in `book.bySector`. It offsets Technology and Communication Services (56.0%). It adds to China (7.3%), but not through a VIE or the tech trade that makes up most of Leo's China exposure. No thesis drove it.
- **What would have to be true.** Profit has to hold at a lower metal price, because this is a commodity producer earning a high margin, and a low multiple on high-margin earnings is how a cyclical traps a buyer. Four things to check. **One:** the cost side. Profit at a normal aluminium price, from the segment and cost tables, has to support the price paid. H1 2025, at a lower metal price, earned RMB12.36bn attributable. **Two:** the ceiling. China's cap on primary aluminium capacity has to keep holding supply back. The press reports that brokers disagree on whether it will be breached, and that has not been checked at source. **Three:** commitments. The convertible-bond liability due within a year rose to RMB9.43bn from RMB0.24bn. The group has also guaranteed up to US$1.78bn of a project's funding, which reads as its Guinea iron-ore venture (both from the interim filing). **Four:** the accounts themselves. In 2017 a short-seller accused Hongqiao of overstating profit. Ernst & Young paused its audit and then resigned, and the shares were suspended from March to October. The allegations were never proven ([Yicai Global](https://yicaiglobal.com/news/china-hongqiao-shares-resume-trading-to-rise-40-percent-following-negative-emerson-analytics-report), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/business/companies/article/2076710/china-hongqiao-fights-short-seller-emersons-allegations-profit), narrative). Leo is a CPA, and the current auditor's opinion and its key audit matters in the 2025 annual report are the first thing to read. **The 7532.T test:** this is the commodity version of being "blinded by the tourist rebound". The rebound here is the aluminium price with Gulf supply missing. The test is earnings at a normal price, not the fall from the high. The repo has no P/E record for Hongqiao yet, and adding it gets one.
