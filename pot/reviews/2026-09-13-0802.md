## The accepted break cannot see all of its own dominant risk

P6 names the dominant risk as physical demand weakening while the expanded manufacturing asset base
stops earning its improved returns and pricing hides the deterioration. The accepted break tests
volume **and** operating margin together. It has no asset denominator, so falling asset returns are
invisible, and pricing that keeps margin at 18% can make declining volumes pass forever.

P3 should have had a standalone asset-return test and a standalone margin route. The current
[11 September MWA top-up proposal](../proposals/2026-09-11-1715-MWA.md) does: it adds an after-tax
operating-return-on-assets measure, separate 10% warning and 8% break thresholds, and a margin-only
route. That is a better falsifier, but the proposal remains open and does not rewrite the conditions
governing the three shares already held. **This is a re-proposal issue, not a sale.** No standing view
in [theses.md](../theses.md) bears on MWA.
