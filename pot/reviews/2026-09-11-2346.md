## The accepted break still cannot see its own dominant risk

Repeating the [10 September Review](2026-09-10-2024.md)'s objection, because it remains open.

P6 names the dominant risk as *"physical demand weakens enough that the expanded manufacturing asset
base stops earning its improved returns, while pricing temporarily hides that deterioration."* That
is a statement about a **denominator** — capital tied up in the expanded factory. The accepted break
tests **operating margin**, which has no denominator for the asset base at all, and tests it
conjunctively with declining volumes. Both failure modes P6 describes therefore read "not tripped":
price holding margin above 18% while volumes fall clears the break by the `and`, and the asset base
ceasing to earn its return is invisible to a margin test whatever the volumes do. The proposal's own
P5 already carries the right denominator — FY2025 ROIC 13.12%, gross profit over assets 28.10%,
turnover 0.777× — and P3 does not use any of it.

P3 should have carried a standalone capital-return test against that 28.10% baseline, and a
standalone margin test not gated on volumes. **This is a re-proposal, not a sale.** The Deep dive has
already written the fix three times over — the current [11 September
MWA](../proposals/2026-09-11-1715-MWA.md) adds an explicit after-tax operating return on assets with
warning at 10% and break at 8%, plus a margin-only route — but that proposal is `open`, not accepted,
and until Leo accepts it the three shares he actually owns are governed by the weaker 8 September
conditions. Nothing here triggers §5.1.
